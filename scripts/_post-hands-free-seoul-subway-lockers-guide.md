Navigating Seoul's transit network with heavy suitcases between hotel check-out and flight departure creates unnecessary friction. Combining subway digital station lockers (T-Locker) for short stays with same-day airport luggage delivery services frees your hands and eliminates the physical toll of climbing station stairwells and navigating narrow transfer corridors.

## The Seoul Metro T-Locker Infrastructure

T-Locker is Seoul Metro's automated digital storage facility operating across major lines. It replaces traditional keys and coin-operated mechanisms with centralized touchscreen kiosks that accept international credit cards.

Lockers are situated both inside paid fare gates and outside in free concourse zones. Checking a station layout map before tapping your transit card ensures you do not waste a subway fare just to store or retrieve a bag.

> "A standard 28-inch check-in suitcase requires an official Large-tier locker compartment. Attempting to force oversized hard-shell luggage into a medium locker jams the digital latch mechanism and incurs manual maintenance fees."

* **Tiered Rental Intervals**: Standard storage starts with an initial 4-hour base window, shifting to automatic recurring hourly surcharges upon expiration.
* **Cashless Operation**: Transactions process via touchscreen kiosks accepting domestic pay services and foreign-issued Visa and Mastercard chip cards.
* **Live Availability Tracking**: The official T-Locker and Tota Subway mobile apps display real-time compartment availability for specific transit stations before arrival.

## Station Storage (T-Locker) vs. Airport Same-Day Delivery

Selecting the correct logistics channel depends directly on your planned schedule and bag sizes.

| Feature / Metric | Subway Station T-Locker | Airport Delivery (TRIPBAG / ZimCarry) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Optimal Use Case** | 2 to 5 hours of localized shopping or dining | Full final day of touring before an evening flight |
| **Capacity Limit** | Fixed locker dimensions; large units sell out fast | Accepts full-size suitcases, duffels, and oversized gear |
| **Route Flexibility** | Requires returning to the exact origin station | Drop off at hotel lobby; pick up directly at airport |
| **Pricing Baseline** | ~4,000 KRW to 6,000 KRW for initial 4 hours (Large) | ~15,000 KRW to 25,000 KRW per piece |
| **Booking Window** | Instant walk-up at the station kiosk | Advance online reservation required by morning |

## 3-Step Protocol for Storing and Moving Luggage

Follow this exact sequence to store bags locally or transfer them straight to the airport terminal.

* **Step 1: Locate and Select an Available Large Locker**
  Approach the station kiosk, tap the "English" button in the upper corner, and select "Store." Locate your locker size on the interactive screen. If storing a check-in suitcase (24 to 30 inches), select a blue (vacant) compartment marked **Large** (대형, approximately 450 × 850 × 600 mm).
* **Step 2: Set Your Passcode and Process Payment**
  Enter a memorable 6-digit numeric PIN twice on the touchscreen. Insert your foreign credit card into the physical chip terminal when prompted. Once payment completes, the locker door pops open automatically; stow your suitcase, shut the door firmly until the latch clicks, and keep the printed receipt containing your compartment number.
* **Step 3: Hand Off Luggage for Same-Day Airport Delivery**
  For final-day itineraries, book an airport delivery service (such as TRIPBAG or ZimCarry) the day prior. Leave your tagged luggage with your hotel front desk before 10:00 AM. Spend the entire day touring unencumbered, take the AREX express train to Incheon International Airport, and retrieve your luggage at the Terminal 1 or Terminal 2 official departures service counter before heading to airline check-in.
