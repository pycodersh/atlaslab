You walk into a bright pink booth, see a giant touchscreen, and have no idea what to press first.

There's no staff to ask. It's just you and the machine.

## The exchange

**이거 어떻게 사용하는 거야?**
*i-geo eo-tteo-ke sa-yong-ha-neun geo-ya?*
"How do you use this?"

If your friend has been before, that's all you need to say. They'll take it from there:

**여기서 먼저 배경을 고르면 돼**
*yeo-gi-seo meo-njeo bae-gyeong-eul go-reu-myeon dwae*
"First pick a background here"

Then you reach for the screen:

**아, 이걸 누르면 돼?**
*a, i-geol nu-reu-myeon dwae?*
"Oh, I just press this?"

**응, 그다음에 촬영 버튼 누르면 사진 찍혀**
*eung, geu-da-eum-e chwal-yeong beo-teun nu-reu-myeon sa-jin jji-kyeo*
"Yeah, then press the shutter button and it takes the photo"

## Words to know

| Korean | Romanization | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| 배경 | bae-gyeong | Background |
| 촬영 버튼 | chwal-yeong beo-teun | Shutter button |
| 고르다 | go-reu-da | To choose, to pick |
| 인화 | in-hwa | Print |
| 꾸미기 | kku-mi-gi | Decorating, stickers |
| 다시 찍기 | da-si jjik-gi | Retake |

## How it works

Most booths follow the same sequence, and the screen walks you through it step by step.

**1단계 — 배경 고르기** (*bae-gyeong go-reu-gi*) — Pick your background

Solid colors, seasonal themes, minimal gradients. Some booths have licensed character options. Scroll through and tap the one you want.

**2단계 — 촬영** (*chwal-yeong*) — Shooting

A countdown appears — usually 3 or 5 seconds — then the camera fires. Most booths take 4 to 8 shots in a row with a short pause between each. The screen shows a preview after each one.

**3단계 — 꾸미기** (*kku-mi-gi*) — Decorating

Add stickers, text, or frames. This is optional and time-limited — a timer counts down in the corner. You can skip it if you want clean photos.

**4단계 — 인화** (*in-hwa*) — Printing

Pick how many copies you want. Standard is one strip of 4 photos, but many booths let you choose 2 strips (one to keep, one to share). Payment is here if you haven't paid at the start.

## Useful phrases at the machine

**다시 찍을 수 있어요?** — "Can we retake it?"
Some booths allow a retake before the print is confirmed. Look for **다시 찍기** on screen.

**인화가 안 나와요** — "The print isn't coming out"
Say this if something goes wrong. Most booths have a QR code or number to contact support.

**몇 장 나와요?** — "How many photos come out?"
*myeot jang na-wa-yo?*

## Tips that aren't obvious

**Pay attention to the timer during decoration.** Most people run out of time before they finish adding stickers. Either decide fast or go minimal.

**The lighting changes by booth brand.** 인생네컷 tends toward warm, soft lighting. 포토이즘 (*Photo-ism*) is known for brighter, more high-contrast results. Try both if you're particular about how your photos look.

**The strip prints in about 30 seconds.** It comes out of a slot at the bottom or side of the machine. Don't walk away — it's easy to forget and leave your photos behind.

**Booths are everywhere.** You'll find them in shopping malls, near subway exits, and on busy shopping streets. Hongdae and Myeongdong have the highest concentration in Seoul.

## What to actually do

1. Pay at the start if the machine asks — most are 4,000 to 6,000 won for one strip
2. Pick your background first — scroll before you commit
3. Watch the countdown and look at the camera, not the screen
4. Move fast during the decoration step — the timer is short
5. Wait by the print slot — your photos come out in about 30 seconds

One friend who's been before makes the whole thing easy. After one round, you won't need the instructions.
