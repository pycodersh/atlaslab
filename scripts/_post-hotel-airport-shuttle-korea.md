You've checked out. Your flight is at 5pm. You have a bag and no idea where the shuttle stops.

One question at the front desk and you're set.

## The exchange

**무료 공항 셔틀은 어디서 타죠?**
*mu-ryo gong-hang syeo-teul-eun eo-di-seo ta-jyo?*
"Where do I take the free airport shuttle?"

**무료** is "free of charge." Lead with it and the staff knows exactly what you're asking about — not a taxi, not an Uber, the complimentary shuttle.

**~은 어디서 타죠?** is the standard way to ask where you board something — a bus, a shuttle, a subway. A useful frame to remember.

**정문 바로 앞에서 탑승하시면 됩니다.**
*jeong-mun ba-ro a-pe-seo tap-seung-ha-si-myeon doem-ni-da.*
"You can board right in front of the main entrance."

**정문** is "main entrance" — 정 (正) means "main" or "front." **바로 앞에** is "right in front." When you hear both, walk out the front door and look left or right — you'll see it.

**혹시 몇 분마다 출발하나요?**
*hok-si myeot bun-ma-da chul-bal-ha-na-yo?*
"How often does it depart?"

**몇 분마다** — "every how many minutes" — works for any scheduled service. Buses, ferries, elevators in busy buildings.

**30분마다 출발하고, 다음 버스는 10분 뒤에 옵니다!**
*sam-sip bun-ma-da chul-bal-ha-go, da-eum beo-seu-neun sip bun dwi-e om-ni-da!*
"Every 30 minutes — and the next one arrives in 10 minutes!"

**~분 뒤에** — "in ~ minutes" — is the phrase to listen for when you need a countdown. Ten minutes. You have time.

## Which hotels have free shuttles

Most mid-range to upscale hotels near Incheon International Airport (ICN) and Gimpo (GMP) run complimentary shuttles. Budget hotels and guesthouses usually don't. If your hotel isn't near an airport, there's no shuttle — but the next section has alternatives.

**공항 셔틀 있나요?** — "Is there an airport shuttle?" — is the question to ask when you're booking, not the morning of checkout. Get the schedule in writing.

## If there's no shuttle

Three options that work from almost any hotel in Seoul:

**공항버스 어디서 타요?** — "Where do I catch the airport bus?"
The AREX (Airport Railroad Express) is another. Ask at the front desk before you leave — they know the nearest stop.

**공항까지 택시로 얼마나 걸려요?** — "How long does it take by taxi to the airport?"
Good backup if the shuttle just left and you're cutting it close.

**공항버스 예약해 주실 수 있어요?** — "Could you book the airport bus for me?"
Some hotels will call or reserve for you. Always worth asking.

## Checking the schedule the night before

Don't leave this to the morning rush. The night before, ask:

**내일 아침 공항 셔틀 시간이 어떻게 되나요?**
(*nae-il a-chim gong-hang syeo-teul si-ga-ni eo-tteo-ke doe-na-yo?*)
"What time does the airport shuttle run tomorrow morning?"

Pick a departure that gives you at least 15 minutes of buffer. Shuttles stop for traffic, and ICN is a long drive.

## What to actually do

1. The night before, confirm the shuttle schedule at the front desk
2. Morning of departure, say **무료 공항 셔틀은 어디서 타죠?** if you forgot
3. Head to **정문 바로 앞** — the main entrance
4. Ask **다음 버스는 몇 분 뒤에 와요?** if you're not sure of the wait

Thirty-minute intervals are common. If you just missed one, find the hotel café, get one last Americano, and relax.
