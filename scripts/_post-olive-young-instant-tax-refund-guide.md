## Reading Price Labels: Value Sets vs. Standalone SKUs

Olive Young shelves feature brand-exclusive bundles designed to deliver bonus volume or complementary travel miniatures at the exact retail price of a single product.

Failing to verify the packaging descriptors often leads to purchasing standard singles instead of bundled limited editions. Look specifically for the Korean markings "기획" (Curated Special Set) or "단독" (Olive Young Exclusive) printed on shelf-edge price tags.

> "A package labeled '1+1' or '기획' delivers double the volume or accompanying deluxe treatment pads for the same base cost. Never purchase a standalone bottle without checking the adjacent shelf space for its promotional set equivalent."

* **1+1 Bundles**: Two full-sized identical products packaged together and sold for the unit price of one.
* **Special Sets (기획세트)**: A primary full-sized product bundled with official refills, sheet masks, or travel cleansers at zero extra charge.
* **Volume-Up Editions (증정/UP)**: Extended limited packaging providing 30% to 50% more fluid volume within a single container.

## 3-Step Execution Protocol for Olive Young Checkout

Follow this protocol to guarantee maximum volume per SKU and secure instant tax deductions before leaving the store.

* **Step 1: Verify the Special Edition Barcode on the Display Shelf**
  Inspect the lower corner of the packaging for the Korean characters "기획" (Gihwoek) or "1+1". Single-item boxes and promotional double-packs frequently sit side by side on identical shelves; compare the volumetric milliliters printed on the box face to ensure you hold the value-added edition.
* **Step 2: Present Your Physical Passport Before the First Barcode Scan**
  Hand your original passport to the clerk before product scanning commences while stating "Tax Free, please." Digital photos, smartphone scans, or photocopy prints are legally invalid under South Korean national tax statutes and will trigger an immediate system refusal.
* **Step 3: Audit the Discount Line on the Terminal Screen and Paper Receipt**
  The optical scanner reads your passport chip, instantly recalculating the transaction total by removing the domestic VAT and individual consumption tax.
