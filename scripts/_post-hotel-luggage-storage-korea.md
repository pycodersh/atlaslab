You arrive at the hotel at 10am. Check-in isn't until 3pm. You have five hours and a heavy bag.

One question solves it.

## The exchange

**혹시 체크인 전에 짐 먼저 맡길 수 있을까요?**
*hok-si che-keu-in jeo-ne jim meo-jeo mat-gil su it-eul-kka-yo?*
"Could I leave my luggage before check-in?"

**혹시** softens a request — it's like saying "by any chance" or "if possible." You'll hear it at the start of polite questions everywhere in Korea.

**맡기다** means to leave something in someone's care. The same word works for luggage, dry cleaning, and pets. A useful verb to know.

**네, 물론이죠! 짐 번호표 드릴게요.**
*ne, mul-lon-i-jyo! jim beon-ho-pyo deu-ril-ge-yo.*
"Of course! Here's your luggage tag."

**물론이죠** is "of course" — enthusiastic and warm. When you hear it, the answer is yes.

**번호표** is the numbered tag they attach to your bag. Keep it. You'll need it to pick up your luggage later.

**감사합니다. 몇 시부터 방에 들어갈 수 있나요?**
*gam-sa-ham-ni-da. myeot si-bu-teo bang-e deu-reo-gal su it-na-yo?*
"Thank you. What time can we enter the room?"

**몇 시부터** — "from what time" — is a versatile phrase. Use it any time you need to know when something starts.

**3시부터 입실 가능하십니다!**
*se-si-bu-teo ip-sil ga-neung-ha-sim-ni-da!*
"Check-in is available from 3 PM!"

**입실** is the formal word for entering your room — literally "entering the room." You'll see it on hotel signs next to the check-in time.

## Words to know

| Korean | Romanization | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| 혹시 | hok-si | By any chance, if possible |
| 짐 | jim | Luggage, baggage |
| 맡기다 | mat-gi-da | To leave in someone's care |
| 번호표 | beon-ho-pyo | Numbered tag, ticket |
| 몇 시부터 | myeot si-bu-teo | From what time |
| 입실 | ip-sil | Entering the room, check-in |

## The pattern

**혹시 [request] 수 있을까요?**

This is the most useful polite request structure in Korean. Swap in what you need:

- **혹시 짐 맡길 수 있을까요?** — Could I store my luggage?
- **혹시 일찍 체크인할 수 있을까요?** — Could I check in early?
- **혹시 늦게 체크아웃할 수 있을까요?** — Could I check out late?

All three are things hotels deal with every day. Ask politely and the answer is usually yes, or at least "we'll try."

## Early check-in and late checkout

Standard hotel hours in Korea: check-in at 3pm, checkout at 11am or noon.

**일찍 체크인 가능한가요?** — "Is early check-in possible?"
Some hotels offer it for free if the room is ready. Others charge a half-day fee. Worth asking.

**늦은 체크아웃 가능한가요?** — "Is late checkout possible?"
Same situation — free if it works out, sometimes a fee. Ask the night before or that morning.

## Picking up your luggage

When you're ready to get your bag:

**짐 찾으러 왔어요** — "I'm here to pick up my luggage"
*jim cha-jeu-reo wass-eo-yo*

Hand over your **번호표** and they'll bring it right out.

## What to actually do

1. Arrive at the hotel, go straight to the front desk
2. Say **혹시 체크인 전에 짐 맡길 수 있을까요?**
3. Get your **번호표** — put it somewhere you won't lose it
4. Ask **몇 시부터 입실 가능해요?** so you know when to come back
5. Go explore; return at check-in time with your tag

Five hours of free time instead of standing around a lobby. One question makes the difference.
