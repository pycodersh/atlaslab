## T-Locker vs. T-Luggage vs. Other Storage

Seoul Metro runs two services with similar names, and mixing them up is the most common mistake. **T-Locker** is the self-service locker. **T-Luggage** is a staffed counter that also handles same-day luggage delivery between subway stations and the airport. You can reserve it online at Tluggage.co.kr or register at the counter on the day.

Staffed T-Luggage counters sit on the B1 level at Seoul Station (between Exits 1 and 2), Hongik University Station (in the Airport Railroad connecting passage), Myeongdong Station (inside Exits 1, 9 and 10) and Gimpo Airport Station. The service only works on Seoul Metro Lines 1–9, and for delivery one end of the trip must be a staffed counter — locker-to-locker delivery is not available yet.

| | T-Locker | T-Luggage counter | App-booked shops & hotels |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **How it works** | Self-service touchscreen kiosk | Staff take and tag your bag | Book in an app or ask at the front desk |
| **Hours** | Station hours (reported as roughly 05:00 to midnight) | Roughly 10:00 to 22:00 — confirm on the day | Set by each shop or hotel |
| **Price** | Weekday base for 4 hours: 2,200 / 3,300 / 4,400 KRW (small / medium / large), then 500 / 800 / 1,000 KRW per extra hour | Priced by bag size — check Tluggage.co.kr | Listings often show around 7,000 KRW per bag per day |
| **Best for** | A few hours between sights | Heavy bags, or sending bags on to the airport | When station lockers are full |

**Convenience stores** such as GS25 and CU are built around sending parcels, not holding a bag for the day. If you want to ship something, see our guide to [sending packages from a post office or convenience store](/blog/en/k-patto/sending-packages-in-korea-post-office-convenience-store-lockers).
