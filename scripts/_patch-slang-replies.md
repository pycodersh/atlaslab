## Three-second replies to a Korean friend

You rarely need a full sentence. A quick answer plus a laugh is the standard reply, and two or three stacked initials cover most situations.

| Your friend texts | You reply | It means |
|---|---|---|
| "Sorry, I'm ten minutes late!" | ㄱㅊㄱㅊ | no worries |
| "This place is so good" | ㅇㅈ ㅋㅋㅋ | agreed, haha |
| "I passed the exam!" | ㅊㅋㅊㅋ ㅎㅎ | congrats! |
| "Watch this horror trailer" | ㄷㄷ ㅋㅋ | yikes, haha |
| "Dinner at seven?" | ㅇㅋ ㄱㄱ | okay, let's go |
| "I finally finished the project" | ㅅㄱ ㅎㅎ | good work |

The formula is **answer + laugh**. Pick the answer from the initials above, then add ㅋㅋ for a friend your age or ㅎㅎ for something a little softer.
