You walk in and a staff member says something as you pass. You nod and keep walking, not sure what was said.

It was an offer to help. And you probably needed it.

## The exchange

**도움 필요하시면 말씀해 주세요**
*do-um pil-yo-ha-si-myeon mal-sseum-hae ju-se-yo*
"Let me know if you need any help"

This is the standard greeting at Olive Young and most Korean beauty stores. You don't need to respond — a nod is fine. But if you do need something, this is your cue.

**저기, 혹시 컬러 립밤 어디 있어요?**
*jeo-gi, hok-si keol-leo rip-bam eo-di it-eo-yo?*
"Excuse me, where can I find the tinted lip balms?"

**저기** (*jeo-gi*) gets someone's attention — the equivalent of "excuse me" when approaching staff. **혹시** (*hok-si*) softens the question, like "by any chance" — it makes the ask feel less abrupt.

**립밤 코너는 이쪽 안쪽에 있어요**
*rip-bam ko-neo-neun i-jjok an-jjok-e it-eo-yo*
"The lip balm section is right over here inside"

**안쪽** (*an-jjok*) means "the inner area" or "further back." Korean stores often keep smaller or specialty items deeper inside, away from the entrance displays.

**아, 안쪽에 있었네요. 감사합니다!**
*a, an-jjok-e it-eot-ne-yo. gam-sa-ham-ni-da!*
"Ah, it was in the back. Thank you!"

## The most useful pattern in any store

**[item] 어디 있어요?** — "Where is [item]?"

Put whatever you're looking for in front of **어디 있어요?** and you have a complete question.

| What you want | How to ask |
|---|---|
| 선크림 (sunscreen) | 선크림 어디 있어요? |
| 마스크팩 (sheet mask) | 마스크팩 어디 있어요? |
| 쿠션 파운데이션 (cushion foundation) | 쿠션 파운데이션 어디 있어요? |
| 클렌징 오일 (cleansing oil) | 클렌징 오일 어디 있어요? |
| 토너 (toner) | 토너 어디 있어요? |

The product names are mostly the same as English, just said with Korean pronunciation. **선크림** is the one exception — Koreans say it instead of "sunscreen."

## Words to know

| Korean | Romanization | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| 저기요 | jeo-gi-yo | Excuse me (to get attention) |
| 혹시 | hok-si | By any chance, perhaps |
| 어디 있어요? | eo-di it-eo-yo? | Where is it? |
| 안쪽 | an-jjok | The back, inner area |
| 이쪽 | i-jjok | This way, over here |
| 코너 | ko-neo | Section, corner |

## Other things you'll be asked

**피부 타입이 어떻게 되세요?** — "What's your skin type?"
*pi-bu ta-ip-i eo-tteo-ke doe-se-yo?*

Staff at Olive Young often ask this before recommending a product. Useful answers: **건성** (*geon-seong*, dry), **지성** (*ji-seong*, oily), **복합성** (*bok-hap-seong*, combination), **민감성** (*min-gam-seong*, sensitive).

**샘플 드릴까요?** — "Would you like a sample?"
*saem-peul deu-ril-kka-yo?*

Say **네, 주세요** and you'll get a small packet to try. Olive Young is generous with samples.

**멤버십 있으세요?** — "Do you have a membership?"
**괜찮아요** if you don't — no pressure.

## What to actually do

1. When staff greet you, a nod is enough — no need to respond in Korean
2. To find something, say **저기요** to get attention, then **[item] 어디 있어요?**
3. **안쪽** means go further into the store — follow the direction they point
4. If asked about skin type, pick the closest one from the four above
5. Say **네, 주세요** when offered samples — they're free and useful

One phrase — **[item] 어디 있어요?** — gets you through any beauty store in Korea.
