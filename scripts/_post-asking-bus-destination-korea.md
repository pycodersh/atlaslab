You're at the bus stop. A bus pulls up. You're not sure if it's the right one.

This is the moment most visitors freeze. One sentence fixes it.

## The exchange

**기사님, 이거 명동역 가나요?**
*gi-sa-nim, i-geo myeong-dong-yeok ga-na-yo?*
"Excuse me, does this go to Myeongdong Station?"

**기사님** is how you address a driver — literally "driver sir." It works for bus drivers, taxi drivers, and delivery drivers. Always a good start.

**네, 명동 가요. 얼른 타요!**
*ne, myeong-dong ga-yo. eol-reun ta-yo!*
"Yes, it goes to Myeongdong. Hop on quick!"

**얼른** means "quickly" or "hurry up." Bus drivers say this a lot — not rudely, just efficiently. When you hear it, move.

**네, 감사합니다!**
*ne, gam-sa-ham-ni-da!*
"Okay, thank you!"

**손잡이 꼭 잡으세요!**
*son-ja-bi kkok ja-peu-se-yo!*
"Make sure to hold onto the handrail tight!"

Seoul buses accelerate fast. The driver is looking out for you.

## Words to know

| Korean | Romanization | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| 기사님 | gi-sa-nim | Driver (polite address) |
| 이거 | i-geo | This (bus/train) |
| ~가나요? | -ga-na-yo? | Does it go to ~? |
| 얼른 | eol-reun | Quickly, hurry |
| 손잡이 | son-ja-bi | Handrail, handle |
| 꼭 잡으세요 | kkok ja-peu-se-yo | Hold on tight |

## The pattern

**기사님, 이거 [destination] 가나요?**

Swap in any stop name:

- **홍대입구역 가나요?** — Does this go to Hongik University Station?
- **동대문 가나요?** — Does this go to Dongdaemun?
- **인사동 가나요?** — Does this go to Insadong?

If the answer is no, the driver will usually point or say the number of the bus you need. **몇 번 버스요?** (*myeot beon beo-seu-yo?* — "Which bus number?") is useful to follow up.

## How Seoul buses work

Seoul buses run on a color system:
- **Blue** (파란색) — long-distance routes across the city
- **Green** (초록색) — local neighborhood routes
- **Red** (빨간색) — express routes connecting suburbs to downtown
- **Yellow** (노란색) — loop routes in central areas

The number on the front and side tells you the route. Naver Maps and Kakao Maps both show real-time bus locations, so you can check before the bus arrives.

## Paying

Tap your T-money card or credit card when you board — the reader is next to the driver. The fare deducts automatically.

If you're transferring from the subway within 30 minutes, the transfer fare is discounted. Same card, same tap.

**카드 되나요?** (*ka-deu doe-na-yo?*) — "Do you take card?" — though all Seoul city buses do.

## When you're not sure which stop to get off

Turn on the audio announcements — they call every stop in Korean and English on most routes. Or just track your location on the map.

If you miss your stop: **다음 정류장에서 내릴게요** (*da-eum jeong-nyu-jang-e-seo nae-ril-ge-yo*) — "I'll get off at the next stop."

## What to actually do

1. Check the bus number on Naver or Kakao Maps before it arrives
2. When the bus pulls up, ask **기사님, 이거 [destination] 가나요?**
3. Board quickly when you hear **얼른 타요**
4. Tap your card, grab the handrail
5. Watch your stop on the map and tap out when you get off

One sentence at the door — **기사님, 이거 [destination] 가나요?** — and you're on the right bus every time.
