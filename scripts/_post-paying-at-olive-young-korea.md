You bring your items to the register. The staff asks two questions before the total appears.

Both are easy once you know what's coming.

## The exchange

**봉투 필요하세요? 100원 추가되세요**
*bong-tu pil-yo-ha-se-yo? baek won chu-ga-doe-se-yo*
"Do you need a bag? It's an extra 100 won."

Plastic bags cost money at almost every store in Korea — supermarkets, convenience stores, beauty stores. The charge is usually 100 to 200 won. Staff ask every time instead of bagging automatically.

**아니요, 그냥 가방에 넣을게요**
*a-ni-yo, geu-nyang ga-bang-e neo-eul-ge-yo*
"No, I'll just put it in my bag."

Or if you want one:

**네, 담아주세요** — "Yes, please bag it."

**결제는 카드로 도와드릴까요?**
*gyeol-je-neun ka-deu-ro do-wa-deu-ril-kka-yo?*
"Shall I help you pay by card?"

**네, 카드로 결제해 주세요**
*ne, ka-deu-ro gyeol-je-hae ju-se-yo*
"Yes, please process it by card."

## Words to know

| Korean | Romanization | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| 봉투 | bong-tu | Bag |
| 추가되다 | chu-ga-doe-da | To be added, extra charge |
| 결제 | gyeol-je | Payment |

| 현금 | hyeon-geum | Cash |

## The bag question

**봉투 필요하세요?** is one of the most common questions you'll hear at any Korean register. The answer is always one of these:

**네, 담아주세요** — "Yes, please"
**괜찮아요** — "No thanks"
**그냥 가방에 넣을게요** — "I'll put it in my bag"

**괜찮아요** works as a polite no to almost any offer at the register — the bag, a receipt, a points card. One phrase handles all of it.

## Paying by card

Foreign cards work at Olive Young with no issues. The reader is on the counter facing you — tap or insert it yourself.

**할부로 하시겠어요?** — "Would you like to pay in installments?"

This comes up for larger purchases. **일시불로 해주세요** (*il-si-bul-lo hae-ju-se-yo*) means "pay in full" — say this and the transaction goes through as a single charge.

**영수증 드릴까요?** — "Would you like a receipt?"
**괜찮아요** — or **네, 주세요** if you want one.

## Membership and points

**멤버십 있으세요?** — "Do you have a membership?"

Olive Young's app gives you points on every purchase and access to member-only discounts. If you're staying in Korea for more than a few days, it's worth setting up. You'll need a phone number to register.

If you don't have one: **괜찮아요** — no pressure, the transaction goes through normally.

## What to actually do

1. When asked **봉투 필요하세요?**, say **괜찮아요** to skip the bag or **네, 담아주세요** to get one
2. For card payment, say **카드로 결제해 주세요** or just tap your card when the reader lights up
3. If asked **할부로 하시겠어요?**, say **일시불로 해주세요** to pay in full
4. **괜찮아요** handles the receipt and points questions
5. Check for an Olive Young app discount before you pay — member prices are often lower

The whole checkout takes under a minute. **봉투**, **카드**, **괜찮아요** — three words cover most of it.
