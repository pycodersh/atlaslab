## Three more initials worth knowing

| Slang | Full form | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| ㄱㅊ | 괜찮아 | it's okay, no worries |
| ㄷㄷ | 덜덜 | trembling — "wow, scary" or "that's intense" |
| ㅈㄱㄴ | 제목이 곧 내용 | "the title is the content" |

**ㄱㅊ** is what you send when a friend says sorry for running late. Doubling it, **ㄱㅊㄱㅊ**, makes it warmer.

**ㄷㄷ** imitates shaking. Use it for something frightening, or something so impressive it gives you chills.

**ㅈㄱㄴ** shows up mostly in forum and community posts, where the title already says everything. You will read it more often than you type it.
