You're standing in front of two products that look almost identical. Same size, similar price, different name.

You could guess. Or you could just ask.

## The exchange

**이거 두 개 중에 뭐가 더 촉촉해요?**
*i-geo du gae jung-e mwo-ga deo chok-chok-hae-yo?*
"Between these two, which one is more moisturizing?"

**촉촉하다** (*chok-chok-ha-da*) is the word Koreans use for that dewy, hydrated skin feeling. You'll see it everywhere on packaging and in reviews. It doesn't translate perfectly — "moist" is close but not quite right. Think of it as the opposite of tight or dry.

**건조하시면 이 파란색 제품 추천드려요**
*geon-jo-ha-si-myeon i pa-ran-saek je-pum chu-cheon deu-ryeo-yo*
"If your skin is dry, I recommend this blue one"

Staff at Olive Young are usually well-trained and happy to give a real recommendation. Telling them your skin concern gets you a much more useful answer than pointing at a product and asking if it's good.

**테스터 써봐도 괜찮아요?**
*te-seu-teo sseo-bwa-do gwaen-chan-a-yo?*
"Can I try the tester?"

**네, 손등에 편하게 테스트해 보세요**
*ne, son-deung-e pyeon-ha-ge te-seu-teu-hae bo-se-yo*
"Yes, feel free to test it on the back of your hand"

**손등** (*son-deung*) is the back of the hand — the standard spot for testing skincare and foundation in Korean stores.

## Words to know

| Korean | Romanization | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| 촉촉하다 | chok-chok-ha-da | Moisturizing, dewy |
| 건조하다 | geon-jo-ha-da | Dry |
| 추천 | chu-cheon | Recommendation |
| 테스터 | te-seu-teo | Tester |
| 손등 | son-deung | Back of the hand |
| 제품 | je-pum | Product |

## Describing your skin concern

The more specific you are, the better the recommendation. These are the most useful phrases:

**피부가 건조해요** — "My skin is dry"
**피부가 지성이에요** — "I have oily skin"
**민감한 편이에요** — "I'm on the sensitive side"
**트러블이 자주 나요** — "I break out often"
**모공이 신경 쓰여요** — "I'm concerned about my pores"
**잡티가 있어요** — "I have some blemishes"

Pick the one that fits and add it before **추천해 주실 수 있어요?** — "Could you give me a recommendation?"

## Asking between two products

**두 개 중에 뭐가 더 ~해요?** is the pattern.

| What you want to compare | How to ask |
|---|---|
| More moisturizing | 뭐가 더 촉촉해요? |
| Lighter texture | 뭐가 더 가벼워요? |
| Better for sensitive skin | 민감한 피부에 뭐가 더 좋아요? |
| More popular | 뭐가 더 인기 있어요? |
| Better value | 뭐가 더 가성비 좋아요? |

**가성비** (*ga-seong-bi*) — value for money — is one of the most useful words in any Korean store. Staff will always have an opinion on it.

## At the tester station

Most Olive Young stores have a tester for every product on the shelf. Cotton swabs and wipes are usually provided nearby.

**이거 테스터 있어요?** — "Is there a tester for this?"
**이게 제 피부 톤이랑 맞을까요?** — "Will this match my skin tone?"

For foundation and cushion, staff will often help you find the right shade if you ask. Point at your face and say **제 톤에 맞는 거 추천해 주세요** — "Please recommend one that matches my tone."

## What to actually do

1. Pick up the two products you're deciding between and ask **두 개 중에 뭐가 더 촉촉해요?**
2. Mention your skin concern — **건조해요**, **지성이에요**, or whatever fits
3. When they recommend one, ask **테스터 써봐도 괜찮아요?**
4. Test on the back of your hand — **손등** — not your face
5. If you like it, say **이걸로 할게요** — "I'll take this one"

Two questions — what's better and can I try it — cover most of what happens at the product shelf.
