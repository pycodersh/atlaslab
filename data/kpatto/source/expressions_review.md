## [770] ~주세요

- **literal_en**: Give me ~, please.
- **usage_en**: Used to politely request something or place an order.
- **examples**:
  - ko: 물 주세요.
    en: Water, please.
  - ko: 메뉴판 주세요.
    en: Please give me the menu.
  - ko: 영수증 주세요.
    en: Please give me the receipt.
