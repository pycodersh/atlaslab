This master file will be the single source of truth for all pattern
cards.

## Pattern Card Format

### Pattern (KO)

\~주세요

### Literal Meaning (EN)

Give me \~, please.

### Usage (EN)

Used to politely request something or place an order.

### Examples

-   🇰🇷 물 주세요. 🇺🇸 Water, please.
-   🇰🇷 메뉴판 주세요. 🇺🇸 Please give me the menu.
-   🇰🇷 영수증 주세요. 🇺🇸 Please give me the receipt.
