Ask a Seoul local to name the city's grand hotel and most will say the Shilla without hesitating. Since 1979 it has hosted heads of state, celebrity weddings and the country's most important business dinners from its hillside perch in Jangchung-dong, where Namsan's forested slopes meet the old city wall. The setting feels almost suburban — sculpture gardens, pine trees, a sweeping driveway — yet Myeongdong and Dongdaemun are only minutes away by car.

Owned by Samsung's hospitality arm, the Shilla is famous less for flashy design than for consistency: immaculate rooms, polished service and some of the best food in any Korean hotel, including three-Michelin-starred La Yeon.

## Location & Getting There

<StayMap id="shilla" />

The Shilla sits on a hill above Dongho-ro, next to the Shilla Duty Free store and the Seoul City Wall trail.

* **Dongguk University Station (Line 3)**: Exit 5 is about 6 minutes on foot (0.4 km) downhill; the hotel also runs a shuttle to nearby subway and shopping stops — ask at the desk for the current route.
* **Myeongdong**: About 10 minutes by taxi.
* **Dongdaemun Design Plaza (DDP)**: About 5 minutes by taxi, for night markets and fashion malls.
* **N Seoul Tower**: Reachable on foot via Namsan's trails, or by taxi to the cable car.
* **From Incheon Airport**: Airport limousine buses serve the hotel area (about 70–90 minutes); a private transfer is easier with luggage.

## Rooms, Dining & Facilities

### The Rooms

The 463 rooms were fully redesigned in 2013 in a soft, modern style — warm woods, neutral fabrics and large marble bathrooms. Views look either toward Namsan's greenery or over the city.

* **Deluxe Rooms**: Comfortable and well-equipped, with a choice of mountain or city view.
* **Executive Rooms**: Include access to the executive lounge for breakfast and evening drinks.
* **Suites**: Popular with visiting dignitaries, with separate living and dining areas.
* **Best For**: First luxury trips to Seoul, special occasions and travelers who prize service above all.

### Dining & Drinks

* **La Yeon**: Three-Michelin-starred Korean fine dining on the top floor, serving refined royal-court-inspired menus.
* **The Parkview**: The hotel's all-day buffet, a Seoul institution for weekend family meals.
* **The Library**: A quiet bar-lounge for whisky and cocktails.

### Urban Island & Wellness

**Urban Island** is the Shilla's heated outdoor pool, open year-round, with hot tubs and cabanas set among the hotel gardens — swimming in warm water on a snowy winter evening is a uniquely Seoul experience. Access rules and fees for some areas vary by season and rate, so check when you book. An indoor pool, fitness centre and spa complete the facilities.

## The Verdict: Honest Pros & Cons

### What We Loved
* **Service and Consistency**: Few hotels in Asia run as smoothly.
* **Urban Island**: A year-round heated outdoor pool is a rare treat in Seoul.
* **World-Class Korean Dining**: La Yeon alone justifies a visit.

### What to Consider
* **Hillside Location**: Walking back up from the station is steep; you will rely on taxis or the shuttle.
* **Busy Weekends**: Weddings and events can make the lobby crowded on Saturdays.
* **Classic, Not Cutting-Edge**: Rooms are elegant but less design-forward than Seoul's newest openings.
