Halfway up Namsan, behind a screen of trees on Jangchungdan-ro, Banyan Tree Club & Spa Seoul feels nothing like a city hotel. There are only 50 rooms, all of them suites, and almost every one has its own **relaxation pool** set against a window full of forest and, in many cases, N Seoul Tower. Cars glide up a private driveway; the noise of downtown never quite reaches the terrace.

It is the closest thing Seoul has to a resort: a place to stay put, swim, take a long spa treatment and look out over the mountain rather than race around the city.

## Location & Getting There

<StayMap id="banyan-tree-seoul" />

The resort sits on Namsan's eastern slopes, near the National Theater of Korea and a short drive from the Shilla.

* **N Seoul Tower**: About 1.8 km uphill on foot along Namsan's paths, or a short taxi ride to the cable car or Namsan circulation buses.
* **Myeongdong**: About 15 minutes by taxi.
* **Nearest subway**: Dongguk University Station (Line 3) is downhill; most guests use taxis rather than walking back up.
* **From Incheon Airport**: Around 70–90 minutes by private transfer or taxi; airport limousine buses stop in central areas nearby.

## Rooms, Dining & Facilities

### The Suites

Every one of the 50 rooms is a suite, with generous living space, warm timber and stone, and a large bathroom. The signature feature is the **in-room relaxation pool** — a heated plunge pool beside a full-height window.

* **Deluxe Suites**: The entry category, with a relaxation pool and a forest or city outlook.
* **Namsan / Tower View Suites**: Ask for a room facing N Seoul Tower for the classic night view from the pool.
* **Larger Suites**: Extra space for families or special celebrations.
* **Best For**: Couples, honeymooners and anyone planning a slow, spa-centred stay.

### Dining

The resort's restaurants cover an all-day buffet, a lounge for afternoon tea and drinks, and in-suite dining — many guests simply order dinner to the room and stay in the pool. Check the current restaurant line-up when you book, as outlets and hours change seasonally.

### Spa & Pools

* **Banyan Tree Spa**: The brand's signature Asian-inspired massages and rituals, a highlight of any stay.
* **Oasis**: The large outdoor pool and lounge, a summer social scene in Seoul. Oasis is seasonal and usually charged separately, even for hotel guests — check dates and prices before you go.
* **Indoor pool, sauna and fitness centre**: Available year-round.

## The Verdict: Honest Pros & Cons

### What We Loved
* **A Pool in Every Suite**: The relaxation pools make this one of Seoul's most memorable rooms.
* **True Calm**: With only 50 suites and a forest setting, the resort is serene.
* **Namsan on the Doorstep**: Hiking trails and the tower are right above you.

### What to Consider
* **You'll Need Taxis**: The hillside location is far from subway exits.
* **Ageing Details**: Opened in 2010, some guests find parts of the property dated, including air conditioning in a few rooms.
* **Extra Costs**: Oasis access and dining add up quickly on top of suite rates.
