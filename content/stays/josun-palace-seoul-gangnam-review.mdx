The Josun name carries weight in Seoul. The original Chosun Hotel opened in 1914 as one of the first Western-style hotels in Korea, and in 2021 its owners revived that legacy with Josun Palace, their most ambitious property in a century. It occupies the upper floors of Centerfield, the twin office towers on Teheran-ro, and it is part of Marriott's Luxury Collection — officially *Josun Palace, a Luxury Collection Hotel, Seoul Gangnam*.

The design brief was "Korean Art Deco": geometric brass, lacquered timber, deep blues and golds, and a contemporary Korean art collection running through the public spaces. It feels grand in a way few new hotels dare to be, and the service — attentive, unhurried, personal — matches the setting.

**Quick Verdict** — Gangnam's most polished luxury hotel, with outstanding service and Michelin-level dining. Choose it for a special stay in the business heart of Gangnam; choose elsewhere if palaces and old Seoul are your priority.

## Location & Getting There

<StayMap id="josun-palace" />

Josun Palace sits on Teheran-ro, Gangnam's main business avenue, between Yeoksam and Seolleung stations. A separate hotel entrance at street level whisks you up to the sky lobby.

* **Yeoksam Station (Line 2)**: About 7 minutes on foot (0.5 km).
* **Gangnam Station**: One stop west on Line 2, for shopping and nightlife.
* **COEX & Starfield**: About 10 minutes by taxi.
* **Garosu-gil and Apgujeong**: 15–20 minutes by taxi for boutiques, cafés and K-beauty flagships.
* **From Incheon Airport**: Allow 70–90 minutes by airport limousine bus or private transfer, depending on traffic.

## Rooms, Dining & Facilities

### The Rooms

The hotel's 254 rooms and suites are some of the most generously finished in Seoul: dark timber, brass detailing, marble bathrooms and plush, tailored furnishings. Large windows look over Gangnam's grid of towers and, from some rooms, toward the hills south of the city.

* **Deluxe Rooms**: The entry category, already spacious, with a separate bathtub and walk-in shower.
* **Grand Deluxe & Club Rooms**: Higher floors and, with club categories, access to the executive lounge.
* **Suites**: Come with the hotel's private, butler-style service for arrivals, unpacking and dinner reservations — this is where Josun Palace truly distinguishes itself.
* **Best For**: Milestone trips, luxury travelers loyal to Marriott Bonvoy, and business travelers who want something more memorable than a chain box.

### Dining & Drinks

* **Constans**: The all-day buffet, widely considered among the best hotel buffets in Seoul, with an emphasis on carefully cooked Korean and international dishes rather than sheer volume.
* **Eatanic Garden**: A Michelin-starred tasting room serving contemporary Korean cuisine with sweeping city views.
* **1914 Lounge & Bar**: Named after the original Chosun Hotel's opening year, for cocktails and afternoon tea.

### Wellness

A fitness centre, an indoor pool and a spa round out the facilities; all are restrained and immaculately kept.

## The Verdict: Honest Pros & Cons

### What We Loved
* **Service at Its Peak**: Staff remember names and preferences; suite butler service is a genuine luxury.
* **Distinctive Design**: Korean Art Deco gives the hotel a sense of place that most Gangnam towers lack.
* **Destination Dining**: Constans and Eatanic Garden are worth a visit even for non-guests.

### What to Consider
* **Business-District Location**: Teheran-ro is lively on weekdays but quieter at weekends, and the palaces are 40+ minutes away.
* **Not Directly on the Subway**: Yeoksam Station is a short walk, not an indoor connection.
* **Premium Pricing**: Rates sit at the very top of the Seoul market, and dining prices follow.
