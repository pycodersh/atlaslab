Rising high above the Songdo international district inside the landmark 68-story Posco Tower, Oakwood Premier Incheon delivers a luxury experience built around soaring scale and complete independence. While conventional 5-star hotels focus solely on bedrooms, Oakwood blends five-star hospitality services with full residential apartment layouts, positioning all guest suites between the 38th and 64th floors.

For international travelers flying in through Incheon Airport (ICN), families traveling with children, and digital nomads planning multi-week stays, it solves the most common hotel frustrations: cramped quarters, lack of laundry facilities, and reliance on expensive room service for every single meal.

---

## The Space & Heritage

Occupying the upper half of what was once South Korea’s tallest skyscraper, Oakwood Premier was designed to capture the maritime grandeur of the Yellow Sea and the geometric precision of Songdo’s master plan:
* **Sky Lobby on the 36th Floor**: Check-in begins high above the ground, where sweeping glass facades immediately frame panoramic vistas of the Incheon Grand Bridge and tidal mudflats.
* **Apartment Living, Hotel Standards**: Unlike vacation rental apartments, guests enjoy 24-hour concierge desks, daily housekeeping, bell service, and secure high-speed elevators.
* **Direct Underground Integration**: The tower connects directly through underground corridors to Lotte Mart, Convensia Convention Center, and nearby dining arcades, keeping you completely shielded from winter winds or summer rain.

## Room Deep Dive: Which Category to Book?

Every room here functions as a self-contained private residence featuring full induction cooktops, dishwashers, full-size refrigerators, and in-unit washer/dryers:

### 1. Studio Superior / Deluxe (Open-Plan Living)
* **The Experience**: An expansive open-layout suite (approx. 50–55 sqm) combining a king bed, living lounge, and a modern open kitchen.
* **Best For**: Solo business travelers and couples seeking generous living space beyond conventional hotel room sizes.

### 2. One-Bedroom Superior Suite (Editor’s Pick)
* **The Experience**: Features a separate private bedroom closed off by sliding pocket doors from a sprawling living room and 4-person dining table.
* **Why Upgrade**: Offers true residential zoning—work or relax in the living room without disturbing a partner sleeping in the bedroom. Includes a deep soaking tub positioned beside expansive view windows.

### 3. Two-Bedroom Suites & Penthouse (Extended Family Stays)
* **The Experience**: Massive residential layouts with multiple en-suite bathrooms, full culinary kitchens, and separate dining rooms.
* **Best For**: Large families, traveling groups, or long-term assignees who require dedicated private bedrooms under one roof.

## Dining & Sky Lounges: Panoramic 65

While suites allow full self-catering, the property hosts standout dining destinations:
* **Oak Restaurant (36th Floor)**: Serves a comprehensive morning buffet blending Western eggs-to-order with authentic Korean hot stations (including seasonal seaweed soup, braised short ribs, and fresh kimchi).
* **Panoramic 65 (65th Floor)**: Set on the tower's top floor, it is one of the highest hotel lounge bars in South Korea. Divided into a casual wine bar, private dining alcoves, and a live performance stage, it offers craft cocktails framed by 360-degree night views over the city grid and offshore shipping lanes.

## The Verdict: Honest Pros & Cons

### What We Loved
* **Complete Self-Sufficiency**: Having a washer/dryer in your own bathroom and a full-size refrigerator completely transforms extended travels and family logistics.
* **Unobstructed Views**: Being situated from the 38th floor upward means even standard rooms enjoy clear, sweeping skyline or coastal perspectives.
* **Direct Transit & Mall Access**: Grocery shopping and dining without ever needing an umbrella or winter coat.

### What to Consider
* **Elevator Transfers**: Reaching your room requires taking an elevator to the 36th-floor Sky Lobby first, then transferring to guest-room elevator banks.
* **No Outdoor Pool**: While an indoor fitness center and children’s playroom are available, there is no outdoor sun deck or leisure swimming pool on-site.
