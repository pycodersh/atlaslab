Yeouido's skyline changed for good when Parc.1 rose beside the Han River: a cluster of towers framed by giant red steel columns, designed by the late Richard Rogers' studio. Fairmont Ambassador Seoul occupies part of that complex, and the red frame follows you inside, from the dramatic arrival to the floor-to-ceiling windows in the rooms.

Opened in 2021, it is one of Seoul's newest luxury hotels, and its biggest draw sits directly below: **The Hyundai Seoul**, the city's most talked-about department store, reached through an underground connection. Add river views, Yeouido Hangang Park and the cherry-blossom avenues nearby, and you have a stylish, practical base on the west side of the city.

## Location & Getting There

<StayMap id="fairmont-ambassador" />

The hotel sits in Parc.1 on Yeoui-daero, in the heart of Yeouido.

* **The Hyundai Seoul**: Connected underground — shops, food halls and the indoor garden without going outside.
* **Yeouido Hangang Park & cruise pier**: About 12 minutes on foot (0.9 km), near Yeouinaru Station (Line 5).
* **Yeouido Station (Lines 5 and 9)**: A short walk away, with Line 9 express trains to Gimpo Airport and Gangnam.
* **63 Square**: About 5 minutes by taxi.
* **From Incheon Airport**: Airport limousine buses serve Yeouido; or take AREX to Gongdeok, then Line 5 two stops to Yeouido.

## Rooms, Dining & Facilities

### The Rooms

The 308 rooms are crisp and contemporary — pale woods, soft greys, marble bathrooms — with big windows over Yeouido or toward the river.

* **Fairmont Rooms**: The entry category, comfortable and modern.
* **Han River / Skyline View Rooms**: Worth the upgrade for sunset over the water.
* **Fairmont Gold**: Club-level rooms with lounge access for breakfast and evening drinks.
* **Best For**: Shoppers, business travelers and families who want new rooms and easy logistics.

{/* IMAGE PLACEHOLDER: Fairmont Ambassador Seoul room with a Yeouido skyline view */}

### Dining & Drinks

* **Mariposa**: The hotel's high-floor bar, with cocktails and views over the Yeouido skyline and the river.
* **All-day dining and specialty restaurants**: A buffet and à la carte options cover breakfast through dinner.
* **The Hyundai Seoul food hall**: One of the city's best, directly connected.

### Wellness

An indoor pool, fitness centre and sauna are available; some guests report that pool sessions need to be booked in advance, so reserve your slot at check-in.

## The Verdict: Honest Pros & Cons

### What We Loved
* **The Hyundai Seoul Connection**: Seoul's most exciting department store is downstairs.
* **New, Stylish Rooms**: The Parc.1 architecture and modern interiors feel fresh.
* **River Access**: Yeouido Hangang Park and the cruise pier are easy walks.

### What to Consider
* **West-Side Location**: Gyeongbokgung and Bukchon are 30–40 minutes away.
* **Thin Walls in Places**: A few guests mention noise between rooms.
* **Quiet Weekends**: Yeouido is a business district; nightlife is limited after the malls close.
