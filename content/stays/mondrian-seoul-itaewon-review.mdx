Itaewon has always been Seoul's most international neighbourhood — a hillside tangle of global restaurants, cocktail bars, vintage shops and late-night clubs — and Mondrian Seoul Itaewon was built to plug straight into it. Opened in 2020, it brings Mondrian's playful, fashion-forward style to a 295-room property within walking distance of Itaewon-ro, Gyeongnidan-gil and the galleries and boutiques of Hannam-dong.

This is a hotel for the evening as much as the night: rooftop drinks with a Namsan view, a pool scene in summer, then dinner somewhere down the hill.

## Location & Getting There

<StayMap id="mondrian" />

The hotel sits on Jangmun-ro, on the quieter southern side of Itaewon.

* **Itaewon Station (Line 6)**: About 10 minutes on foot (0.9 km).
* **Leeum Museum of Art**: About 20 minutes on foot (1.6 km) through Hannam-dong, Seoul's top private art museum.
* **Gyeongnidan-gil**: Cafés and small restaurants on the slopes toward Namsan, a short taxi ride away.
* **N Seoul Tower**: About 15 minutes by taxi.
* **From Incheon Airport**: Airport limousine buses serve the Itaewon / Yongsan area (about 70–90 minutes); or AREX to Seoul Station, then a short taxi.

## Rooms, Dining & Facilities

### The Rooms

Rooms are stylish and compact, with bold colour accents, curated art and sleek bathrooms. Upper floors look toward Namsan or across Yongsan.

* **Standard / Deluxe Rooms**: Well-designed but on the smaller side — pack light.
* **Namsan View Rooms**: Worth requesting for the city-and-tower outlook.
* **Suites**: More space and better views for longer stays.
* **Best For**: Couples, friends' trips, nightlife lovers and design fans.

{/* IMAGE PLACEHOLDER: Mondrian Seoul Itaewon room with bold design accents */}

### Dining & Drinks

* **Privilege**: The rooftop bar, with cocktails, DJs and a wide view toward Namsan — the hotel's social heart.
* **All-day restaurant**: Breakfast and casual dining downstairs.
* **Itaewon & Hannam**: Hundreds of restaurants, from Middle Eastern to modern Korean, within a short walk or taxi.

### Pool & Wellness

**Altitude**, the outdoor pool, is the summer hotspot; access rules and fees vary by season and some areas (including the sauna) carry extra charges, so check before you book. A fitness centre is available year-round.

## The Verdict: Honest Pros & Cons

### What We Loved
* **Rooftop Atmosphere**: Privilege is one of the best hotel bars in Seoul for a view and a buzz.
* **Neighbourhood Access**: Itaewon, Gyeongnidan-gil and Hannam-dong are all close.
* **Design-Led Style**: Bold, playful interiors unlike Seoul's corporate hotels.

### What to Consider
* **Small Rooms**: Entry categories are compact; families should look at suites.
* **Noise**: A lively pool, bar and neighbourhood mean some rooms hear the party.
* **Extra Fees**: Outdoor pool and sauna access can cost extra.
