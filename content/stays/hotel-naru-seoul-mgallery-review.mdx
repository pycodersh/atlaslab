Few hotels in Seoul sit as close to the Han River as Hotel Naru. Opened in 2022 at the north end of Mapo Bridge, the 196-room MGallery property faces the water head-on: Bamseom's wooded islets in the foreground, the towers of Yeouido across the river, and a continuous ribbon of bridges and traffic lights after dark. "Naru" is an old Korean word for a river ferry crossing — fitting for a hotel built right where boats once landed at Mapo.

Its signature is the **infinity pool**, which seems to spill straight into the river, and a design language of soft curves, warm wood and muted tones that feels more boutique than big-brand.

**Quick Verdict** — Seoul's best Han River view hotel, with a show-stopping infinity pool and a balanced location for the airport, Hongdae and Yeouido. Ideal for couples and design lovers; less ideal if you want to walk to the palaces.

## Location & Getting There

<StayMap id="hotel-naru" />

The hotel stands on Mapo-daero beside the north end of Mapo Bridge.

* **Mapo Station (Line 5)**: About 4 minutes on foot (0.3 km).
* **Gongdeok Station**: One stop on Line 5, where you can connect to the AREX airport railway and Lines 6 and Gyeongui-Jungang.
* **Yeouido Hangang Park**: About 5 minutes by taxi across Mapo Bridge, or one stop on Line 5 to Yeouinaru.
* **Hongdae**: Around 15 minutes by taxi.
* **From Incheon Airport**: AREX (all-stop train) to Gongdeok, then one stop on Line 5 to Mapo.

## Rooms, Dining & Facilities

### The Rooms

The rooms are calm and contemporary — pale wood, curved furniture, soft textiles — with large windows designed around the river.

* **City View Rooms**: The entry category; comfortable, but you'll be looking inland.
* **Han River View Rooms**: The reason to book Naru — wake up to Bamseom and the Yeouido skyline.
* **Suites**: Corner layouts with wider river panoramas.
* **Best For**: Couples, photographers and anyone who wants the river as a backdrop.

{/* IMAGE PLACEHOLDER: Hotel Naru Seoul Han River view room facing Bamseom and Yeouido */}

### Dining & Drinks

* **Voisin**: The hotel's signature restaurant, with the river as a backdrop to its menus.
* **In-room dining**: Frequently praised by guests — a fine alternative on a rainy night.
* **Mapo's local food streets**: Famous for grilled pork and galbi restaurants, a short walk away.

### Infinity Pool & Wellness

The **infinity pool** looks straight out over the Han River and is the most photographed spot in the hotel; sunset sessions are especially popular. A fitness centre is also available. Pool access may be scheduled by time slot on busy days, so check at check-in.

## The Verdict: Honest Pros & Cons

### What We Loved
* **The River View**: Possibly the best Han River outlook of any Seoul hotel.
* **The Infinity Pool**: Spectacular at sunset.
* **Balanced Location**: Easy reach of the airport line, Hongdae and Yeouido.

### What to Consider
* **City-View Rooms**: Without the river, the experience is far less special — budget for the upgrade.
* **Not in the Palace District**: Gyeongbokgung and Bukchon are 25–30 minutes away.
* **Busy Pool**: The infinity pool is popular and can feel crowded at peak times.
