L7 is Lotte's playful lifestyle brand, and its Hongdae outpost might be its best location: right on Yanghwa-ro, about three minutes from Hongik Univ. Station, where the AREX airport line, Line 2 and the Gyeongui-Jungang Line meet. Opened in 2018, the 340-room hotel keeps prices sensible while adding the one thing every Hongdae visitor secretly wants — a **rooftop pool** with a bar, looking across the neighbourhood's rooftops toward the Han River.

It is a smart, fun and practical base for first-time visitors who plan to spend their days exploring and their evenings in Hongdae.

## Location & Getting There

<StayMap id="l7-hongdae" />

* **Hongik Univ. Station (AREX, Line 2, Gyeongui-Jungang Line)**: Exit 9 is about 3 minutes on foot.
* **Hongdae streets**: About 11 minutes on foot to the busking and club streets near Hongik University.
* **Yeonnam-dong & Gyeongui Line Forest Park**: About 14 minutes on foot.
* **Myeongdong & City Hall**: About 20 minutes on Line 2.
* **From Incheon Airport**: AREX all-stop train straight to Hongik Univ. Station (about 50 minutes).

## Rooms, Dining & Facilities

### The Rooms

Rooms are bright, compact and cleverly designed, with good beds, smart storage and playful details.

* **Standard Rooms**: Efficient and comfortable for short stays.
* **Higher-Floor & Corner Rooms**: Better views over Hongdae toward the river.
* **Family / Larger Rooms**: Available, but still tight by Western standards — check square metres when booking.
* **Best For**: Friends' trips, couples and value-minded travelers.

{/* IMAGE PLACEHOLDER: L7 Hongdae room with bright, compact design */}

### Rooftop Pool & Bar

The **rooftop pool** is the hotel's showpiece, paired with a bar and an Italian restaurant so you can swim, then stay for drinks as the lights come on across Hongdae. Pool hours and access rules can change by season, so check at check-in.

### Other Facilities

A fitness centre and luggage storage — handy on arrival and departure days — round out the facilities.

## Sample 1-Day Itinerary

* **09:00 — Quick breakfast** in Yeonnam-dong's cafés.
* **10:30 — Gyeongbokgung Palace**: Line 2 and Line 3, or use a Discover Seoul Pass for palace and attraction entry.
* **14:00 — Bukchon & Insadong**: Hanok alleys and tea houses.
* **17:00 — Back to Hongdae**: A swim in the rooftop pool.
* **19:30 — Dinner in Hongdae**: Korean barbecue or street food.
* **21:30 — Rooftop bar**, then live music in the neighbourhood.

## The Verdict: Honest Pros & Cons

### What We Loved
* **Station on the Doorstep**: Airport rail and Line 2 three minutes away.
* **Rooftop Pool**: Rare in this price bracket.
* **Strong Value**: Good design and service for a four-star rate.

### What to Consider
* **Small Rooms**: Especially family categories.
* **Road Noise**: Yanghwa-ro is busy at night; ask for a higher floor.
* **Crowds**: Hongdae is packed at weekends.
