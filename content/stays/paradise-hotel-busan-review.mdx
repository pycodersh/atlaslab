Of all the hotels lining Haeundae Beach, Paradise Hotel Busan has the most classic seaside address: right on the sand in the middle of Korea's most famous beach, with the hotel's outdoor pools looking straight out to sea. Open since 1987 and renovated in 2017, the 529-room resort has hosted generations of Korean family holidays — and it still delivers the kind of all-in-one beach stay that is hard to find in a big city.

Its hot-spring-fed **Cimer** spa, outdoor ocean-view pools and easy access to Dongbaek Island and Haeundae Market make it a natural first choice for a Busan escape.

## Location & Getting There

<StayMap id="paradise-busan" />

The hotel stands on Haeundaehaebyeon-ro, facing the centre of Haeundae Beach.

* **Haeundae Beach**: Directly in front of the hotel.
* **Haeundae Traditional Market**: A few minutes on foot, for fish cakes, gukbap and seafood.
* **Haeundae Station (Busan Metro Line 2)**: About 12 minutes on foot (0.9 km).
* **Dongbaek Island**: About 20 minutes along the beach (1.5 km), with a coastal trail and the Nurimaru APEC House.
* **Haeundae Blueline Park (Mipo)**: At the east end of the beach, for the Beach Train and Sky Capsules.
* **From Seoul**: KTX to Busan Station (about 2.5 hours), then Metro Line 1 and Line 2 or a 30–40 minute taxi.
* **From Gimhae Airport**: About 40–50 minutes by taxi or airport limousine bus.

## Rooms, Dining & Facilities

### The Rooms

Rooms are spread across two buildings, with a mix of ocean-view and city-view categories.

* **Ocean View Deluxe**: Worth the upgrade — wake up to the full sweep of Haeundae Beach.
* **City View Rooms**: Cheaper, but you lose the hotel's main appeal.
* **Suites & Family Rooms**: Extra space for families, with kids' club access nearby.
* **Best For**: Families, couples and first-time visitors to Busan.

{/* IMAGE PLACEHOLDER: Paradise Hotel Busan Ocean View Deluxe room over Haeundae Beach */}

### Dining

* **On the Plate**: The hotel's large international buffet, a popular family choice.
* **Specialty restaurants**: Korean, Japanese and Western dining across the property.
* **Haeundae Market**: Busan street food a short walk away.

### Cimer Spa & Pools

**Cimer** is the hotel's ocean spa, combining hot-spring baths, saunas and relaxation areas with sea views; it gets busy at peak times, so early mornings are calmest. The **outdoor ocean-view pools** are heated and open year-round, and a kids' club keeps younger guests busy.

## The Verdict: Honest Pros & Cons

### What We Loved
* **Directly on the Beach**: No road to cross — the sand is right there.
* **Year-Round Ocean Pools & Spa**: Cimer and the heated outdoor pools work in every season.
* **Family-Friendly**: Kids' club, buffet and easy beach access.

### What to Consider
* **Dated in Places**: Some rooms and facilities show their age despite the 2017 renovation.
* **Peak-Season Crowds**: Cimer and the pools get busy in summer and on holidays.
* **Pricey Extras**: Some guests find the breakfast buffet expensive for what it offers.
