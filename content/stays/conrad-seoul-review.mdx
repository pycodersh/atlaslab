Conrad Seoul is Yeouido's original luxury landmark: a glass tower inside the IFC complex, sharing its base with IFC Mall and looking straight out over Yeouido Park and the Han River. Since opening in 2012 it has earned a loyal following among business travelers, Hilton Honors members and families, mainly for one reason — the rooms are big.

It is also one of the easiest luxury hotels in Seoul to reach from either airport. An underground walkway links the hotel to Yeouido Station, where Line 9 runs straight to Gimpo Airport and Line 5 connects to the AREX airport railway two stops away.

## Location & Getting There

<StayMap id="conrad" />

The hotel is part of IFC Seoul on Gukjegeumyung-ro, Yeouido's financial district.

* **Yeouido Station (Lines 5 and 9)**: About 7 minutes on foot through IFC Mall and the underground passage.
* **Yeouido Hangang Park & cruise pier**: About 10 minutes on foot (0.7 km).
* **The Hyundai Seoul**: Across the road, a few minutes' walk.
* **63 Square**: About 5 minutes by taxi.
* **From Incheon Airport**: AREX to Gongdeok, then Line 5 to Yeouido; or AREX/Line 9 via Gimpo. Airport limousine buses also serve Yeouido.
* **From Gimpo Airport**: Line 9 to Yeouido, around 15–20 minutes on an express train.

## Rooms, Dining & Facilities

### The Rooms

The 434 rooms start at sizes that would count as junior suites in many Seoul hotels, with deep bathtubs, walk-in showers and large windows. Interiors are modern and neutral; a few details now look slightly dated, but space and comfort are excellent.

* **Deluxe Rooms**: Ask for a **Han River view** to look over Yeouido Park and the water.
* **Executive Rooms**: Include lounge access for breakfast, afternoon snacks and evening drinks.
* **Suites**: Popular with families for longer stays.
* **Best For**: Families, business travelers, Hilton Honors members.

### Dining & Drinks

* **37 Grill & Bar**: High-floor grill and cocktails with sweeping views over the river.
* **Zest**: The hotel's all-day buffet.
* **IFC Mall**: Dozens of restaurants, cafés and a cinema directly below.

### Wellness

An indoor pool, a well-equipped fitness centre, sauna and spa round out the facilities.

## The Verdict: Honest Pros & Cons

### What We Loved
* **Generous Room Sizes**: Among the most spacious standard rooms of any Seoul luxury hotel.
* **Airport-Friendly Transit**: Underground access to Lines 5 and 9 makes both airports easy.
* **River Views**: Han River and Yeouido Park outlooks from many rooms.

### What to Consider
* **Late Check-In**: Standard check-in is 16:00, an hour later than most Seoul hotels.
* **Slightly Dated Décor**: Guests mention some rooms showing their age, and Wi-Fi can be inconsistent.
* **Quiet After Dark**: Yeouido's business district empties out at night.
