Jeju's Jungmun Resort area has long been the island's luxury coast, and Parnas Hotel Jeju, opened in 2022, is its most striking newcomer. The 307-room resort sits on the cliffs above **Jungmun Saekdal Beach**, with a path leading down to the sand and — its headline — a **110-metre outdoor infinity pool**, billed as the longest in Korea, that seems to pour straight into the sea.

From the pool deck, the view sweeps across Jeju's southern coast; behind you, the slopes rise toward Hallasan. It is the kind of place where you plan one big day out and spend the rest by the water.

## Location & Getting There

<StayMap id="parnas-jeju" />

The resort sits in the Jungmun Tourist Complex on the south coast, in Seogwipo.

* **Jungmun Saekdal Beach**: A path with stairs leads down from the hotel, about 7 minutes on foot.
* **Cheonjeyeon Falls**: About 5 minutes by taxi — a three-tier waterfall below the Seonimgyo Bridge.
* **Daepo Jusangjeolli Cliff**: About 10 minutes by taxi, for hexagonal basalt columns rising from the sea.
* **Jungmun's museums and attractions**: Teddy Bear Museum and others close by.
* **From Jeju Airport**: About 1 hour by taxi or rental car; Airport Limousine Bus 600 serves the Jungmun resort hotels (check the stop nearest the hotel).

## Rooms, Dining & Facilities

### The Rooms

Rooms are bright and contemporary, with balconies in many categories and ocean or garden views.

* **Ocean View Rooms**: The ones to book — sunsets over the south coast from your balcony.
* **Garden / Mountain View Rooms**: Quieter and cheaper, facing inland.
* **Suites & Family Rooms**: Extra space for families on longer island stays.
* **Best For**: Couples, families and pool-focused holidays.

{/* IMAGE PLACEHOLDER: Parnas Hotel Jeju ocean view room with a balcony */}

### Dining

The resort offers an all-day buffet and specialty restaurants, plus lounges for drinks with sea views. Breakfast is generous but priced at a premium, so compare room rates with breakfast included.

### The Infinity Pool & Wellness

The **110-metre infinity pool** is the reason most guests come: heated, set right on the cliff edge and spectacular at sunset. Indoor pool, sauna, spa and fitness facilities give you options on windy or rainy Jeju days. Pool access and hours vary by season and can be busy in summer.

## The Verdict: Honest Pros & Cons

### What We Loved
* **The Infinity Pool**: Truly one of a kind in Korea.
* **Beach Access**: Jungmun Saekdal Beach is a short walk down the cliff.
* **Resort Setting**: Sea views, gardens and a calm, holiday atmosphere.

### What to Consider
* **Car-Dependent**: You'll need taxis, a rental car or a driver to explore Jeju.
* **Wear and Tear**: Some guests report rust on balconies and ageing bathroom details despite the recent opening.
* **Weather-Dependent Views**: Jeju's wind and fog can hide the sea.
