Since 1988, the Grand InterContinental Seoul Parnas has been one of Gangnam's defining addresses — the hotel that hosted summits, trade delegations and generations of Seoul wedding banquets on Teheran-ro. After a major renovation completed in 2020, it reopened with larger, brighter rooms and a much more contemporary feel. IHG now lists it as *InterContinental Grand Seoul Parnas*, so you may see both names when booking.

Its real superpower is logistics. The hotel connects underground to COEX Mall, Parnas Mall and Samseong Station, and airport buses stop right at the entrance. For a first trip to Seoul, an early flight, or a COEX trade show, very few hotels make life this easy.

## Location & Getting There

<StayMap id="grand-intercontinental-parnas" />

The hotel stands at the corner of Teheran-ro and Yeongdong-daero, joined underground to the whole COEX complex.

* **Samseong Station (Line 2)**: Reached indoors via Parnas Mall.
* **COEX Mall, Starfield Library & COEX Aquarium**: An indoor walk through the mall; the aquarium at the far end is about 1 km.
* **Bongeunsa Temple**: About 8 minutes on foot across Bongeunsa-ro.
* **Lotte World Tower & Jamsil**: About 15 minutes by taxi.
* **From Incheon Airport**: Airport limousine buses on the COEX route stop at the hotel entrance, and the City Airport Terminal building next door is another limousine hub. In-town airline check-in there has been limited in recent years, so confirm services before relying on it.

## Rooms, Dining & Facilities

### The Rooms

The 2020 renovation reworked every room, and entry categories feel comfortably spacious by Seoul standards. The look is light and modern — pale wood, soft fabrics, large windows over Gangnam.

* **Deluxe Rooms**: Comfortable, practical and family-friendly; connecting rooms are available.
* **Club InterContinental Rooms**: Include access to the club lounge for breakfast, afternoon tea and evening cocktails, plus a smoother check-in. Good value if you would otherwise buy breakfast and drinks.
* **Suites**: Separate living areas for longer stays and families.
* **Best For**: Families, first-time visitors, trade-show attendees and IHG One Rewards members.

### Dining & Drinks

* **Grand Kitchen**: The all-day buffet, popular with locals for weekend brunch.
* **Specialty restaurants and lobby lounge**: Cover Chinese, Japanese and Western dining, plus afternoon tea.
* **Parnas Mall & COEX**: Hundreds of casual restaurants and cafés a short indoor walk away.

### Wellness

An indoor pool, fitness centre and sauna are available to guests; the pool is a pleasant, spacious option for families after a day in the mall.

## Sample 1-Day Itinerary

* **08:00 — Breakfast in the club lounge** (or at Grand Kitchen).
* **09:30 — Bongeunsa Temple**: A calm morning walk among the halls and the giant Maitreya Buddha.
* **11:00 — Starfield Library**: The famous floor-to-ceiling bookshelves inside COEX Mall.
* **12:30 — COEX Aquarium**: An easy indoor highlight for families.
* **15:00 — Hyundai Department Store & K-pop shops**: Browse the COEX area's shopping.
* **18:00 — Rest at the hotel**, then dinner in Parnas Mall.
* **Departure day**: Catch the airport limousine from the hotel entrance — no subway transfers with luggage.

## The Verdict: Honest Pros & Cons

### What We Loved
* **Airport Made Easy**: Limousine buses at the entrance are a real gift on arrival and departure days.
* **Indoor Everything**: Subway, COEX, Parnas Mall and the aquarium are all reachable without stepping outside.
* **Renovated Rooms**: The 2020 makeover delivered more space and a modern feel.

### What to Consider
* **Big and Busy**: With 550 rooms and frequent events, the lobby can feel bustling, especially on convention days.
* **Room Size Varies**: Some guests still find certain categories smaller than expected; compare square metres when booking.
* **Breakfast Costs Extra**: The buffet is pricey on room-only rates, which makes club rooms worth comparing.
