At the eastern end of Haeundae Beach, the LCT Landmark Tower rises above the sand like a glass sail — Busan's tallest building and home to Signiel Busan, Lotte's ultra-luxury brand. Opened in 2020, the 260-room hotel brings the polish of Signiel Seoul to the seaside: calm, residential interiors, floor-to-ceiling windows and a direct line of sight down the length of Haeundae Beach toward Dongbaek Island and, on clear nights, the lights of Gwangan Bridge across the bay.

Unlike its Seoul sibling, Signiel Busan keeps you close to the water rather than in the clouds. The beach, the Blueline Park station at Mipo and the BUSAN X the SKY observatory at the top of the same tower are all a short walk from your room.

## Location & Getting There

<StayMap id="signiel-busan" />

The hotel occupies part of LCT on Dalmaji-gil, where Haeundae Beach ends and the Mipo coastline begins.

* **Haeundae Beach**: Right outside.
* **Haeundae Blueline Park (Mipo Station)**: A few minutes on foot, for the Beach Train and Sky Capsules.
* **BUSAN X the SKY**: The observatory at the top of the same tower.
* **Jung-dong & Haeundae stations (Busan Metro Line 2)**: About 10–20 minutes on foot.
* **Dongbaek Island**: About 30 minutes along the beach (2.2 km).
* **From Seoul**: KTX to Busan Station (about 2.5 hours), then a 30–40 minute taxi.
* **From Gimhae Airport**: About 40–50 minutes by taxi; the hotel does not run its own airport shuttle.

## Rooms, Dining & Facilities

### The Rooms

Rooms are serene and generously sized, with soft neutral palettes, deep bathtubs and windows that frame the sea.

* **Deluxe Ocean View**: The essential choice — Haeundae Beach and the ocean from bed.
* **Premier & Executive Rooms**: Higher categories, some with access to **Salon de Signiel**, the hotel's private lounge for breakfast, afternoon tea and evening drinks. Confirm lounge access in your rate.
* **Suites**: Expansive corner layouts with panoramic ocean views.
* **Best For**: Couples, honeymooners and special-occasion trips.

{/* IMAGE PLACEHOLDER: Signiel Busan Deluxe Ocean View room facing Haeundae Beach */}

### Dining & Drinks

The hotel's restaurants cover an all-day buffet, Korean and fine-dining options and a lounge bar, all designed around the sea view. Haeundae's seafood restaurants and the Mipo raw-fish market are a short walk away.

### Infinity Pool & Wellness

The **outdoor infinity pool** looks straight over Haeundae Beach and is the hotel's signature spot; an indoor pool, spa and fitness centre are also available. Outdoor pool hours vary by season.

## The Verdict: Honest Pros & Cons

### What We Loved
* **Sea Views from Every Angle**: Rooms, pool and lounge all face the ocean.
* **Beach and Blueline Park on Foot**: The best of Haeundae is right outside.
* **Luxury Polish**: Elegant rooms and a refined lounge.

### What to Consider
* **Premium Prices**: Among the most expensive stays in Busan.
* **Formal Service**: Some guests find the service correct but stiff, and check-in can be slow at peak times.
* **East-End Location**: Central Haeundae and the metro are a 10–20 minute walk.
