The Josun story begins in 1914, when the original Chosun Hotel opened on this very site as one of the first Western-style hotels in Korea. More than a century later, The Westin Josun Seoul still stands in Sogong-dong, sharing its grounds with **Hwangudan**, the small octagonal altar pavilion built for the Korean Empire's heaven rituals. From many windows you look down on its tiled roof surrounded by office towers — a perfect snapshot of Seoul.

Today the hotel is part of Marriott's Westin brand and is known for steady, professional service, a strong business clientele and one of the most walkable locations in the city: Myeongdong, City Hall, Deoksugung Palace and Cheonggyecheon Stream are all within a short stroll.

## Location & Getting There

<StayMap id="westin-josun" />

The hotel sits on Sogong-ro, between City Hall Plaza and Myeongdong.

* **Euljiro 1-ga Station (Line 2)**: About 300 m away; **City Hall Station (Lines 1 and 2)** is also a short walk.
* **Myeongdong Street**: About 8 minutes on foot (0.6 km).
* **Deoksugung Palace & City Hall Plaza**: A few minutes' walk.
* **Gyeongbokgung Palace**: About 20 minutes on foot (1.6 km), past Cheonggyecheon and Gwanghwamun Square.
* **Seoul Station (AREX)**: About 10 minutes by taxi, making the airport run easy.

## Rooms, Dining & Facilities

### The Rooms

The 462 rooms are calm and classic, with the Westin Heavenly Bed, neutral tones and practical work spaces. Some rooms look over Hwangudan; others face the downtown towers.

* **Deluxe Rooms**: Comfortable and quiet despite the central location.
* **Hwangudan View Rooms**: Ask for one — the view of the altar pavilion is the hotel's signature.
* **Club Rooms & Suites**: Include lounge access, useful for business travelers.
* **Best For**: First-time visitors, business trips and travelers who like to explore on foot.

### Dining & Drinks

* **Aria**: The hotel's all-day buffet and breakfast restaurant, with Korean and international stations.
* **Specialty restaurants and lounges**: Korean, Japanese and Chinese dining plus a lobby lounge for afternoon tea.
* **Myeongdong**: Street food, noodle houses and cafés a short walk away.

### Wellness & Service

An indoor pool, fitness centre and sauna are available, and the concierge team is one of the most experienced in the city — a real asset for restaurant bookings and day-trip planning.

## The Verdict: Honest Pros & Cons

### What We Loved
* **Walk-Everywhere Location**: Myeongdong, City Hall and palaces without needing a taxi.
* **Heritage and Hwangudan**: Few hotels have a national historic site in the garden.
* **Experienced Service**: Polished front desk and concierge.

### What to Consider
* **Dated in Places**: The building is from 1970 with a 2011 renovation; some areas feel older than newer rivals.
* **Smaller Breakfast Spread**: Some guests find Aria's buffet less extensive than other five-stars.
* **Soft Beds**: The Heavenly Bed is very soft — not everyone's preference.
