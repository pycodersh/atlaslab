Some hotels sell a view; JW Marriott Hotel Seoul sells connections. It sits inside the Central City complex in Banpo, physically linked to **Express Bus Terminal Station**, where three subway lines meet, to the long-distance bus terminals that serve almost every city in Korea, and to **Shinsegae Department Store Gangnam**, one of the busiest department stores in the country.

Opened in 2000 and fully renovated in 2018, the 376-room hotel now feels fresh and contemporary, with warm neutral rooms and one of Seoul's most extensive hotel gyms. If your trip mixes Seoul with a few days in Busan, Gyeongju or Jeonju, few bases make the logistics easier.

## Location & Getting There

<StayMap id="jw-marriott-seoul" />

The hotel is connected underground to **Express Bus Terminal Station (Lines 3, 7 and 9)**, Shinsegae Gangnam and the Famille Station food and dining arcade, so you can shop, eat and catch a train without going outdoors.

* **Line 9 Express**: Fast trains run west along the river to Yeouido and Gimpo Airport.
* **Line 3**: Straight north to Apgujeong, then across the river toward Gyeongbokgung and Anguk (Bukchon).
* **Seoul Express Bus Terminal & Central City Terminal**: Intercity buses to Busan, Gwangju, Jeonju and dozens of other cities depart steps from the lobby.
* **Banpo Hangang Park**: About 20 minutes on foot (1.4 km) to the Moonlight Rainbow Fountain on Banpo Bridge.
* **KTX trains**: Seoul Station and Yongsan Station are about 30 minutes away by subway or taxi.
* **From Incheon Airport**: Airport limousine buses stop at the Central City / Express Bus Terminal area (about 60–80 minutes).

## Rooms, Dining & Facilities

### The Rooms

The 2018 renovation replaced the old heavy décor with soft greys, warm wood and crisp white marble bathrooms, plus plenty of power outlets and good desks.

* **Deluxe Rooms**: The standard category, with a choice of city or Han River–direction views; ask for a high floor.
* **Executive Rooms**: Include access to the executive lounge for breakfast, afternoon snacks and evening drinks — worthwhile if you plan early starts.
* **Suites**: Separate living areas for families or longer stays.
* **Best For**: Multi-city travelers, shoppers, Marriott Bonvoy members and fitness-focused guests.

### Dining & Drinks

* **Flavors**: The hotel's large international buffet, a favourite with Seoul families for weekend lunches.
* **Specialty restaurants**: Japanese and Chinese restaurants and a steakhouse cover more formal dinners.
* **Famille Station & Shinsegae food hall**: Dozens of casual restaurants and a vast gourmet food hall right outside the hotel doors.

### Marquis Fitness Club

The hotel's **Marquis Fitness Club** is one of the most extensive hotel fitness facilities in Seoul, with a large gym, an indoor pool, saunas and a range of studios. If keeping up your training routine matters on the road, this is a strong reason to book.

## The Verdict: Honest Pros & Cons

### What We Loved
* **Exceptional Connectivity**: Three subway lines, national bus terminals and a department store, all under one roof.
* **A Serious Gym**: The Marquis Fitness Club outclasses most hotel gyms in Seoul.
* **Fresh Rooms**: The 2018 renovation means modern bathrooms, good lighting and practical layouts.

### What to Consider
* **Busy Surroundings**: The station and mall are crowded at weekends and rush hour; finding your way underground takes a day or two.
* **Not a Design Statement**: Comfortable and polished, but less distinctive than Park Hyatt or Josun Palace.
* **KTX Isn't Next Door**: The bus terminal is connected, but KTX trains leave from Seoul or Yongsan stations, about 30 minutes away.
