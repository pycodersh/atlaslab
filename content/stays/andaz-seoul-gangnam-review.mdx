Apgujeong is where Gangnam dresses up: flagship fashion houses along Cheongdam, the boutiques and cafés of Garosu-gil, K-pop agency neighbourhoods, and the dermatology and beauty clinics that draw visitors from around the world. Andaz Seoul Gangnam, Hyatt's lifestyle luxury brand, opened here in 2019 and puts all of it within walking distance.

The hotel's 241 rooms take their cues from *jogakbo*, the Korean patchwork wrapping cloth — soft layered colours, natural textures and a relaxed, residential mood. It is polished without being stiff, which suits a neighbourhood built on style.

## Location & Getting There

<StayMap id="andaz-seoul" />

The hotel stands on Nonhyeon-ro at Apgujeong Station.

* **Apgujeong Station (Line 3)**: Exit 3 is right by the hotel entrance — Line 3 runs north across the river toward Gyeongbokgung and Anguk (Bukchon).
* **Hyundai Department Store (Apgujeong main store)**: A few minutes' walk.
* **Garosu-gil**: About 13 minutes on foot (1 km).
* **Galleria Department Store & Cheongdam**: About 17 minutes on foot (1.2 km), with luxury flagships along the way.
* **Han River**: Jamwon and Apgujeong riverside parks are a short walk north.
* **From Incheon Airport**: Airport limousine buses serve the Apgujeong / Sinsa area (about 70–90 minutes).

## Rooms, Dining & Facilities

### The Rooms

Rooms feature warm woods, layered textiles inspired by jogakbo and large bathrooms; Andaz's relaxed approach includes complimentary non-alcoholic minibar items in many rates.

* **Andaz King / Twin Rooms**: Stylish and comfortable entry categories.
* **Larger Rooms & Suites**: More space and better views across Gangnam.
* **Best For**: Shoppers, beauty and wellness travelers, couples and design fans.

{/* IMAGE PLACEHOLDER: Andaz Seoul Gangnam room with jogakbo-inspired textiles */}

### Dining & Drinks

* **Jogakbo**: The hotel's signature restaurant, serving modern Korean dishes with seasonal ingredients.
* **Lobby and bar spaces**: Coffee by day, cocktails by night.
* **Apgujeong & Sinsa**: Some of Seoul's most fashionable restaurants and cafés are minutes away.

### The Summer House & Wellness

**The Summer House** is the hotel's indoor pool and wellness space, a bright, resort-feeling retreat that is especially welcome in winter; guests frequently praise the heated pool. A fitness centre, sauna and spa complete the facilities.

## The Verdict: Honest Pros & Cons

### What We Loved
* **Unbeatable Neighbourhood**: Apgujeong, Garosu-gil and Cheongdam on foot.
* **Station at the Door**: Apgujeong Station Exit 3 is right outside.
* **Relaxed Luxury**: Warm design, a beautiful pool and highly rated breakfasts.

### What to Consider
* **Far from Old Seoul**: Palaces and Bukchon are 30+ minutes away.
* **Premium Pricing**: Rates and dining are at the top of the market.
* **Busy Area**: Nonhyeon-ro traffic is constant; request a higher floor.
