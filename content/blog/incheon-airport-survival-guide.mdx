Incheon International Airport is about an hour from central Seoul, and getting into the city is easy once you know your options. After immigration, sort out your phone data and some cash, then take the AREX train, an airport bus or a taxi depending on where you're staying.

<KeyTakeaways>

- **Two terminals:** T1 for most foreign airlines, T2 for Korean Air and its partners; a free shuttle links them
- **Fastest to Seoul Station:** AREX Express, roughly 45–50 minutes
- **Cheapest:** AREX All-Stop train with a T-money card
- **Avoid:** unlicensed drivers approaching you in the arrivals hall; use the official taxi stands

</KeyTakeaways>

## Terminal 1 or Terminal 2?

| | Terminal 1 (T1) | Terminal 2 (T2) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Airlines** | Most foreign airlines and low-cost carriers | Korean Air, Delta and other SkyTeam partners |
| **Arrivals** | 1st floor | 1st floor |
| **Train station** | Basement level (B1) | Basement level (B1) |
| **Getting between** | Free shuttle bus and the AREX train | Same |

Check your ticket before departure: going to the wrong terminal for your flight home costs 15–20 minutes.

| Task | Where | Tip |

## Getting to Seoul compared

| Option | Time to central Seoul | Rough cost | Best for |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **AREX Express** | About 45–50 min to Seoul Station, non-stop | Around 10,000–11,000 KRW | Hotels near Seoul Station, speed |
| **AREX All-Stop** | About 60 min to Seoul Station, connects to subway lines | Around 4,000–5,000 KRW with T-money | Budget travel, Hongdae and Gimpo areas |
| **Airport limousine bus** | 60–90 min, depending on traffic | Around 16,000–18,000 KRW | Heavy luggage, hotels on a bus route |
| **Taxi** | 60–80 min | Roughly 60,000–100,000 KRW plus tolls | Groups, late arrivals, door to door |

Fares and timetables change, so check the official AREX and airport bus sites before you fly.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Is AREX Express worth it?**
If your hotel is near Seoul Station, yes: it's non-stop with reserved seats. If you need to transfer to other subway lines, the All-Stop train is cheaper and stops at more transfer stations.

**Can I use a foreign credit card on AREX?**
For the Express train, you can buy tickets with a card at machines or counters. For the All-Stop train, a T-money card is the easiest way to pay.

**How early should I get to Incheon for my flight home?**
Allow at least 2.5–3 hours for international flights, more in peak seasons, and add the travel time from Seoul.

**Is there Wi-Fi at the airport?**
Yes, free Wi-Fi covers both terminals, so you can message your hotel or check routes before you sort out data.
