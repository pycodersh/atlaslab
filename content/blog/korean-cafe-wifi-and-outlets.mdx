Korean cafes are made for staying. People work through whole afternoons, and nobody hovers over your table wondering when you'll leave. Two questions get you set up.

## The exchange

**A:** 혹시 콘센트 사용할 수 있는 자리 있나요?  
*(Is there a seat with a power outlet?)*  
**B:** 안쪽에 있어요.  
*(In the back.)*  
**A:** 와이파이 비번 알 수 있을까요?  
*(Could I get the wifi password?)*  
**B:** 영수증에 있어요.  
*(It's on the receipt.)*

**혹시** ("by any chance") softens a request, and you'll hear it at the start of many polite questions.

## Asking for an outlet

| Korean | Romanization | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| 혹시 콘센트 사용할 수 있는 자리 있나요? | hoksi konsenteu sayonghal su inneun jari innayo? | Is there a seat where I can use an outlet? |
| 콘센트 자리 있어요? | konsenteu jari isseoyo? | A seat with an outlet? |
| 충전해도 돼요? | chungjeonhaedo dwaeyo? | Is it okay to charge my phone? |

**콘센트** is the only Korean word for a power outlet. Outlet seats are usually along the walls.

## Asking for the wifi password

**와이파이 비번 알 수 있을까요?** (*waipai bibeon al su isseulkkayo?*), "Could I get the wifi password?"

**비번** is the everyday short form of **비밀번호** (password). Both work; 비번 is what people actually say. Before you ask, check the counter, the wall, and the bottom of your receipt, where most cafes print it.

## What staff will tell you

| You'll hear | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 안쪽에 있어요. | They're in the back. |
| 창가 쪽에 있어요. | They're by the window. |
| 지금은 자리가 없어요. | No seats right now. |
| 영수증에 있어요. | It's on the receipt. |
| 저기 적혀 있어요. | It's written over there. |
| 대문자예요. | It's uppercase. |

Listen for **대문자** (uppercase) and **소문자** (lowercase): getting the capitals wrong is the most common reason the wifi won't connect.

## Polite or casual?

| Polite (staff) | Casual (friends) | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| 콘센트 자리 있어요? | 콘센트 자리 있어? | Is there an outlet seat? |
| 와이파이 비번 알 수 있을까요? | 와이파이 비번 뭐야? | What's the wifi password? |

<Callout type="tip" title="How long can you stay?">

Usually as long as you like. Three or four hours with a laptop is normal. Some busy chains post a limit at peak times, often **2시간** (two hours). If you're staying a long time, ordering a second drink is a kind gesture, not a rule.

</Callout>

## What to actually say

- Need power: **콘센트 자리 있어요?**
- Need wifi: **와이파이 비번 알 수 있을까요?**
- Charging: **충전해도 돼요?**
