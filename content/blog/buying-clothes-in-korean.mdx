Clothes shopping in Korea involves more conversation than in many countries. Staff greet you, follow up, and often bring things over. Four or five phrases make that easy instead of awkward.

## What staff will say

| You'll hear | Meaning | Answer |
|---|---|---|
| 뭐 찾으세요? | What are you looking for? | 구경할게요. |
| 사이즈 어떻게 되세요? | What size are you? | 66이요. / M이요. |
| 입어 보세요. | Try it on. | 네. |
| 잘 어울리세요. | It suits you. | 감사합니다. |

**구경할게요** (*gugyeonghalgeyo*, "I'm just looking") ends the conversation politely. If you do want help, say what you're after: **원피스 찾고 있어요** (*wonpiseu chatgo isseoyo*, "I'm looking for a dress").

**어울리다** is the verb for something suiting a person, and **잘 어울리세요** is the compliment you'll hear most.

## Sizes

| Korean | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 프리 사이즈 | one size (usually a Korean S to M) |
| 55 / 66 / 77 | women's sizes, roughly S / M / L |
| S / M / L | also common |

| Korean | Romanization | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| 다른 사이즈 있어요? | dareun saijeu isseoyo? | Do you have another size? |
| 더 큰 거 있어요? | deo keun geo isseoyo? | Do you have a bigger one? |
| 더 작은 거 있어요? | deo jageun geo isseoyo? | Do you have a smaller one? |

Korean sizes tend to run small, so it's worth trying on when you can.

## Trying things on

| Korean | Romanization | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| 입어 봐도 돼요? | ibeo bwado dwaeyo? | Can I try it on? |
| 탈의실 어디예요? | taruisil eodiyeyo? | Where's the fitting room? |

Some small shops and [market stalls](/blog/en/k-patto/shopping-at-a-korean-traditional-market) don't allow trying on, especially for tops. If the answer is no, it's the shop's rule, not about you.

## Colors

| Korean | English |
|---|---|
| 검정 | black |
| 흰색 | white |
| 회색 | gray |
| 남색 | navy |
| 베이지 | beige |

**다른 색 있어요?** (*dareun saek isseoyo?*) asks for another color.

## Buying, or leaving politely

| Korean | Romanization | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| 이걸로 할게요. | igeollo halgeyo | I'll take this one. |
| 카드로 할게요. | kadeuro halgeyo | By card. |
| 좀 더 생각해 볼게요. | jom deo saenggakae bolgeyo | I'll think about it. |

**좀 더 생각해 볼게요** is the polite exit. It's understood as no, and nobody pushes.

## Returns

Return rules vary by shop, and small shops and sale items often don't take returns at all. Ask before you pay if it matters: **교환 돼요?** (*gyohwan dwaeyo?*, "can I exchange it?") or **환불 돼요?** (*hwanbul dwaeyo?*, "can I get a refund?").

## Polite or casual?

| Polite (staff) | Casual (friends) | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| 이거 어때요? | 이거 어때? | How's this? |
| 잘 어울려요. | 잘 어울려. | It suits you. |
| 다른 색 있어요? | 다른 색 없나? | Is there another color? |

## What to actually say

- Browsing: **구경할게요.**
- Size: **더 큰 거 있어요?**
- Trying on: **입어 봐도 돼요?**
- Leaving: **좀 더 생각해 볼게요.**
