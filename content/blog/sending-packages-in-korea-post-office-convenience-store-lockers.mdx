You can send parcels in Korea from a post office (우체국) or from a convenience store such as GS25 or CU, and domestic parcels usually arrive in one to two days. Use the post office for international shipments and heavy boxes, and a convenience store for cheap, 24-hour domestic drop-off.

<KeyTakeaways>

- **International:** go to a post office; staff help with customs forms and EMS
- **Domestic, any hour:** convenience store parcel kiosks work 24/7
- **Cheapest:** store-to-store services (GS25 반값택배, CU 알뜰택배) from roughly 2,000 KRW
- **Avoid:** bringing an unboxed item; couriers only accept sealed, rectangular boxes

</KeyTakeaways>

## Post office vs convenience store

| | Post office (우체국) | Convenience store (GS25 · CU) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Best for** | International mail, EMS, heavy or large boxes | Small to medium domestic parcels |
| **Hours** | Weekdays, roughly 09:00–18:00 | 24 hours, every day |
| **Boxes and tape** | Boxes for sale (sizes 1–5); tape and bubble wrap at the packing table | Bring your parcel already packed and sealed |
| **How you drop off** | At the counter with a clerk | Self-service kiosk with a built-in scale, then pay at the register |
| **Domestic cost** | Roughly 4,000–10,000 KRW by size | Roughly 3,500–9,000 KRW door to door |
| **Cheapest option** | — | Store-to-store pickup from roughly 2,000 KRW (slower; the recipient collects at a store) |
| **International** | Yes: EMS, airmail, surface mail | Limited, and the screens are mostly in Korean |
| **English help** | Clerks at larger branches often manage basic English | Usually none; the kiosk does the work |

Prices change with size, weight and destination, so treat these as ballpark figures and check the screen or counter.

## Using a convenience store parcel kiosk

The kiosk sits near the entrance or the register, with a scale built in or next to it. Some machines have a language button (look for a globe icon or "English" in a corner of the start screen), but many are Korean-only. The steps are the same either way.

| Step | What to do | Tip |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. Language** | Tap the globe or "English" button if there is one | No button? Use your phone's camera translator on the screen |
| **2. Start** | Choose 택배 접수 (parcel drop-off), then 비회원 (non-member) | Non-member works without a Korean app account |
| **3. Service** | Pick 일반택배 (door to door) or 반값 · 알뜰택배 (store to store) | Store to store is cheaper but slower |
| **4. Weigh** | Place the box flat on the scale, nothing hanging over the edge | Stay under the size and weight limits shown on screen |
| **5. Addresses** | Enter sender and recipient names, phone numbers and addresses | A Korean mobile number is usually required for tracking texts |
| **6. Label and pay** | Print the barcode label, stick it on the box, pay at the register | Keep the receipt; it has your tracking number |

<Callout type="tip" title="Weighing and sizing">
Add up length, width and height. Store parcels usually need a total under about 120–160 cm and a weight under about 20 kg, depending on the service. Over that, go to a post office.
</Callout>

Batteries, liquids, aerosols and fresh food are restricted. Declare electronics with lithium batteries at the counter, and leave loose power banks out of international parcels.

<DoDont>
<Do>

- Use a sturdy, rectangular, fully taped box
- Write the 5-digit postal code and road-name address
- Pre-fill EMS details in the Korea Post app to save time at the counter
- Keep the tracking slip (운송장) until the parcel arrives

</Do>
<Dont>

- Tape two boxes together or ship in plastic bags
- Send liquids, sprays or loose batteries
- Expect an English kiosk at every convenience store
- Leave the phone field blank; drivers text before delivery

</Dont>
</DoDont>

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Can I send an international package from a convenience store?**
Some GS25 and CU branches offer it, but the steps and customs details are mostly in Korean. A post office is easier: staff can help with the customs form.

**What if I don't have a Korean phone number?**
Ask your hotel or host whether you can use their number, with their permission. Otherwise track the parcel manually online using the number on your receipt.

**Do I need my own box at the post office?**
No. Post offices sell boxes in several sizes for roughly 500–3,000 KRW, and tape, scissors and bubble wrap are free at the packing table.

**How fast is domestic delivery?**
Parcels dropped off before the daily cutoff usually arrive the next business day. Store-to-store services take a few days longer.
