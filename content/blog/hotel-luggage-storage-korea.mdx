You reach the hotel at 10 a.m. Check-in isn't until 3 p.m. You have five hours and a heavy bag. One question solves it.

## The exchange

**A:** 혹시 체크인 전에 짐 먼저 맡길 수 있을까요?  
*(Could I leave my luggage before check-in?)*  
**B:** 네, 물론이죠! 짐 번호표 드릴게요.  
*(Of course! Here's your luggage tag.)*  
**A:** 감사합니다. 몇 시부터 방에 들어갈 수 있나요?  
*(Thank you. What time can we enter the room?)*  
**B:** 3시부터 입실 가능하십니다!  
*(Check-in is available from 3 PM!)*

**맡기다** (*matgida*) means leaving something in someone's care: luggage, a coat, dry cleaning. **번호표** (*beonhopyo*) is the numbered tag; keep it, because you'll need it to get your bag back. **입실** is the formal word for entering your room, and it's on hotel signs next to the check-in time.

## The pattern

**혹시 ~ 수 있을까요?** ("could I possibly ~?") is one of the most useful polite requests in Korean.

| Korean | Romanization | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| 혹시 짐 맡길 수 있을까요? | hoksi jim matgil su isseulkkayo? | Could I leave my luggage? |
| 혹시 일찍 체크인할 수 있을까요? | hoksi iljjik chekeuinhal su isseulkkayo? | Could I check in early? |
| 혹시 늦게 체크아웃할 수 있을까요? | hoksi neutge chekeuautal su isseulkkayo? | Could I check out late? |

## What staff will say

| You'll hear | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 번호표 드릴게요. | Here's your tag. |
| 3시부터 입실 가능하세요. | You can go in from 3. |
| 방 준비되면 연락드릴게요. | We'll contact you when the room is ready. |
| 귀중품은 따로 챙겨 주세요. | Please keep valuables with you. |

## Early check-in

Check-in is usually mid-afternoon and checkout late morning, but it varies by hotel. **일찍 체크인 가능한가요?** asks about early check-in: some hotels allow it free if the room is ready, and some charge. It never hurts to ask.

## Picking up your bag

Hand over the tag and say **짐 찾으러 왔어요** (*jim chajeureo wasseoyo*, "I'm here for my luggage"). Most hotels will also hold bags after checkout if your flight is later. [Late checkout](/blog/en/k-patto/hotel-late-checkout-korea) covers that request.

## Polite or casual?

**혹시** and **-ㄹ 수 있을까요?** make this request polite. With a friend, it's simply **짐 맡기고 가자** ("let's leave the bags and go").

## What to actually say

- Dropping off: **혹시 짐 맡길 수 있을까요?**
- Room time: **몇 시부터 입실 가능해요?**
- Collecting: **짐 찾으러 왔어요.**
