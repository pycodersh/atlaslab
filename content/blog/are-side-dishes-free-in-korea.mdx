Before you've ordered anything, six small plates land on your table: kimchi, bean sprouts, pickled radish, seasoned spinach. They aren't on the menu, and they aren't on the bill.

## The exchange

**A:** 저기요, 김치 더 주세요.  
*(Excuse me, more kimchi, please.)*  
**B:** 네, 잠시만요.  
*(Sure, one moment.)*  
**A:** 물도 주세요.  
*(Some water too, please.)*

These are **반찬** (*banchan*), and they come with the meal. Refills are free too, and asking for more is completely normal. A bowl of rice, a soup and several banchan is simply what a proper Korean meal looks like.

## Asking for more

| Korean | Romanization | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| 더 주세요. | deo juseyo | More, please. |
| 반찬 좀 더 주세요. | banchan jom deo juseyo | More side dishes, please. |
| 김치 더 주세요. | gimchi deo juseyo | More kimchi, please. |
| 이거 더 주세요. | igeo deo juseyo | More of this, please. |
| 물도 주세요. | muldo juseyo | Water too, please. |

**이거** ("this") lets you point at any empty dish. Adding **좀** ("a little") softens the request. You'll hear it constantly.

## What you'll hear back

| You'll hear | Romanization | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| 네, 잠시만요. | ne, jamsimanyo | Sure, one moment. |
| 반찬은 셀프예요. | banchaneun selpeuyeyo | Side dishes are self-serve. |
| 저쪽에 있어요. | jeojjoge isseoyo | They're over there. |
| 이건 추가 요금이 있어요. | igeon chuga yogeumi isseoyo | This one costs extra. |

**셀프** means self-serve. If you see a **셀프바** near the counter, with banchan, water and sometimes soup, get up and help yourself. To check, ask **셀프예요?**

Everything here is polite **-요** speech. With friends you'd drop it: **김치 더 달라고 하자** ("let's ask for more kimchi").

## Where free stops

- **Premium sides:** a few dishes, such as steamed egg or a seafood item, may be priced on the menu.
- **Expensive items:** a large piece of grilled fish or raw crab served as a side usually isn't refilled for free.
- **All-you-can-eat (무한리필):** the restaurant's own posted rules apply.

In an ordinary restaurant, kimchi, sprouts, radish and greens are always free.

<Callout type="tip" title="Ask for what you'll eat">

Food waste is taken seriously, so refill what you're actually eating rather than everything. Nobody will comment if you ask for kimchi three times and never touch the spinach.

</Callout>

## Water

Water is free and often self-serve, from a dispenser or a fridge with pitchers and metal cups. If nobody brings it, that's why. Restaurants default to cold water; for warm, ask **따뜻한 물 주세요** (*ttatteutan mul juseyo*).

## What to actually say

- Calling first: **저기요!**
- Refill: **이거 더 주세요.**
- Self-serve?: **셀프예요?**
