Ask a Korean where to spend a warm evening and many will say **한강**, the Han River. Not a bar or a restaurant: a lawn by the water, convenience store food, and a mat on the grass. Almost nothing needs arranging in advance.

## How it works

Pick a park (**여의도**, **반포** and **뚝섬** are popular), get food from a convenience store or a delivery app, sit on the grass and stay until it gets cold. The [Han River parks guide](/blog/en/k-patto/han-river-parks-seoul-guide) compares the parks.

## A mat and a spot

**돗자리** (*dotjari*) is a picnic mat. Convenience stores in the parks sell them, and some areas rent them.

| Korean | Romanization | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| 돗자리 있어요? | dotjari isseoyo? | Do you have mats? |
| 돗자리 어디서 빌려요? | dotjari eodiseo billyeoyo? | Where can I rent a mat? |
| 여기 앉아도 돼요? | yeogi anjado dwaeyo? | Can we sit here? |

## Delivery to a lawn

Delivery apps let you choose a Han River delivery zone (**배달존**) as your address, and the rider meets you at a marked pickup point. Fried chicken is the classic, and **치맥** (*chimaek*), chicken plus beer, is the word you'll hear all evening. [Ordering food delivery](/blog/en/k-patto/ordering-food-delivery-in-korea) covers the app vocabulary.

## Ramyeon by the river

Convenience stores in the parks have instant-noodle cookers: buy a packet of ramyeon in a paper bowl, put it under the machine, and it adds hot water and cooks it for you. Staff will point if you ask **이거 어떻게 해요?** ("how does this work?").

## What you'll hear from friends

| You'll hear | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 자리 잡았어? | Did you get us a spot? |
| 치킨 시킬까? | Shall we order chicken? |
| 배달 왔대! | The delivery's here! |
| 야경 진짜 예쁘다. | The night view is so pretty. |
| 좀 춥다, 갈까? | It's getting cold, shall we go? |

## Polite or casual?

A picnic is friends, so it's casual speech (반말). The [politeness levels guide](/blog/en/k-patto/korean-politeness-levels-explained) explains when to switch.

| Casual (friends) | Polite | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| 여기 앉자. | 여기 앉아요. | Let's sit here. |
| 다 같이 있어서 좋다. | 다 같이 있어서 좋아요. | It's nice being all together. |
| 좀 추워. | 좀 추워요. | It's a bit cold. |

## What to bring

| Korean | What it is |
|---|---|
| 돗자리 | picnic mat |
| 담요 | blanket |
| 물티슈 | wet wipes |
| 쓰레기봉투 | trash bag |

<Callout type="info" title="Cleaning up">

Bins are placed around the parks, often with separate sections for recycling and food waste. Leaving trash on the grass is noticed. **쓰레기통 어디 있어요?** asks where the bin is.

</Callout>

## What to actually say

- Finding a spot: **여기 앉자!**
- Ordering: **치킨 시킬까?**
- The view: **야경 진짜 예쁘다.**
