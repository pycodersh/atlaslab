Jeju's east coast has the island's most famous landform and one of its prettiest beaches. Climb Seongsan Ilchulbong, a crater that rises straight out of the sea, then head back west along the coast to Hamdeok, where shallow turquoise water meets white sand and a grassy hill.

<KeyTakeaways title="At a glance">

- **The route:** Seongsan Ilchulbong → Hamdeok Beach, about 32 km by road
- **Getting there:** about an hour's drive from Jeju City to Seongsan; buses run along the coast but take longer
- **Seongsan hours:** from 6:00 am to 6:00 pm (last tickets 5:00 pm) from November to February, longer from March to October
- **Closed:** Seongsan Ilchulbong closes on the first Monday of each month
- **Avoid:** climbing in the middle of the day in summer; go early or late

</KeyTakeaways>

## Seongsan Ilchulbong

Seongsan Ilchulbong, "Sunrise Peak," is a tuff cone formed by an underwater eruption on Jeju's eastern tip. A stairway climbs the side of the crater to the rim, where the bowl opens up below you and the sea and Udo island spread out beyond. It's part of Jeju's UNESCO World Natural Heritage sites.

| | |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Hours** | November to February 6:00 am to 6:00 pm; March, April, September and October 5:00 am to 7:00 pm; May to August 4:30 am to 8:00 pm. Ticket sales end an hour before closing |
| **Closed** | First Monday of each month (the next day if that Monday is a public holiday) |
| **Fee** | A small fee for the trail to the summit; the lower coastal path is free |

The climb is all stairs and steep in places, so allow about an hour up and down at an easy pace. Opening hours start before dawn because people come for the sunrise. Food and drinks aren't allowed on the trail. Ferries to Udo leave from Seongsan port nearby if you want to add the island.

Back toward Jeju City, about 20 km east of the airport, Hamdeok Beach is a wide stretch of pale sand with shallow, clear water that shades from green to deep blue. Next to it rises **Seoubong**, a grassy hill with a short walking trail and views back over the bay.

Hamdeok is an easy last stop on the way back to the city or the airport, with cafes and restaurants along the waterfront. The water is shallow a long way out, which makes it popular with families in summer.

For dinner back in town, try Jeju's famous black pork. Our [Jeju black pork guide](/blog/en/k-pantry/jeju-black-pork-vs-samgyeopsal) explains whether it's worth the price.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**When is Seongsan Ilchulbong closed?**
On the first Monday of each month, or the next day if that Monday is a public holiday.

**Can I watch the sunrise from Seongsan Ilchulbong?**
Yes. The trail opens before dawn, at 6:00 am in winter and as early as 4:30 am in summer, so you can climb for sunrise.

**How long does the climb take?**
Allow about an hour up and back down, including time at the top. The trail is stairs the whole way.

**Is Hamdeok Beach good for swimming?**
In summer, yes. The water is shallow and clear, and swimming is allowed in the marked area during the official beach season.
