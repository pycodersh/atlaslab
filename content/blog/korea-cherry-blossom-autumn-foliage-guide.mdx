Korea's two most beautiful seasons are cherry blossom season in early spring and autumn foliage season in October and November. Blossoms and leaves peak at different times in each region, so check the forecast a few weeks ahead and plan to be flexible.

<KeyTakeaways>

- **Cherry blossoms:** roughly late March in the south to early April in Seoul; peak lasts about a week
- **Autumn foliage:** mountains in the north turn first in October; Seoul peaks around late October to mid-November
- **Forecasts:** Korean weather services publish bloom and foliage predictions each year
- **Avoid:** weekend afternoons at famous spots; go early on a weekday

</KeyTakeaways>

## When to go

| Season | Where it starts | Seoul peak (approx.) | How long it lasts |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Cherry blossom** | Jeju and the south coast (late March) | Early April | About a week of full bloom |
| **Autumn foliage** | Northern mountains (early October) | Late October to mid-November | Two to three weeks across regions |

Dates move a little each year with the weather, so treat these as rough guides and check the latest forecast.

## Best spots

| Spot | Season | Why go |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Yeouido (Seoul)** | Blossom | Long avenue of cherry trees, spring festival |
| **Seokchon Lake (Seoul)** | Blossom | Trees around a lake beside Lotte World Tower |
| **Jinhae (Changwon)** | Blossom | Korea's biggest cherry blossom festival |
| **Gyeongju** | Blossom | Blossoms around ancient tombs and lakes |
| **Seoraksan** | Foliage | Dramatic mountain colors, early in the season |
| **Naejangsan** | Foliage | One of Korea's most famous maple valleys |
| **Nami Island** | Foliage | Ginkgo and metasequoia lanes; see our [Nami guide](/blog/en/k-patto/nami-island-day-trip-seoul-guide) |
| **Seoul palaces** | Both | Gyeongbokgung and Changdeokgung with seasonal color |

<Callout type="tip" title="Beat the crowds">
Arrive just after sunrise, visit on weekdays, and try lesser-known parks near your hotel. Famous spots on peak weekends can be shoulder to shoulder.
</Callout>

## Seasonal festivals

| Festival | When (approx.) | Where |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Jinhae Gunhangje** | Late March to early April | Changwon, South Gyeongsang |
| **Yeouido Spring Flower Festival** | Early April | Seoul |
| **Gyeongju cherry blossom events** | Early April | Gyeongju |
| **Seoraksan foliage season** | Early to mid-October | Gangwon |
| **Naejangsan foliage season** | Late October to early November | North Jeolla |

Festival dates shift with the forecast, so confirm them each year before booking. Most festivals are free, with food stalls and night lighting.

## Planning tips

| Tip | Why |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Check forecasts** | Bloom and foliage predictions are updated through the season |
| **Stay flexible** | Rain or wind can end blossom season early |
| **Book early** | Hotels near festivals fill quickly |
| **Use transit** | Roads near festivals are jammed; take the subway or train |
| **Layer up** | Spring and autumn mornings are chilly |

## Frequently Asked Questions

**When is cherry blossom season in Seoul?**
Usually early April, though the exact week changes each year. The south blooms earlier, from late March.

**When do autumn leaves peak in Seoul?**
Around late October to mid-November. Mountains further north and higher up change color earlier.

**Where is the best cherry blossom festival?**
Jinhae's festival near Changwon is the largest, while Yeouido and Seokchon Lake are the most popular in Seoul.

**Do I need to book anything?**
Festivals are free to enter, but book hotels and trains early if you're traveling to a famous festival town.
