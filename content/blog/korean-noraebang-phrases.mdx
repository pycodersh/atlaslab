**노래방** (*noraebang*) isn't karaoke in a bar. It's a private room you rent, with a song machine, two microphones and a tambourine that someone will hand you within minutes. The Korean involved is short, and most of it happens in the first thirty seconds at the counter.

## Two kinds of noraebang

**일반 노래방** is the regular kind: you rent a room by the hour and split the cost across the group. Prices vary by area and time of night.

**코인 노래방** is the coin kind: small booths, no staff, and you pay the machine for a few songs at a time. It's the low-pressure option for one or two people. [Adding more time at a coin noraebang](/blog/en/k-patto/adding-time-coin-norebang-korea) covers how it works.

## What staff will ask at the counter

| You'll hear | Meaning | Answer |
|---|---|---|
| 몇 분이세요? | How many people? | 두 명이요. |
| 몇 시간 하실 거예요? | How many hours? | 한 시간이요. |
| 3번 방으로 가세요. | Go to room 3. | 네. |
| 음료는 셀프예요. | Drinks are self-serve. | 네. |

That's the whole booking. You get a room number and go.

## 서비스: the free extra time

When your time runs low, the counter on screen sometimes jumps up by itself. That's **서비스**: free extra time the owner adds. You can also ask **서비스 좀 주세요** (*seobiseu jom juseyo*). It's a normal request and often works on a quiet night. In Korean, 서비스 means something given free, not customer service; the [Konglish guide](/blog/en/k-patto/konglish-words-when-english-becomes-korean) has more words like it.

## The remote

| Button | Romanization | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| 예약 | yeyak | queue a song |
| 취소 | chwiso | cancel |
| 간주점프 | ganjujeompeu | skip the instrumental break |
| 시작 | sijak | start |

To find a song, search by number or by title. Artist searches often need the Korean name: [how to search for songs](/blog/en/k-patto/how-to-search-songs-korean-norebang) lists the big K-pop names.

## Inside the room

| Korean | Romanization | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| 이 노래 같이 불러요. | i norae gachi bulleoyo | Let's sing this one together. |
| 신청곡 있어요? | sincheonggok isseoyo? | Any requests? |
| 저 노래 잘 못해요. | jeo norae jal motaeyo | I'm not a good singer. |
| 한 곡만 더 해요. | han gongman deo haeyo | Just one more song. |

**한 곡만 더** ends every session, usually several times before anyone actually leaves.

## Polite or casual?

| Polite (staff, new people) | Casual (friends) | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| 같이 불러요. | 같이 부르자. | Let's sing together. |
| 한 곡만 더 해요. | 한 곡만 더! | One more song! |
| 이 노래 알아요? | 이 노래 알아? | Do you know this song? |

Singing in Korean, even badly, wins more goodwill than singing well in English.
