For day trips from Seoul, the KTX is faster and more predictable, while express buses are cheaper and reach towns without a train station. Choose the train for long distances and tight schedules, and the bus for budget trips and places like small coastal towns.

<KeyTakeaways>

- **KTX:** fastest for long distances, unaffected by traffic
- **Express bus:** cheaper, frequent, reaches more towns
- **Premium buses (우등 · 프리미엄):** wide, reclining seats close to train comfort
- **Avoid:** taking a bus on holiday weekends; highway traffic can double the journey

</KeyTakeaways>

## Train vs bus compared

| | KTX / SRT | Express bus |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Speed** | Very fast on high-speed lines | Depends on traffic |
| **Price** | Higher | Usually much cheaper |
| **Comfort** | Spacious, can walk around, restrooms | Premium buses have wide reclining seats; restrooms only at rest stops |
| **Frequency** | Frequent on main lines | Very frequent on popular routes |
| **Reach** | Major cities | Many smaller towns too |
| **Booking** | Korail or SRT app and website | Bus apps, websites or the terminal |
| **Seoul hubs** | Seoul, Yongsan, Suseo stations | Express Bus Terminal, Dong Seoul, Nambu |

## Popular day trips

| Destination | Better choice | Why |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Busan** | KTX | Long distance; too far for a comfortable bus day trip |
| **Gyeongju** | KTX | Fast to Singyeongju, then local bus |
| **Jeonju** | Either | KTX to Jeonju, or a direct bus to the city center |
| **Gangneung** | KTX | The KTX line makes it a quick trip to the east coast |
| **Sokcho** | Bus | No high-speed rail; buses are direct |
| **Andong** | Either | KTX-Eum or a direct bus |

<Callout type="tip" title="Bus classes">
Express buses come in general (일반), deluxe (우등, three seats per row) and premium (프리미엄) classes. Deluxe is a small step up in price and much more comfortable for trips over two hours.
</Callout>

## A sample day trip timeline

A day trip works best when you leave early and book the return in advance. Here's how a train day to Gyeongju might look.

| Time | Plan |
| :--- | :--- |
| **07:30** | KTX from Seoul Station to Singyeongju |
| **10:00** | Local bus or taxi into Gyeongju's historic center |
| **10:30–17:00** | Bulguksa Temple, Daereungwon tombs, Hwangnidan-gil lunch |
| **18:00** | Back to Singyeongju Station |
| **18:30** | KTX back to Seoul, arriving in the evening |

## Booking each

| | How to book | Tip |

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Is the KTX worth the extra cost?**
For long distances like Busan or Gyeongju, yes: it saves hours and isn't affected by traffic. For shorter trips, buses offer better value.

**Can I book express buses in English?**
Some bus booking apps and websites have English options, and terminal ticket machines often have an English mode.

**Do buses stop on the way?**
Longer express routes stop at a highway rest area for about 15 minutes, which is the restroom and snack break.

**Which Seoul bus terminal should I use?**
The Express Bus Terminal serves many southern and western routes, Dong Seoul serves eastern routes like Gangwon, and Nambu serves some southern routes. Check your ticket.
