You're standing in front of two products that look almost identical: same size, similar price, different name. You could guess, or you could ask. Staff at Korean beauty stores expect the question and usually have an opinion.

## The exchange

**A:** 이거 두 개 중에 뭐가 더 촉촉해요?  
*(Between these two, which one is more moisturizing?)*  
**B:** 건조하시면 이 파란색 제품 추천드려요.  
*(If your skin is dry, I recommend this blue one.)*  
**A:** 테스터 써봐도 괜찮아요?  
*(Can I try the tester?)*  
**B:** 네, 손등에 편하게 테스트해 보세요.  
*(Yes, feel free to test it on the back of your hand.)*

**촉촉하다** (*chokchokada*) is the dewy, hydrated feeling that's on every package and in every review. **손등** (*sondeung*), the back of the hand, is where you test skincare and foundation.

## Words to know

| Korean | Romanization | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| 촉촉해요 | chokchokaeyo | moisturizing, dewy |
| 건조해요 | geonjohaeyo | dry |
| 추천 | chucheon | recommendation |
| 테스터 | teseuteo | tester |
| 손등 | sondeung | back of the hand |
| 제품 | jepum | product |

## Describing your skin

The more specific you are, the better the recommendation.

| Korean | Romanization | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| 피부가 건조해요. | pibuga geonjohaeyo | My skin is dry. |
| 지성 피부예요. | jiseong pibuyeyo | I have oily skin. |
| 민감한 편이에요. | mingamhan pyeonieyo | My skin is on the sensitive side. |
| 트러블이 자주 나요. | teureobeuri jaju nayo | I break out often. |
| 모공이 신경 쓰여요. | mogongi singyeong sseuyeoyo | I'm bothered by my pores. |

Then add **추천해 주실 수 있어요?** (*chucheonhae jusil su isseoyo?*, "could you recommend something?").

## Comparing two products

The pattern is **두 개 중에 뭐가 더 ~해요?** ("of the two, which is more ~?").

| What you want | How to ask |
|---|---|
| More moisturizing | 뭐가 더 촉촉해요? |
| Lighter texture | 뭐가 더 가벼워요? |
| Better for sensitive skin | 민감한 피부에 뭐가 더 좋아요? |
| More popular | 뭐가 더 인기 있어요? |
| Better value | 뭐가 더 가성비 좋아요? |

**가성비** (*gaseongbi*) means value for money, and it's useful in any Korean shop.

## What staff will say

| You'll hear | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 피부 타입이 어떻게 되세요? | What's your skin type? |
| 이 제품 제일 많이 나가요. | This one sells the most. |
| 손등에 테스트해 보세요. | Try it on the back of your hand. |
| 톤 맞춰 드릴까요? | Shall I help match your shade? |

For foundation and cushion, staff will often help find your shade. **제 톤에 맞는 거 추천해 주세요** asks for one that matches your skin tone.

## Polite or casual?

| Polite (staff) | Casual (friends) | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| 뭐가 더 촉촉해요? | 뭐가 더 촉촉해? | Which is more moisturizing? |
| 테스터 써 봐도 괜찮아요? | 이거 발라 봐도 돼? | Can I try this? |
| 이걸로 할게요. | 이걸로 할래. | I'll take this one. |

## What to actually say

- Comparing: **두 개 중에 뭐가 더 촉촉해요?**
- Your skin: **피부가 건조해요.**
- Testing: **테스터 써 봐도 괜찮아요?**
- Deciding: **이걸로 할게요.**
