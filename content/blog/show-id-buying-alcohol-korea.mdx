You put two cans of beer on the counter. The staff scans one, then stops and says something before going any further. Nothing is wrong: they need to see your ID.

## The exchange

**A:** 주류는 구입할 때 신분증 확인이 필요합니다.  
*(We need to check ID for alcohol purchases.)*  
**A:** 신분증이나 여권 보여주세요.  
*(Please show me your ID or passport.)*  
**B:** 아 네, 여기요.  
*(Oh sure, here you go.)*

**주류** (*juryu*) is the formal word for alcohol, the one on signs and shelf labels. In conversation people say **술**.

## What staff will say

| You'll hear | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 신분증 보여 주세요. | Please show me your ID. |
| 신분증 확인할게요. | I'll check your ID. |
| 여권 있으세요? | Do you have a passport? |
| 미성년자에게는 판매하지 않습니다. | We don't sell to minors. |

The first line in the exchange ends in **-습니다**, the formal style used for rules and announcements. Staff switch back to polite **-요** for the rest.

## Answering

| Korean | Romanization | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| 아 네, 여기요. | a ne, yeogiyo | Oh sure, here you go. |
| 여권이요. | yeogwoniyo | It's my passport. |
| 잠시만요. | jamsimanyo | One moment. |
| 두고 왔어요. | dugo wasseoyo | I left it behind. |

If you don't have it, they can't sell to you. The only fix is to go and get it.

## What counts as ID

Bring your **여권** (*yeogwon*, passport): it's the document that works everywhere. If you live in Korea, your **외국인등록증** (*oegugin deungnokjeung*, residence card) works too.

A photo of your passport on your phone usually isn't accepted, and staff often can't check a foreign driver's license. Carry the physical passport if you plan to buy alcohol.

## They're not suspicious of you

Selling to a minor can bring a penalty for the store, so many staff check anyone who might be young. Korea's drinking age is 19, counted by birth year: you can buy from January 1 of the year you turn 19. If you're clearly an adult, it's just routine.

<Callout type="info" title="No closing time">

Korean convenience stores sell alcohol whenever they're open, and most are open 24 hours. There's no late-night cutoff and no separate liquor store. Drinking at the store's own tables is a different matter, and some branches don't allow it.

</Callout>

## Polite or casual?

You only need polite speech here: **여기요**, **잠시만요**. With friends, you'd say **신분증 챙겼어?** ("did you bring your ID?").

## What to actually say

- Handing it over: **아 네, 여기요.**
- Looking for it: **잠시만요.**
- Don't have it: **두고 왔어요.**
