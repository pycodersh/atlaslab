You're pulling up to your stop. The driver says something about arriving, and you're not sure whether you need to do anything before you get out. It depends on how you booked.

## If you used Kakao T

**A:** 앱으로 자동결제 맞죠?  
*(It's auto-payment on the app, right?)*  
**B:** 네, 결제되셨어요. 영수증은 앱에서 확인하시면 됩니다.  
*(Yes, all paid. You can check your receipt in the app.)*

<YouTube id="W0QewjynAK0" />

With auto-payment (**자동결제**, *jadonggyeolje*), the fare is charged to the card in the app when the ride ends. There's nothing to tap. **맞죠?** ("…right?") is the easy way to check something you think is true, and once you hear **결제되셨어요** ("it's been paid") you can get out. The receipt is in the app's ride history.

## What the driver will say

| You'll hear | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 다 왔습니다. | We're here. |
| 결제되셨어요. | It's paid. |
| 카드 여기 대 주세요. | Tap your card here. |
| 영수증 필요하세요? | Do you need a receipt? |

## If you hailed on the street

There's no app and no auto-payment. The fare shows on the meter between the front seats.

| Korean | Romanization | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| 카드로 할게요. | kadeuro halgeyo | I'll pay by card. |
| 현금으로 할게요. | hyeongeumeuro halgeyo | I'll pay in cash. |
| 영수증 주세요. | yeongsujeung juseyo | A receipt, please. |
| 괜찮아요. | gwaenchanayo | No thanks (to the receipt). |
| 잔돈 | jandon | change |

The card reader is usually on the center console or the back of the front seat. Tap or insert the card yourself; the driver doesn't take it from you. Most taxis also take T-money transit cards. Cash works, but drivers may not have change for a large bill. There's no tipping.

## Getting out

**감사합니다. 안녕히 계세요!** (*gamsahamnida. annyeonghi gyeseyo*) means "thank you, goodbye". **안녕히 계세요** is the goodbye you say when you're leaving and the other person is staying. It lands much better than stepping out in silence.

The doors aren't automatic. Open the door and close it behind you.

## Polite or casual?

Drivers often use formal **-습니다** (**다 왔습니다**) mixed with polite **-요**. You only need **-요**: **카드로 할게요**, **괜찮아요**. Among friends splitting the fare: **내가 낼게, 나중에 보내 줘** ("I'll pay, send me your share later").

<Callout type="tip" title="Set up Kakao T before you go">

Kakao T can usually be set up with an international phone number and a foreign card. Once it's set up, you choose the destination in the app and payment happens on its own, which takes almost all the friction out of a ride. Check that auto-payment is on before you get in, not at the destination.

</Callout>

## What to actually say

- Checking the app paid: **자동결제 맞죠?**
- Street hail: **카드로 할게요.**
- Receipt: **괜찮아요.** or **영수증 주세요.**
- Leaving: **감사합니다. 안녕히 계세요!**
