Korean pharmacies handle a lot of what would need [a doctor](/blog/en/k-patto/seeing-a-doctor-in-korea) elsewhere. Cold medicine, painkillers, stomach remedies, and digestive aids are all available over the counter, and the pharmacist will usually ask a couple of questions before choosing one for you.

That conversation is short, but it is a conversation, which is more than most tourist phrasebooks prepare you for.

---

## Finding one

**약국** — pharmacy. Look for a green cross sign.

Most close in the evening, but 24-hour pharmacies exist near large hospitals. In an emergency, hospital emergency rooms dispense too.

---

## Describing what's wrong

The pattern is **~이/가 아파요** — *~ hurts*.

| Korean | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 머리가 아파요 | I have a headache |
| 배가 아파요 | I have a stomachache |
| 목이 아파요 | I have a sore throat |
| 이가 아파요 | I have a toothache |

Other common symptoms:

- 열이 나요. — I have a fever.
- 기침을 해요. — I have a cough.
- 콧물이 나요. — I have a runny nose.
- 소화가 안 돼요. — I have indigestion.
- 설사를 해요. — I have diarrhea.

---

## Asking for medicine

**~ 있어요?** — *do you have ~?*

- 감기약 있어요? — Do you have cold medicine?
- 두통약 있어요? — Headache medicine?
- 소화제 있어요? — Digestive medicine?
- 반창고 있어요? — Bandages?

| Korean | What it is |
|---|---|
| 감기약 | cold medicine |
| 두통약 | headache medicine |
| 진통제 | painkiller |
| 소화제 | digestive aid |
| 해열제 | fever reducer |
| 연고 | ointment |
| 파스 | medicated patch |

---

## What the pharmacist will ask

- 기침도 하세요? — Do you have a cough too?
- 열도 있으세요? — Do you have a fever as well?
- 언제부터 아팠어요? — Since when has it hurt?
- 알레르기 있으세요? — Do you have any allergies?

Short answers are fine: 네 / 아니요 / 어제부터요 (since yesterday).

---

## Understanding the instructions

This is the part worth learning properly.

- 하루 세 번 드세요. — Take it three times a day.
- 식후에 드세요. — Take it after meals.
- 식전에 드세요. — Take it before meals.
- 이건 졸려요. — This one makes you drowsy.
- 운전하시면 안 돼요. — You mustn't drive.
- 커피랑 같이 안 먹는 게 나아요. — Better not to take it with coffee.

식후 (after eating) and 식전 (before eating) appear on almost every package. Learning those two words covers most instructions you will get.

---

## Useful to know

- 처방전 없어요. — I don't have a prescription.
- 외국인이에요. 보험 없어요. — I'm a foreigner. I don't have insurance.
- 얼마예요? — How much is it?

Over-the-counter medicine is inexpensive by most standards — a course of cold medicine often runs a few thousand won.

---

## A full visit

> **You:** 목이 아픈데, 감기약 있어요?
> **Pharmacist:** 기침도 하세요?
> **You:** 네, 조금요.
> **Pharmacist:** 이건 졸려요. 운전하시면 안 돼요.
> **You:** 그럼 밤에 먹을게요.
> **Pharmacist:** 하루 세 번, 식후에 드세요.
