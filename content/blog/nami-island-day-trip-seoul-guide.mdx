Nami Island is a tree-lined river island near Gapyeong, about 1.5 hours from Seoul, famous for its metasequoia lanes and autumn colors. Take the ITX train or a shuttle bus, cross on the short ferry, and arrive early to enjoy the paths before tour groups arrive.

<KeyTakeaways>

- **Travel time:** about 1–1.5 hours from Seoul to the ferry wharf
- **Fastest:** ITX-Cheongchun train to Gapyeong, then a short taxi or bus ride
- **Best time:** arrive before about 9 am, especially on weekends
- **Avoid:** leaving the return trip until the last minute on weekends; trains and buses fill up

</KeyTakeaways>

| Option | Time | Cost (rough) | Best for |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **ITX-Cheongchun train** | About 1 hour to Gapyeong + 10 min taxi | Moderate | Speed and comfort |
| **Gyeongchun Line subway** | About 1.5–2 hours to Gapyeong | Cheap, T-money | Budget travelers |
| **Shuttle bus** | About 1.5 hours direct | Moderate | No transfers; departs from central Seoul |
| **Tour package** | Full day | Higher | Combining with Petite France or the Garden of Morning Calm |

From Gapyeong Station, take a taxi or the Gapyeong City Tour Bus to the ferry wharf. The ferry ride itself takes only a few minutes.

## What to see

| Spot | Best season | Why go |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Metasequoia Lane** | Autumn, summer | The island's most famous photo spot |
| **Ginkgo Lane** | Late October to early November | Golden leaves carpeting the path |
| **Central pine paths** | All year | Quiet forest walks |
| **Riverside paths** | Spring, summer | Views over the Bukhan River |
| **Bike rental** | Spring to autumn | A loop of the island in about an hour |

| **Autumn** | Peak season: red metasequoias and golden ginkgo |

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Do I need a passport for Nami Island?**
No. The "Naminara Republic" visa is just the island's playful name for its entry ticket.

**How long should I spend on Nami Island?**
About three to four hours is enough to walk the main paths, rent a bike and have lunch.

**When are the autumn colors best?**
Usually late October to early November, though it varies with the weather each year.

**Can I combine Nami Island with other sights?**
Yes. Petite France and the Garden of Morning Calm are nearby and reachable by the Gapyeong City Tour Bus or a tour package.
