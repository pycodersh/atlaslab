Nami Island is a tree-lined river island near Gapyeong, famous for its metasequoia lanes and autumn colors. Take the train or a shuttle bus, cross on a short ferry, and arrive early to walk the paths before the tour groups.

<KeyTakeaways title="At a glance">

- **Where:** Gapyeong, about 1 to 1.5 hours east of Seoul
- **Getting there:** ITX-Cheongchun train or the Gyeongchun Line to Gapyeong Station, then about 10 minutes by taxi or bus to the ferry wharf
- **How long:** 3 to 4 hours on the island; a half to full day door to door
- **Cost and booking:** an entry ticket that includes the ferry; no reservation needed for the island, but book a weekend return train in the morning
- **Avoid:** arriving after 10 am on weekends, or leaving the return trip to the last minute

</KeyTakeaways>

| Option | Time | Best for |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **ITX-Cheongchun train** | about 1 hour to Gapyeong, plus a 10-minute taxi | speed and comfort |
| **Gyeongchun Line subway** | about 1.5 to 2 hours to Gapyeong | the cheapest option, paid with T-money |
| **Shuttle bus** | about 1.5 hours, direct | no transfers, from central Seoul |
| **Tour package** | full day | adding Petite France or the Garden of Morning Calm |

From Gapyeong Station, take a taxi or the Gapyeong City Tour Bus to the ferry wharf; the ferry crossing takes only a few minutes. Search **남이섬** in Naver Map or Kakao Map for live train and bus times. The [KTX vs express bus guide](/blog/en/k-patto/ktx-vs-express-bus-korea-day-trips) explains how to book trains as a visitor.

## What to see

| Spot | Best season | Why go |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Metasequoia Lane** | Autumn, summer | The island's most famous photo spot |
| **Ginkgo Lane** | Late October to early November | Golden leaves carpeting the path |
| **Central pine paths** | All year | Quiet forest walks |
| **Riverside paths** | Spring, summer | Views over the Bukhan River |
| **Bike rental** | Spring to autumn | A loop of the island in about an hour |

<Callout type="tip" title="Beat the crowds">
Arrive at the wharf before about 9 am, explore the far side of the island first, and leave by mid-afternoon to avoid the exit queues at the ferry.
</Callout>

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Do I need a passport for Nami Island?**
No. The "Naminara Republic" visa is just the island's playful name for its entry ticket.

**How long should I spend on Nami Island?**
About three to four hours is enough to walk the main paths, rent a bike and have lunch.

**When are the autumn colors best?**
Usually late October to early November, though it varies with the weather each year.

**Can I combine Nami Island with other sights?**
Yes. Petite France and the Garden of Morning Calm are nearby and reachable by the Gapyeong City Tour Bus or a tour package.
