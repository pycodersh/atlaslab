Seoul is a huge, fast-paced capital packed with palaces, shopping and nightlife, while Busan is a relaxed port city of beaches, seafood and hillside villages. With a week or more, see both; with only a few days, start with Seoul.

<KeyTakeaways>

- **Seoul:** history, shopping, K-culture and nightlife; give it at least 3–4 days
- **Busan:** beaches, seafood and a slower pace; 2–3 days is ideal
- **Between them:** about 2.5–3 hours by KTX
- **Avoid:** squeezing Busan into a single rushed day trip from Seoul

</KeyTakeaways>

## The two cities compared

| | Seoul | Busan |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Vibe** | Big, busy, trendy | Coastal, relaxed, local |
| **Highlights** | Palaces, Bukchon, Myeongdong, Hongdae, Seongsu, Han River | Haeundae and Gwangalli beaches, Gamcheon Village, Jagalchi Market |
| **Food** | Everything, from street food to fine dining | Seafood, pork soup with rice (dwaeji gukbap), milmyeon |
| **Getting around** | Dense subway and bus network | Subway plus buses; more walking up hills |
| **Costs** | Slightly higher, especially hotels | Slightly lower, beachfront hotels pricier in summer |
| **Best season** | Spring and autumn | Summer for beaches, autumn for festivals |

## Best areas to stay

Where you sleep shapes the trip. In both cities, staying near a subway line saves a lot of time.

| City | Area | Good for |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Seoul** | Myeongdong / Jung-gu | First-timers, shopping, central location |
| **Seoul** | Hongdae / Mapo | Nightlife, cafes, a younger crowd, airport train access |
| **Seoul** | Gangnam | Business trips, upscale dining, SRT access |
| **Busan** | Haeundae | Beach, resort hotels |
| **Busan** | Seomyeon | Central hub, food, transport connections |
| **Busan** | Gwangalli | Bridge night views, bars by the beach |

## How many days?

| Trip length | Suggested split |
| :--- | :--- |
| **3–4 days** | Seoul only, maybe a day trip nearby |
| **5–7 days** | 4 days Seoul + 2–3 days Busan |
| **8–10 days** | Seoul, Busan, plus Gyeongju or Jeonju on the way |
| **2 weeks** | Add Jeju or the east coast |

<Callout type="tip" title="Fly in one, out the other">
Some international flights use Busan's Gimhae Airport. Flying into Seoul and out of Busan (or the reverse) saves a return train trip and gives you an extra half day of sightseeing.
</Callout>

| Option | Time | Rough cost | Note |

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Is Busan worth visiting if I've been to Seoul?**
Yes. Busan has a completely different feel, with beaches, mountains and seafood, and it's an easy train ride away.

**Which is better for a first trip, Seoul or Busan?**
Seoul, for most first-timers. It has more famous sights and easier connections, and Busan is a great addition with more time.

**Is Busan cheaper than Seoul?**
Generally a little, especially for food and some hotels, though beachfront hotels are expensive in summer.

**Can I visit Busan in winter?**
Yes. It's milder than Seoul in winter, though too cold for swimming. Markets, temples and night views are still great.
