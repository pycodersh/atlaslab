KSPO Dome is the roughly 15,000-seat arena inside Olympic Park in southeast Seoul, and one of the most common stops for K-pop tours. It's easy to reach, but the crowds, passport checks and late-night exits catch out first-timers. Here's what to sort out before show day.

<KeyTakeaways title="At a glance">

- **Where:** 424 Olympic-ro, Songpa-gu, inside Olympic Park (Korean name: 올림픽체조경기장)
- **Getting there:** Olympic Park Station, Lines 5 and 9, then about a 5 to 7 minute walk
- **How long:** arrive at least 2 hours before showtime if you need to collect tickets or buy merch
- **Bring:** your original passport and a small bag
- **Cost:** seat prices vary by show; check your ticket page

</KeyTakeaways>

## Getting to KSPO Dome

| Route | How | Good to know |
|---|---|---|
| **Olympic Park Station** | Lines 5 and 9 | Closest. Guides name Exit 3 or Exit 4, and staff may close one after events |
| **Line 5 from central Seoul** | Board toward Macheon | The line splits, so a train to Hanam won't get you there |
| **Line 9 from Gangnam** | Express trains stop here | Often the most comfortable option |
| **From Incheon Airport** | Airport Bus 6300 | Get off at Olympic Sports Village APT |

From the station, follow the crowd and the concert banners. On the Exit 4 route you'll pass the large thumb sculpture on your way into the park.

## Tickets and ID

If you bought through a global site such as NOL World, the rules on your booking page come first. In general:

- **Bring your original passport.** Photos and copies may be refused.
- **The name has to match.** The name on your booking should match your passport exactly.
- **Find the foreigner booth early.** Its location changes by show, so check the concert notice. One past show placed it near Gate 1.
- **Don't arrive at the last minute.** Booths can close about 30 minutes before the show, and ID checks can take 20 to 40 minutes on busy nights.

Only buy from official sellers. Tickets from resellers can be canceled, and your name may not match at the door.

## Bags and lockers

Many concerts limit bags to about A4 size, and the limit is set for each show. Larger bags may be sent to a bag-check point, if the organizer runs one.

Coin lockers at Olympic Park Station are the usual option, but they fill early on concert days and the station closes around midnight. Our [subway locker guide](/blog/en/k-patto/hands-free-seoul-subway-lockers-guide) covers how to use them. If you have a suitcase, the easiest plan is to leave it at your hotel.

## Food and restrooms

Meeting Plaza inside the park has restaurants and cafes. Eat before you queue, and don't assume you can leave the hall and come back, since re-entry depends on the event.

Restrooms inside the hall get very crowded. Use one outside before you enter, and carry tissues.

## Picking a seat

Seats run across three floors, and ticket classes vary by show. The steep upper rear tier makes the stage feel small, so take a lower side seat if the price difference is small. The steep stairs in the upper levels are also worth knowing about if you have mobility concerns.

## Getting home

This is where a little planning pays off. Shows often end between 9:30 and 10:30 p.m., and Line 5 gets packed.

| Option | Best for | Note |
|---|---|---|
| **Line 9, Olympic Park** | Gangnam, Yeouido | Usually moves faster than Line 5 |
| **Hanseong Baekje Station** | Avoiding the crush on Line 9 | About a 20 minute walk |
| **Mongchontoseong Station** | Line 8 connections | About a 23 minute walk |
| **Taxi** | Late nights, groups | Walk to a main road first, because the station queue is long |

Olympic Park's walking paths and gates have set hours, and some close at around 10 p.m., so stay with the main crowd after dark. Trains can stop before midnight on weekends and holidays. Check the last train for your exact direction before the show starts, and see our [Korean taxi guide](/blog/en/k-patto/korea-taxi-survival-guide) as a backup.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What is the nearest station to KSPO Dome?**
Olympic Park Station on Lines 5 and 9. The arena is about a 5 to 7 minute walk away.

**Do I need my passport at a K-pop concert in Korea?**
Usually yes if you bought a ticket as a foreign visitor. Bring the original, because photos and copies may be refused.

**Can I bring a suitcase to KSPO Dome?**
It's best not to. Many shows limit bags to about A4 size, and station lockers fill early on concert days.

**What is the fastest way home after a concert?**
Line 9 from Olympic Park Station is often quicker than Line 5. Walking to Hanseong Baekje Station can also help you avoid the crowd.
