Olive Young and stores like it are on almost every corner in Seoul, and they're one of the few shopping situations where staff come to you and ask questions. Most of the vocabulary is borrowed from English, which helps until you hit the words that changed meaning on the way in.

## What staff will say first

| You'll hear | Meaning | Answer |
|---|---|---|
| 어서 오세요. | Welcome. | a nod |
| 뭐 찾으세요? | What are you looking for? | 구경할게요. / 선크림 찾고 있어요. |
| 도움 필요하시면 말씀해 주세요. | Let me know if you need help. | 네, 감사합니다. |

**구경할게요** (*gugyeonghalgeyo*, "I'm just looking") ends the conversation politely. Nobody takes it as rude. To find something specific, the pattern is **[item] 어디 있어요?**, covered in [finding what you need](/blog/en/k-patto/finding-items-olive-young-korea).

## Describing your skin

| Korean | Romanization | Skin type |
|---|---|---|
| 건성 | geonseong | dry |
| 지성 | jiseong | oily |
| 복합성 | bokapseong | combination |
| 민감성 | mingamseong | sensitive |
| 여드름 피부 | yeodeureum pibu | acne-prone |

Say **저는 건성이에요** ("I have dry skin") or **민감성 피부예요** ("I have sensitive skin"), and staff can point you to a shelf in seconds. [Asking for recommendations](/blog/en/k-patto/asking-recommendations-olive-young-korea) goes further: comparing two products and testing them.

## Asking about products

| Korean | Romanization | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| 이거 뭐에 좋아요? | igeo mwoe joayo? | What is this good for? |
| 후기 좋아요? | hugi joayo? | Are the reviews good? |
| 좀 더 저렴한 거 있어요? | jom deo jeoryeomhan geo isseoyo? | Anything cheaper? |
| 샘플 있어요? | saempeul isseoyo? | Do you have samples? |
| 이거 원플러스원이에요? | igeo won peulleoseu wonieyo? | Is this buy one, get one? |

## The Konglish trap

Some product names are English words that mean something else here. The [Konglish guide](/blog/en/k-patto/konglish-words-when-english-becomes-korean) has many more.

| Korean | Sounds like | Actually means |
|---|---|---|
| 스킨 | skin | toner, the watery step after cleansing |
| 로션 | lotion | a light moisturizer |
| 쿠션 | cushion | compact foundation in a sponge |
| 미스트 | mist | face spray, used over makeup |
| 선크림 | sun cream | sunscreen |

**스킨** and **토너** are the same step; brands use both names. **에센스** and **세럼** are both concentrated treatments, and the line between them is more marketing than chemistry.

## Deals

Tags marked **1+1** or **2+1** are everywhere. **기획** on a box means a special set, often with an extra mini product inside. If you're unsure, ask **이거 행사예요?** ("is this on promotion?").

## Polite or casual?

Staff use honorific service Korean (**찾으세요?**, **드릴까요?**). You only need polite **-요**: **샘플 있어요?**, **이걸로 할게요**. With friends: **이거 어때?** ("how's this?").

## A full visit

> **Staff:** 뭐 찾으세요?  
> **You:** 선크림 찾고 있어요. 저는 민감성 피부예요.  
> **Staff:** 이거 어떠세요? 후기 진짜 좋아요.  
> **You:** 샘플 있어요?  
> **Staff:** 네, 드릴게요.  
> **You:** 그럼 이걸로 할게요.
