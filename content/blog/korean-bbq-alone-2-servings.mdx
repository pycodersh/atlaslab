Every table through the window has three or four people around a grill, and you're on your own. Walking in alone is fine. There's just one rule that catches almost every solo visitor.

## The exchange

**A:** 혼자 먹어도 돼요?  
*(Can I eat alone?)*  
**B:** 네, 근데 2인분부터예요.  
*(Yes, but the minimum is two servings.)*  
**A:** 2인분 주세요.  
*(Two servings, please.)*

Most grill restaurants require at least **2인분** (*iinbun*, two servings) whatever the size of your group. It's about the grill: lighting a burner for one small portion doesn't work for them. You can absolutely eat alone; you'll just order food for two.

## Asking and ordering

| Korean | Romanization | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| 혼자 먹어도 돼요? | honja meogeodo dwaeyo? | Can I eat alone? |
| 1인분 돼요? | irinbun dwaeyo? | Is one serving possible? |
| 2인분 주세요. | iinbun juseyo | Two servings, please. |
| 1인분 추가해 주세요. | irinbun chugahae juseyo | One more serving, please. |

**인분** counts portions with Sino-Korean numbers: 일인분, 이인분, 삼인분 (not 둘인분). The [counters guide](/blog/en/k-patto/korean-counters-gae-myeong-jan-byeong) covers why.

## What staff will say to you

| You'll hear | Romanization | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| 2인분부터예요. | iinbunbuteoyeyo | It starts from two servings. |
| 네, 괜찮아요. | ne, gwaenchanayo | Yes, that's fine. |
| 구워 드릴게요. | guwo deurilgeyo | I'll grill it for you. |
| 다 익었어요. | da igeosseoyo | It's cooked. |
| 뒤집지 마세요. | dwijipji maseyo | Don't flip it yet. |

At many restaurants staff grill and cut the meat for you; at others you do it yourself. If you're unsure, ask **이거 다 익었어요?** ("is this done?").

## Polite or casual?

| Polite (staff) | Casual (friends) | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| 2인분 주세요. | 2인분 시키자. | (Let's order) two servings. |
| 다 익었어요? | 다 익었어? | Is it done? |
| 가위 주세요. | 가위 좀 줘. | Pass the scissors. |

## Places without the minimum

Newer chains with individual grills cater to solo diners, and many barbecue places serve a **점심특선** (lunch special) of single-portion rice dishes. Restaurants that welcome **혼밥** (*honbap*, eating alone) sometimes say so on the door.

## What arrives with the meat

| Korean | Romanization | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| 상추 | sangchu | Lettuce for wrapping |
| 쌈장 | ssamjang | Thick dipping paste |
| 마늘 | maneul | Raw garlic to grill |
| 파절이 | pajeori | Seasoned scallion salad |

None of it costs extra. Wrap a piece of meat in lettuce with a dab of ssamjang and eat it in one bite. Meat is cut at the table with kitchen scissors; if there are none, ask **가위 주세요**.

## What to actually say

- At the door: **혼자 먹어도 돼요?**
- Ordering: **2인분 주세요.**
- Checking: **이거 다 익었어요?**
