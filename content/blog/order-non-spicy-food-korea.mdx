You order something that looks manageable and it arrives red. Korean spice tolerance runs on a different scale, and so do the words people use to describe it.

## The exchange

**A:** 이거 매워요?  
*(Is this spicy?)*  
**B:** 조금 매워요.  
*(It's a little spicy.)*  
**A:** 안 맵게 해주세요.  
*(Please make it not spicy.)*

All three lines are polite **-요** speech, which is what you use with staff. Take **조금 매워요** seriously. A Korean "a little" is often "quite spicy" for a first-timer.

## Asking before you order

| Korean | Romanization | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| 이거 매워요? | igeo maewoyo? | Is this spicy? |
| 안 매운 거 있어요? | an maeun geo isseoyo? | Do you have something that isn't spicy? |
| 매운 거 못 먹어요. | maeun geo mon meogeoyo | I can't eat spicy food. |

**안** in front of a word makes it negative: 매워요 is "it's spicy", 안 매워요 is "it's not spicy". **못 먹어요** ("I can't eat") also covers allergies: **돼지고기 못 먹어요** ("I can't eat pork").

## What staff will tell you

| You'll hear | Romanization | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| 안 매워요. | an maewoyo | It's not spicy. |
| 조금 매워요. | jogeum maewoyo | It's a little spicy. |
| 많이 매워요. | mani maewoyo | It's very spicy. |
| 맵기 조절 돼요. | maepgi jojeol dwaeyo | We can adjust the spice. |
| 그건 안 돼요. | geugeon an dwaeyo | That one can't be changed. |

If someone says **많이 매워요**, believe them twice over.

## Changing your order

| Korean | Romanization | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| 안 맵게 해 주세요. | an maepge hae juseyo | Please make it not spicy. |
| 덜 맵게 해 주세요. | deol maepge hae juseyo | Please make it less spicy. |
| 순한 맛으로 주세요. | sunhan maseuro juseyo | The mild version, please. |

Say it when you order. **덜 맵게** keeps some heat; **순한 맛** asks for the lowest level at places with set spice levels.

## When it can't be adjusted

Some dishes are spicy by nature. Batch-cooked stews like 김치찌개 and 부대찌개 have the spice in the pot already, and marinated dishes like 닭갈비 and 제육볶음 sit in their sauce before cooking. If you hear **안 돼요**, it isn't rudeness: ask what else is mild.

| Dish | What it is |
|---|---|
| 불고기 | Sweet marinated beef |
| 갈비탕 | Clear beef rib soup |
| 삼계탕 | Ginseng chicken soup |
| 잡채 | Glass noodles with vegetables |
| 계란찜 | Steamed egg |
| 돈까스 | Breaded pork cutlet |

<Callout type="tip" title="Already too late?">

Water barely helps; rice and dairy do. Ask for **공기밥 주세요** (*gonggibap juseyo*, a bowl of rice), and keep it within reach. The [spicy food phrases guide](/blog/en/k-patto/spicy-food-korean-phrases) covers what to say mid-burn.

</Callout>

## What to actually say

- Before ordering: **이거 매워요?**
- Ordering: **안 맵게 해 주세요.**
- Plan B: **안 매운 거 있어요?**
