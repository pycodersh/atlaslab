In Seoul, Kakao T is the most widely used taxi app, Uber works with many of the same taxis, and street taxis are still easy outside peak hours. Whichever you use, keep a record of the ride, because that's how you get back anything you leave behind.

<KeyTakeaways>

- **Kakao T:** the largest network; foreign cards often work for in-app payment
- **Uber:** familiar for visitors, connects to local taxis in Seoul
- **Street taxi:** fine in the daytime; pay by card and keep the receipt
- **Lost something?** Use the app's ride history or the receipt, then call 120 or 1330

</KeyTakeaways>

## Kakao T or Uber: which is better for foreigners?

For a basic ride, neither is cheaper: both send the same metered taxis. Uber is easier if you already have an account, while Kakao T has far more drivers, so it's faster to get a car outside central Seoul and late at night.

| Feature | Kakao T | Uber (formerly UT) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Foreign Credit Card** | Often accepted for in-app payment; varies by card | Works with your existing global Uber account |
| **Driver Fleet Size** | Largest by far; most Korean taxis take its calls | Moderate; plentiful in central Seoul |
| **Fare Cost** | Meter fare, plus a call fee for some options | Meter fare, plus fees for some options |
| **English UI** | English menus available | Same app you use at home |
| **Best Scenario** | Hailing anywhere in Korea, busy nights | Zero app setup for tourists who already use Uber |

## Kakao T vs Uber vs street taxi

| | Kakao T | Uber | Street taxi |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Availability** | Highest in Korea | Good in Seoul | Everywhere, harder at night |
| **Language** | Korean and English menus | English | Show the address in Korean |
| **Payment** | In-app (foreign cards often accepted) or in the car | In-app with your existing account | Card, T-money or cash in the car |
| **Fare** | Meter fare, plus a call fee for some options | Meter fare, plus fees for some options | Meter fare |
| **Ride record** | Saved in the app | Saved in the app | Only on your receipt |

All three use the same metered taxis for basic rides, so the base price is similar; premium options and late-night demand add fees.

<Callout type="tip" title="Busy nights">
On Friday and Saturday nights in nightlife areas, app requests can take a while to get accepted. Walk a few blocks away from the busiest street, or try both apps.
</Callout>

## Setting up Kakao T

| Step | What to do |
| :--- | :--- |
| **1. Install** | Download Kakao T before you travel |
| **2. Sign up** | Register with your phone number; international numbers often work |
| **3. Add a card** | Add a foreign card for in-app payment, or plan to pay in the car |
| **4. Set pickup** | Pin your exact location; add a note like the building entrance |
| **5. Ride** | Check the car number on the screen before getting in |

Always match the car number on your screen with the taxi's plate before you get in.

## If you leave something in a taxi

| How you rode | What to do |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Kakao T or Uber** | Open the ride history; it shows the car and lets you contact the driver or support |
| **Street taxi, paid by card** | The receipt shows the car number and company; call the company or 120 |
| **Street taxi, paid with T-money** | The ride may be traceable via the card; ask 120 for help |
| **Paid cash, no receipt** | Report it to the police Lost112 site and call 120 or 1330 |

<Callout type="info" title="Helpful numbers">
**120** is the Seoul city call center (Dasan Call Center), which helps trace taxis and lost items, with foreign-language support. **1330** is the Korea Travel Helpline for interpretation, 24 hours a day.
</Callout>

## Frequently Asked Questions

### If I book Uber in Korea, does a regular taxi come?
Yes. Uber in Korea (formerly UT) mostly dispatches licensed, metered taxis, the same orange, silver and white cars you'd hail on the street. Check that the plate matches the one in the app before you get in.

### Which taxi app is cheapest?
For basic rides, prices are similar because both use metered taxis. Premium options, call fees and late-night demand change the final price.

### What if in-app payment fails?
Tell the driver "카드로 결제할게요" (*kadeuro gyeoljehalgeyo*, "I'll pay by card") and pay with a card or T-money at the reader in the car, or with cash. Afterwards, check the app's ride history and contact support if you were charged twice.

### Can I use Kakao T without a Korean number?
Often yes, with an international number and a foreign card, though features and payment options can vary.

### How do I get back something I left in a taxi?
Check the app ride history or your card receipt for the car number, then contact the driver, the taxi company, or call 120.
