Korean delivery is fast, low-minimum, and goes almost anywhere: a park, a riverbank, an office at midnight. Most of it happens in apps, so the Korean you need is mostly reading, plus a few lines for the door.

## The apps

**배달의민족** (Baemin) is the largest, with **쿠팡이츠** (Coupang Eats) and **요기요** (Yogiyo) close behind. The practical hurdle for visitors is usually account setup and payment, not language. The [delivery apps guide](/blog/en/k-patto/korean-delivery-apps-tourists) covers what works for tourists.

## Words on the order screen

| Korean | Romanization | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| 배달 / 포장 | baedal / pojang | delivery / pick-up |
| 최소주문금액 | choesojumungeumaek | minimum order |
| 배달팁 | baedaltip | delivery fee (not a tip) |
| 예상 시간 | yesang sigan | estimated time |
| 요청사항 | yocheongsahang | requests for the store or rider |
| 결제하기 | gyeoljehagi | pay |

**배달팁** looks like "tip" but it's the delivery fee the app adds, shown before you pay. You don't give the rider anything extra.

## The request box

| Korean | Romanization | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| 안 맵게 해 주세요. | an maepge hae juseyo | Please make it not spicy. |
| 일회용품 안 주셔도 돼요. | irhoeyongpum an jusyeodo dwaeyo | No disposable cutlery needed. |
| 문 앞에 놔 주세요. | mun ape nwa juseyo | Please leave it at the door. |
| 도착하면 전화 주세요. | dochakamyeon jeonhwa juseyo | Please call when you arrive. |

**문 앞에 놔 주세요** is the most common request in Korea: many orders are left at the door, with a photo sent to the app.

## If you call

| Korean | Romanization | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| 배달 되나요? | baedal doenayo? | Do you deliver? |
| 후라이드 치킨 한 마리 주세요. | huraideu chikin han mari juseyo | One whole fried chicken, please. |
| 주소는 ○○예요. | jusoneun ○○yeyo | The address is ○○. |
| 얼마나 걸려요? | eolmana geollyeoyo? | How long will it take? |

**마리** counts whole animals, so chicken is ordered by the bird.

## What you'll hear

| You'll hear | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 주소가 어떻게 되세요? | What's the address? |
| 40분 정도 걸려요. | It takes about 40 minutes. |
| 결제는 어떻게 하시겠어요? | How will you pay? |
| 배달 왔습니다! | Your delivery is here! |

## Polite or casual?

| Polite (restaurant) | Casual (friends) | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| 치킨 한 마리 주세요. | 치킨 시킬까? | (Shall we order) a chicken? |
| 얼마나 걸려요? | 언제 와? | When will it come? |

With friends, **시키다** ("to order in") is the verb: **배달 시키자** ("let's get delivery").

<Callout type="tip" title="반반: half and half">

Fried chicken comes **후라이드** (plain), **양념** (sweet-spicy sauce), or **반반** (half of each), which is what most groups order. You'll also hear **가성비** a lot: "value for money".

</Callout>

## What to actually say

- In the request box: **문 앞에 놔 주세요.**
- At the door: **감사합니다!**
- With friends: **배달 시키자.**
