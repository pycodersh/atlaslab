Korean food delivery is genuinely different from what most countries have. Thirty minutes is normal, minimum orders are low, and drivers will find you in a park, on a riverbank, or on the twelfth floor of an office building at midnight.

Most of it happens through apps now, which means the Korean you need is mostly reading rather than speaking.

---

## The apps

**배달의민족** is the largest. The interface is Korean-only, but it is navigable with a translation app and the layout is consistent.

**요기요** and **쿠팡이츠** are the main alternatives.

The practical barrier is not language — it is that these apps usually require a Korean phone number and a Korean payment method. Visitors often order through a Korean friend, or use the hotel front desk.

## Words on the order screen

| Korean | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 배달 | delivery |
| 포장 | takeout |
| 최소주문금액 | minimum order |
| 배달비 | delivery fee |
| 배달 시간 | delivery time |
| 리뷰 | reviews |
| 요청사항 | special requests |
| 결제 | payment |

**요청사항** is the free-text box, and it is worth using:

- 안 맵게 해 주세요. — Please make it not spicy.
- 일회용품 안 주셔도 돼요. — No disposable utensils needed.
- 문 앞에 놔 주세요. — Please leave it at the door.

## If you call instead

Some smaller places still take phone orders.

- 배달 되나요? — Do you deliver?
- 후라이드 치킨 한 마리 주세요. — One whole fried chicken, please.
- 주소는 ○○예요. — The address is ○○.
- 얼마나 걸려요? — How long will it take?
- 현금으로 낼게요. — I'll pay in cash.

마리 is the counter for whole animals, and chicken is ordered that way — 한 마리, 두 마리.

## What Koreans actually order

| Korean | What it is |
|---|---|
| 치킨 | fried chicken |
| 족발 | braised pig's trotters |
| 보쌈 | boiled pork with wraps |
| 짜장면 | black bean noodles |
| 탕수육 | sweet and sour pork |
| 피자 | pizza |
| 떡볶이 | tteokbokki |

**치킨** is the default. 후라이드 is plain fried, 양념 is sauced, and 반반 gets you half of each — which is what most people order.

## When it arrives

- 얼마예요? — How much is it?
- 카드로 할게요. — By card.
- 감사합니다. — Thanks.

Tipping is not expected and will confuse the driver.

Containers go outside your door after eating. Many buildings have a designated spot, and some restaurants collect their own dishes if you ordered from a place that delivers on real plates.

---

## Useful phrases about delivery

- 배달 시켜요! — Let's order delivery!
- 뭐 시켜요? — What should we order?
- 배달 얼마나 걸려요? — How long does delivery take?
- 가성비 좋아요. — Good value for money.

**가성비** — cost-to-performance ratio — is used constantly in Korean, far more than any English equivalent. It applies to food, phones, apartments, anything.
