Korea runs on iced americano, all year round. It even has a nickname: **아아** (*aa*), short for 아이스 아메리카노. Ordering one takes four words; what catches people out is what the barista asks back.

## The exchange

**A:** 아이스 아메리카노 하나 주세요.  
*(One iced americano, please.)*  
**B:** 사이즈는요?  
*(What size?)*  
**A:** 큰 사이즈로 주세요. 시럽 넣어 주세요.  
*(Large, please. And please add syrup.)*

The whole exchange is polite speech. You answer with **-요** forms; staff will use the honorific **-시-** with you (드시고, 주시면). Casual speech (반말) never comes up at the counter.

## The order

**아이스 아메리카노 하나 주세요** (*aiseu amerikano hana juseyo*): say the drink, add **하나 주세요** ("one, please"), and you're done.

| Korean | Romanization | Drink |
|---|---|---|
| 아메리카노 | amerikano | Americano |
| 라떼 | latte | Latte |
| 카푸치노 | kapuchino | Cappuccino |
| 바닐라 라떼 | banilla latte | Vanilla latte |
| 녹차 라떼 | nokcha latte | Green tea latte |

## What staff will ask you

| You'll hear | Meaning | Answer like this |
|---|---|---|
| 아이스로 드릴까요, 따뜻하게 드릴까요? | Iced or hot? | 아이스요. / 따뜻하게요. |
| 사이즈는요? | What size? | 큰 사이즈로 주세요. |
| 드시고 가세요? | For here? | 여기서 마실게요. / 포장이요. |
| 결제는 어떻게 하시겠어요? | How will you pay? | 카드요. |
| 나오면 불러 드릴게요. | I'll call you when it's ready. | 네, 감사합니다. |

If you already said 아이스 in your order, they skip the first question. You pay at the counter before you get the drink, and there's no tipping.

## Sweetness and other tweaks

A Korean americano has no sugar unless you ask. Many cafes have a self-serve station with syrup and stirrers; if not, ask at the counter.

| Korean | Romanization | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| 시럽 넣어 주세요. | sireop neoeo juseyo | Please add syrup. |
| 연하게 해 주세요. | yeonhage hae juseyo | Make it weaker, please. |
| 샷 추가해 주세요. | syat chugahae juseyo | An extra shot, please. |
| 얼음 적게 주세요. | eoreum jeokge juseyo | Less ice, please. |

<Callout type="tip" title="얼죽아: iced, even in January">

You'll see people carrying iced americanos through snow. It's common enough to have its own slang: **얼죽아**, short for "I'd rather freeze to death than drink it hot." Ordering iced in winter won't get you a second look.

</Callout>

## After you order

At small cafes the barista calls your order out. At most chains you get a **진동벨** (*jindongbel*), a buzzer that vibrates when your drink is ready. Either way you collect it yourself; nobody brings it to your table.

## What to actually say

- Ordering: **아이스 아메리카노 하나 주세요.**
- Size: **큰 사이즈로 주세요.**
- Paying: **카드요.**
- Sweet: **시럽 넣어 주세요.**
