Chuncheon, a lake city an hour from Seoul, is best known for two things: dakgalbi, its spicy stir-fried chicken, and, more recently, LEGOLAND Korea on an island in the lake. This short guide pairs them into one day: the theme park first, then dinner downtown on Myeongdong Dakgalbi Street.

<KeyTakeaways title="At a glance">

- **The route:** LEGOLAND Korea → Myeongdong Dakgalbi Street, about 4 km by road
- **Getting there:** ITX-Cheongchun train or the Gyeongchun Line subway from Seoul to Chuncheon Station
- **Cost:** LEGOLAND needs a ticket; dinner is around the price of two portions of dakgalbi
- **Best timing:** a full day at the park, then dinner from about 6 pm
- **Avoid:** winter visits without checking the park calendar; opening days are limited

</KeyTakeaways>

LEGOLAND Korea opened in 2022 on Jungdo, an island in the lake beside Chuncheon. It's built for families with children roughly between 2 and 12: rides, play areas, building zones and Miniland, a brick-built model world that includes Korean landmarks. There's a LEGOLAND hotel on site.

| | |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Tickets** | Required; buy online in advance for the best price |
| **Opening days** | Seasonal; in winter the park is open on fewer days and some rides close |
| **Getting there** | Shuttle bus or taxi from Chuncheon Station |

<Callout type="warn" title="Check the calendar">
The park's opening days and hours change by season, and it has closed for parts of past winters. Check the official calendar before you book your train.
</Callout>

Downtown Chuncheon's Myeongdong district has an alley packed with dakgalbi restaurants. Dakgalbi is chicken marinated in a spicy red sauce and stir-fried at your table with cabbage, sweet potato and rice cakes on a big round iron plate. When most of it is gone, ask for rice to be fried in the leftover sauce.

Most restaurants need an order of at least two portions. Staff usually cook it for you, so leave the spatula to them until they say it's ready.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Is LEGOLAND Korea open in winter?**
On a reduced schedule, with some rides closed. Check the official calendar before you go.

**How do I get from Seoul to Chuncheon?**
The ITX-Cheongchun train from Yongsan or Cheongnyangni, or the Gyeongchun Line subway.

**Can I order dakgalbi for one person?**
Usually not. Most places need at least two portions.

**Is Nami Island in Chuncheon?**
It's close to Gapyeong, on the way to Chuncheon. Combine the two over two days.
