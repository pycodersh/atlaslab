You've finished your coffee, you stand to leave, and everyone else is carrying their cup somewhere. Most Korean cafes expect you to clear your own table.

## The exchange

**A:** 컵은 어디에 반납할까요?  
*(Where should I return the cup?)*  
**B:** 저한테 주시면 돼요.  
*(You can just give it to me.)*  
**A:** 커피 정말 맛있었어요!  
*(The coffee was really good!)*

The cups go to the **반납대** (*bannapdae*), the return station, usually near the register with a tray rack and bins.

## Asking where it goes

| Korean | Romanization | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| 컵은 어디에 반납할까요? | keobeun eodie bannaphalkkayo? | Where should I return the cup? |
| 반납대 어디예요? | bannapdae eodiyeyo? | Where's the return station? |
| 이거 어디에 둬요? | igeo eodie dwoyo? | Where do I put this? |

**이거** ("this") makes the last one work for a cup, a tray, or anything else in your hands.

## What you'll hear back

| You'll hear | Romanization | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| 저한테 주시면 돼요. | jeohante jusimyeon dwaeyo | You can just give it to me. |
| 저쪽에 있어요. | jeojjoge isseoyo | It's over there. |
| 반납대에 놔 주세요. | bannapdaee nwa juseyo | Please leave it at the return station. |
| 그냥 두고 가셔도 돼요. | geunyang dugo gasyeodo dwaeyo | You can just leave it. |
| 또 오세요! | tto oseyo! | Come again! |

Small cafes often take the cup from you; chains point you to the station. The last answer, "just leave it", is your cue that this cafe clears tables itself.

## Sorting at the station

Larger cafes split it into three: a drain for leftover drink and ice, a bin for lids, straws and napkins, and a rack for cups and trays. Pour out the liquid first, then the plastic, then stack the cup. Mugs usually go to a separate spot or straight to staff.

## A good goodbye

| Polite (staff) | Casual (friends) | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| 커피 정말 맛있었어요. | 커피 진짜 맛있었다. | The coffee was really good. |
| 잘 마셨습니다. | 잘 마셨어. | Thanks, that was great (after a drink). |

**잘 마셨습니다** (*jal masyeotseumnida*) is the drink version of **잘 먹었습니다**, the standard thanks after a meal. Said as you hand over the cup, it lands well.

<Callout type="info" title="Where you don't clear the table">

In restaurants you leave dishes where they are. Full-service cafes and places with a table call bell also clear for you. The return-your-cup rule is mostly a self-service cafe and fast-food habit.

</Callout>

## What to actually say

- Where does it go: **이거 어디에 둬요?**
- Handing it over: **잘 마셨습니다.**
- A compliment: **커피 정말 맛있었어요!**
