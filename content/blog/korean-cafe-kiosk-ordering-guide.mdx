Most budget cafes in Korea, such as Mega Coffee, Compose Coffee and Paik's Coffee, take orders at a self-service kiosk. Choose hot or iced, set your options, pick takeout or for here, and pay by card; your number appears on a screen when it's ready.

<KeyTakeaways>

- **Default order:** 아아 (*a-a*), short for iced americano, even in winter
- **Price:** about 1,500–2,500 KRW at budget chains, 4,500–6,500 KRW at specialty cafes
- **Language:** many kiosks have an English button in a top corner
- **Avoid:** choosing "for here" with a takeaway cup; many cafes give real cups for dine-in

</KeyTakeaways>

## Hot or iced: the menu words

| Term | Korean | Meaning | Note |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **아아** | 아이스 아메리카노 | Iced americano | The national default |
| **뜨아** | 뜨거운 아메리카노 | Hot americano | Say 따뜻한 (warm) or HOT on kiosks |
| **디카페인** | 디카페인 | Decaf | Usually 300–1,000 KRW extra |
| **샷 추가** | 샷 추가 | Extra shot | About 500 KRW |
| **연하게** | 연하게 | Weaker, more water | Free |
| **라떼** | 라떼 | Latte | 바닐라 라떼 is a best-seller |

## Options worth knowing

| Option | Kiosk label | Choices |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Size** | 사이즈 | Regular, large (Mega and Compose are big by default) |
| **Ice** | 얼음 | 적게 (less), 보통 (regular), 많이 (more) |
| **Sweetness** | 당도 · 시럽 | 덜 달게 (less sweet), syrup add or remove |
| **Milk** | 우유 변경 | Oat milk or soy milk, sometimes extra |
| **Takeout** | 포장 / 테이크아웃 | Plastic or paper cup to go |
| **For here** | 매장 / 먹고 가기 | Glass or mug at many cafes |

<Callout type="info" title="Cup rules">
Korea restricts disposable cups for customers drinking inside cafes. Rules and enforcement have shifted over the years, but many cafes still serve dine-in drinks in reusable cups. If you might leave with it, choose takeout from the start.
</Callout>

## Ordering at the kiosk, step by step

| Step | What to do |
| :--- | :--- |
| **1. Language** | Tap English or the globe icon if you see one |
| **2. For here or to go** | 매장 (for here) or 포장 (takeout) |
| **3. Pick a drink** | Coffee tabs come first; seasonal drinks have a NEW badge |
| **4. Options** | Hot or iced, size, extra shot, ice and sweetness |
| **5. Pay** | Insert or tap your card; foreign Visa and Mastercard usually work |
| **6. Pick up** | Watch the screen or listen for your number |

If the kiosk rejects your card, the counter staff can usually take payment directly.

## Budget chains vs specialty cafes

Korea's cafe scene runs on two tracks. Budget chains like Mega Coffee, Compose Coffee and Paik's Coffee sell large drinks cheaply and are built for takeout, often with only a few stools. Specialty cafes compete on beans, desserts and interior design, and charge accordingly; they're where you sit, work or meet friends.

| | Budget chain | Specialty cafe |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Iced americano** | About 1,500–2,500 KRW | About 4,500–6,500 KRW |
| **Ordering** | Kiosk | Counter or kiosk |
| **Seating** | A few seats or none | Comfortable, often for hours |
| **Best for** | A quick caffeine fix on the move | Relaxing, working, desserts |

<DoDont>
<Do>

- Return your tray and cup to the return shelf when you leave
- Order one drink per person if you sit down
- Check the vibrating buzzer or number screen
- Ask for 덜 달게 (less sweet) if you find drinks sugary

</Do>
<Dont>

- Walk out with a dine-in mug
- Camp at a table for hours at a small cafe at peak time
- Pour leftover ice and drink straight into the bin; use the liquid sink
- Forget the restroom code printed on the receipt

</Dont>

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Why do Koreans drink iced americano in winter?**
It's a long-standing habit, and the phrase "eoljuka" (freezing to death but still iced americano) jokes about it. Hot drinks are always available too.

**Can I pay with cash at a kiosk?**
Most kiosks are card-only. Hand cash to the staff at the counter instead.

**What is the difference between budget chains and specialty cafes?**
Budget chains such as Mega Coffee and Compose sell large, cheap drinks for takeout. Specialty cafes charge more for single-origin beans, desserts and seating.

**How do I ask for the restroom code?**
Check the bottom of your receipt first. If it isn't there, ask 화장실 비밀번호가 뭐예요? (*hwajangsil bimilbeonhoga mwoyeyo?*).
