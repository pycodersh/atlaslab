Noraebang (노래방, "song room") is Korean karaoke: you rent a private room with friends instead of singing in front of strangers. Choose a regular noraebang for groups and parties, or a coin noraebang for a cheap few songs, and search English titles with the remote.

<KeyTakeaways>

- **Regular noraebang:** private rooms paid by the hour, around 15,000–30,000 KRW per room
- **Coin noraebang (코노):** small booths paid per song or by time, from about 500 KRW per song
- **English songs:** search by title or artist in English on the remote or tablet
- **Avoid:** leaving the room a mess; tidy up mics and tambourines before you go

</KeyTakeaways>

## Regular vs coin noraebang

| | Regular noraebang | Coin noraebang (코인노래방) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Room size** | Medium to large rooms for groups | Small booths for 1–3 people |
| **Price** | Per hour per room, roughly 15,000–30,000 KRW | Per song (often 500 KRW, or a few songs per 1,000 KRW) or per time block |
| **Payment** | At the counter | Coins, cards or a kiosk at the booth |
| **Extras** | Tambourines, lighting, often snacks and drinks | Basic setup |
| **Best for** | Parties, groups, after dinner | Solo practice, a quick sing |
| **Staff** | Counter staff, may add bonus time | Often unstaffed |

## Using the remote

| Button | Korean | What it does |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Search by title** | 제목 | Type the song title; switch to English input for English songs |
| **Search by artist** | 가수 | Type the artist name |
| **Reserve** | 예약 | Add a song to the queue by its number |
| **Priority reserve** | 우선예약 | Move a song to the front of the queue |
| **Start / cancel** | 시작 · 취소 | Play or stop a song |
| **Key and tempo** | 키 · 템포 | Change the pitch or speed |

Many places now use a touchscreen tablet instead of a remote, with an English menu option.

<Callout type="tip" title="Bonus time">
At regular noraebang, staff often add free extra minutes (서비스, *seobiseu*) near the end of your time, especially if you're friendly or it's quiet. Don't be surprised if the timer jumps up.
</Callout>

## A night out

| Step | What happens |
| :--- | :--- |
| **1. Ask at the counter** | Say how many people and how long |
| **2. Get your room** | Staff show you the room and the remote |
| **3. Order drinks** | Soft drinks and snacks at many places; alcohol rules vary |
| **4. Sing** | Take turns, cheer, use the tambourines |
| **5. Pay** | Usually up front at regular noraebang |

## Frequently Asked Questions

**How much does noraebang cost in Korea?**
Regular noraebang rooms usually cost around 15,000–30,000 KRW per hour, depending on room size and area. Coin noraebang starts at about 500 KRW per song.

**Can I sing English songs at noraebang?**
Yes. Song lists include many English pop songs; search by title or artist in English on the remote or tablet.

**What is coin noraebang?**
A small booth where you pay per song or per time block, often with coins or a card. It's cheap and popular for solo singers and pairs.

**Do I need to book ahead?**
Usually not. Just walk in. On Friday and Saturday nights in busy areas, you may need to wait for a room.
