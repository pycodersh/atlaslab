Hotel shuttles save you a taxi fare, but they run on a timetable, and many need a reservation. A few questions at the front desk, ideally the night before, are all it takes.

## The exchange

**A:** 무료 공항 셔틀은 어디서 타죠?  
*(Where do I take the free airport shuttle?)*  
**B:** 정문 바로 앞에서 탑승하시면 됩니다.  
*(You can board right in front of the main entrance.)*  
**A:** 혹시 몇 분마다 출발하나요?  
*(How often does it depart?)*  
**B:** 30분마다 출발하고, 다음 버스는 10분 뒤에 옵니다!  
*(Every 30 minutes — and the next one arrives in 10 minutes!)*

Staff speak in the formal **-습니다 / -시면 됩니다** style. You don't need to match it: polite **-요** endings are always fine. Notice that staff call the shuttle **버스**. In this conversation 셔틀 and 버스 mean the same vehicle.

## Words in the exchange

| Korean | Romanization | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| 무료 | muryo | free of charge |
| 공항 셔틀 | gonghang syeoteul | airport shuttle |
| 정문 | jeongmun | main entrance |
| 탑승하다 | tapseunghada | to board |
| 몇 분마다 | myeot bunmada | every how many minutes |
| ~분 뒤에 | ~bun dwie | in ~ minutes |

**몇 분마다** works for any scheduled bus, ferry or shuttle. **~분 뒤에** is the part to listen for when you need to know how long you'll wait.

## Does it need a reservation?

Some hotel shuttles simply run on a timetable. Others take a set number of guests and need a booking, especially early-morning runs. Ask first:

| Korean | Romanization | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| 공항 셔틀 있어요? | gonghang syeoteul isseoyo? | Is there an airport shuttle? |
| 무료예요? | muryoyeyo? | Is it free? |
| 예약해야 돼요? | yeyakhaeya dwaeyo? | Do I need to book it? |
| 내일 아침 7시 셔틀로 예약해 주세요. | naeil achim ilgop-si syeoteullo yeyakhae juseyo | Please book me on the 7 a.m. shuttle tomorrow. |
| 첫차는 몇 시예요? | cheotchaneun myeot siyeyo? | What time is the first one? |
| 막차는 몇 시예요? | makchaneun myeot siyeyo? | What time is the last one? |

**첫차** and **막차** literally mean "first car" and "last car", and are used for any scheduled transport. The hour uses native numbers: 7시 is **일곱 시**.

## What staff will ask you

| You'll hear | Meaning | Answer like this |
|---|---|---|
| 예약하셨어요? | Do you have a booking? | 네, 7시로 했어요. |
| 방 번호가 어떻게 되세요? | What's your room number? | 1204호예요. (천이백사 호) |
| 성함이 어떻게 되세요? | May I have your name? | [name]이에요 / 예요. |
| 몇 분이세요? | How many people? | 두 명이에요. |
| 짐이 몇 개세요? | How many bags? | 캐리어 두 개요. |
| 로비에서 기다려 주세요. | Please wait in the lobby. | 네, 감사합니다. |

**캐리어** is the everyday word for a rolling suitcase.

## Arriving at the airport

The same shuttle often runs the other way. Hotels usually tell you the pickup stop number when you book. At Incheon, hotel shuttles generally leave from the bus stops outside the arrivals floor, but the exact stop differs by hotel and terminal. Confirm it before you fly.

- **[호텔 이름] 셔틀은 어디서 타요?** — Where do I catch the [hotel] shuttle?
- **몇 번 정류장이에요?** — Which stop number is it?
- **도착했는데, 셔틀 언제 와요?** — I've arrived. When does the shuttle come? (on the phone to the hotel)

<Callout type="tip" title="Book the night before">

Ask at the desk the evening before you fly: **내일 아침 공항 셔틀 시간이 어떻게 되나요?** ("What are tomorrow morning's shuttle times?") Pick a run that leaves you a buffer: shuttles stop for traffic, and the drive to Incheon can be long.

</Callout>

## If there's no shuttle

- **Airport bus:** **공항버스 어디서 타요?** — Where do I catch the airport bus?
- **AREX:** **공항철도 어디서 타요?** — Where do I catch the airport railroad?
- **Taxi:** **공항까지 택시로 얼마나 걸려요?** — How long is it to the airport by taxi?
- **Ask for help:** **공항버스 예약해 주실 수 있어요?** — Could you book the airport bus for me?

## What to actually say

- When you book: **공항 셔틀 예약해야 돼요?**
- The night before: **내일 아침 셔틀 시간이 어떻게 되나요?**
- Morning of: **무료 공항 셔틀은 어디서 타죠?**
- Waiting: **다음 버스는 몇 분 뒤에 와요?**
