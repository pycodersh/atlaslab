Korean convenience stores (CU, GS25, 7-Eleven, Emart24) work as 24-hour budget diners, with hot water, microwaves and a counter to eat at. Pick a meal from the shelves, try one of the classic combos below, and clean up your spot when you're done.

<KeyTakeaways>

- **A full meal for about 5,000–8,000 KRW:** dosirak, gimbap, ramyeon or a combo
- **Equipment:** hot water dispenser, microwave and an eating counter in most stores
- **Rule:** pay first, then open and heat your food
- **Avoid:** leaving broth or rubbish behind; sort it before you go

</KeyTakeaways>

## What to buy for a real meal

| Food | Korean | Rough price | Note |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Boxed lunch** | 도시락 (*dosirak*) | 4,500–6,500 KRW | Rice, meat and sides; microwave it |
| **Triangle gimbap** | 삼각김밥 | 1,200–2,000 KRW | Tuna mayo is the classic |
| **Cup ramyeon** | 컵라면 | 1,000–2,000 KRW | Hot water from the dispenser |
| **Sandwiches and burgers** | 샌드위치 · 버거 | 2,500–4,500 KRW | Microwave burgers briefly |
| **Fried chicken pieces** | 치킨 | Varies | Hot cabinet by the counter in many stores |
| **Fruit and salad cups** | 과일 · 샐러드 | 3,000–5,000 KRW | For a lighter meal |

## Three classic combos

### The Commuter Classic (Cup Ramyun + Tuna Mayo Triangular Kimbap)

Eat the noodles first, then drop the triangle gimbap into the remaining hot soup. The mayonnaise balances out spicy broths like Shin Ramyun.

### The Mark Meal Hack (Spicy Stir-Fried Noodles + String Cheese + Sausage Stick)

Drain spicy stir-fry cup noodles (Buldak or tteokbokki style), slice a sausage stick into the bowl, tear string cheese over the top, and microwave for about 40 seconds until melted.

### The Ice Cup Beverage (Sealed Pouch + Clear Plastic Ice Cup)

Take a cup of ice from the freezer case, pick a coffee, iced tea or ade pouch from the shelf, pay at the counter, and pour the pouch over the ice.

## Using the equipment

| Equipment | How | Tip |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Hot water dispenser** | Fill to the line inside the cup, close the lid, wait 3–4 minutes | Some stores also have a ramyeon cooker |
| **Microwave** | Follow the label time for the wattage | Store microwaves are usually 1,000W; use the shorter time |
| **Eating counter** | Eat at the window counter or tables | Outdoor tables in summer at many stores |

<Callout type="tip" title="Cleaning up">
Pour leftover broth into the liquid or food-waste bin, put plastic cups and trays in recycling, and throw wrappers and chopsticks in general waste. Wipe the counter before you leave.
</Callout>

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Can I eat inside Korean convenience stores?**
Most have a counter or a few tables, and many add outdoor seating in warm months. Very small branches may not.

**Are convenience store meals healthy?**
Options range from ramyeon to salads, fruit cups and boiled eggs. Dosirak boxed lunches are the most balanced choice.

**Do convenience stores accept foreign cards?**
Yes, Visa and Mastercard are widely accepted, and contactless payment works at most counters.

**What does 1+1 mean?**
Buy one, get one free. 2+1 means buy two and get a third free. Take the free item before paying.
