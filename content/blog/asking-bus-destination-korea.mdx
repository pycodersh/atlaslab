You're at the bus stop, a bus pulls up, and you're not sure it's the right one. This is the moment most visitors freeze. One sentence fixes it.

## The exchange

**A:** 기사님, 이거 명동역 가나요?  
*(Excuse me, does this go to Myeongdong Station?)*  
**B:** 네, 명동 가요. 얼른 타요!  
*(Yes, it goes to Myeongdong. Hop on quick!)*  
**A:** 네, 감사합니다!  
*(Okay, thank you!)*  
**B:** 손잡이 꼭 잡으세요!  
*(Make sure to hold onto the handrail tight!)*

<YouTube id="v0AhiHTP9Cg" />

**기사님** (*gisanim*) is the polite way to address any driver: bus, taxi or delivery. **얼른 타요!** ("hop on quick!") is efficient, not rude: when you hear it, move. Seoul buses pull away fast, which is why the driver adds **손잡이 꼭 잡으세요** ("hold on tight").

## The pattern

**기사님, 이거 [stop] 가나요?** (*gisanim, igeo … ganayo?*)

| Korean | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 홍대입구역 가나요? | Does this go to Hongik University Station? |
| 동대문 가나요? | Does this go to Dongdaemun? |
| 인사동 가나요? | Does this go to Insadong? |

## What the driver will say

| You'll hear | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 네, 가요. 타세요. | Yes, it does. Get on. |
| 안 가요. | It doesn't go there. |
| 건너편에서 타세요. | Take it from across the street. |
| 100번 타세요. | Take bus 100. |

If the answer is no, follow up with **몇 번 버스 타요?** (*myeot beon beoseu tayo?*, "which bus number do I take?"). Drivers often answer by pointing.

## Words to know

| Korean | Romanization | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| 정류장 | jeongnyujang | bus stop |
| 손잡이 | sonjabi | handrail |
| 하차 벨 | hacha bel | stop button |
| 다음 정류장 | daeum jeongnyujang | next stop |

## Paying and getting off

Tap a T-money or other transit card on the reader by the driver as you board. Many Seoul buses no longer take cash. Tap again on the reader by the back door when you get off: that's how the transfer discount to another bus or the subway is worked out.

Press the stop button before your stop. Many routes announce stops in Korean and English. If you miss yours, say **다음 정류장에서 내릴게요** (*daeum jeongnyujangeseo naerilgeyo*, "I'll get off at the next stop").

## Polite or casual?

Drivers often mix polite and casual speech (**얼른 타요**, **잡아요**) because they're busy. Keep using polite **-요** yourself, with **기사님** in front.

## What to actually say

- At the door: **기사님, 이거 명동 가나요?**
- Wrong bus: **몇 번 버스 타요?**
- Missed your stop: **다음 정류장에서 내릴게요.**
