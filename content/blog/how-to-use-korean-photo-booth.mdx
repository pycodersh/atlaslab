You walk into a bright booth, see a big touchscreen, and have no idea what to press first. There's no staff to ask: it's just you, your friends and the machine.

## The exchange

**A:** 이거 어떻게 사용하는 거야?  
*(How do you use this?)*  
**B:** 여기서 먼저 배경을 고르면 돼.  
*(First pick a background here.)*  
**A:** 아, 이걸 누르면 돼?  
*(Oh, I just press this?)*  
**B:** 응, 그다음에 촬영 버튼 누르면 사진 찍혀.  
*(Yeah, then press the shutter button and it takes the photo.)*

This is two friends talking, so it's casual speech (반말). **-면 돼** ("you just ~") is the easy way to explain each step.

## Words on the screen

| Korean | Romanization | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| 배경 | baegyeong | background |
| 촬영 | chwaryeong | shooting |
| 꾸미기 | kkumigi | decorating |
| 인화 / 출력 | inhwa / chullyeok | printing |
| 다시 찍기 | dasi jjikgi | retake |
| 매수 | maesu | number of copies |

## How it works

1. **결제** (*gyeolje*): pay at the start, by card or cash. It usually costs a few thousand won.
2. **배경 고르기**: pick a frame and background. [Picking a background](/blog/en/k-patto/picking-background-korean-photo-booth) covers the options and the arguments.
3. **촬영**: a countdown runs, then the camera takes a series of shots with short pauses between them.
4. **사진 선택**: pick which shots go on the strip.
5. **꾸미기**: add stickers or text if the booth offers it. A timer runs in the corner.
6. **인화**: the strip prints from a slot. Many booths also show a QR code to download the photos.

## What the machine will say

| You'll see or hear | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 결제 방법을 선택해 주세요. | Choose a payment method. |
| 3, 2, 1! | Countdown before each shot |
| 사진을 선택해 주세요. | Choose your photos. |
| 출력 중입니다. | Printing. |
| QR코드를 찍어 주세요. | Scan the QR code. |

The machine uses formal **-습니다** and **-주세요**, like any announcement.

## Asking a friend, or the shop

| Korean | Romanization | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| 이거 어떻게 해요? | igeo eotteoke haeyo? | How does this work? |
| 다시 찍을 수 있어요? | dasi jjigeul su isseoyo? | Can we retake it? |
| 몇 장 나와요? | myeot jang nawayo? | How many prints come out? |
| 사진이 안 나와요. | sajini an nawayo | The photo isn't coming out. |

If a print doesn't come out, look for a support number or QR code posted in the booth.

## Polite or casual?

| Casual (friends) | Polite (a stranger, staff) | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| 이거 어떻게 해? | 이거 어떻게 해요? | How does this work? |
| 이거 누르면 돼? | 이거 누르면 돼요? | Do I press this? |

## What to actually say

- Starting: **이거 어떻게 사용하는 거야?**
- Retake: **다시 찍자!**
- After: **잘 나왔다!** ("it came out well!")

Wait by the slot until the strip drops. It's easy to walk off without it.
