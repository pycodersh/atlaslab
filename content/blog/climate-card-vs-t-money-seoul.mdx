Most visitors need one of three cards: T-money (pay per ride, works nationwide), WOWPASS (a prepaid payment card with T-money built in), or the Climate Card (unlimited rides in Seoul for a fixed number of days). Use the comparison below, then the quick quiz to pick yours.

<KeyTakeaways>

- **Simplest:** T-money, sold at any convenience store, works on subways and buses across Korea
- **Shopping plus transit:** WOWPASS, a prepaid card for payments with a transit function
- **Heavy Seoul use:** the Climate Card short-term pass (1–7 days) for unlimited city rides
- **Avoid:** using the Climate Card for the Incheon Airport train; it isn't covered

</KeyTakeaways>

## The three cards compared

| | T-money | WOWPASS | Climate Card (short-term) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **What it is** | Prepaid transit card | Prepaid payment card + T-money transit | Unlimited-ride pass for Seoul |
| **Where to buy** | Convenience stores, station machines | WOWPASS kiosks (airport, Myeongdong, some hotels and stations) | Station machines and customer centers in Seoul |
| **Card cost** | About 3,000–5,000 KRW | Issued at the kiosk (fees vary) | About 3,000 KRW, plus the pass |
| **Pass or fare** | Pay per ride | Pay per ride (transit part) | Roughly 5,000 KRW (1 day) to 20,000 KRW (7 days) |
| **Top-up** | Mostly cash at stores and station machines | Payment balance: exchange cash at the kiosk; transit part topped up separately, usually with cash | Buy or renew the pass at station machines |
| **Cash needed?** | Usually yes | For the transit part, usually yes | Often yes at machines; card payment is spreading |
| **Coverage** | Subways, buses and taxis nationwide | Same as T-money for transit; card payments in shops | Seoul subways and buses (some lines and sections excluded) |
| **Refunds** | Leftover balance at stores or stations, small fee | Payment balance at kiosks, with a fee | Passes are non-refundable once started |

Rules, prices and coverage change, so check the official site or station machine before buying.

<Callout type="warn" title="Airport trips">
The Climate Card doesn't cover the Incheon Airport stations. Use T-money or a separate ticket for the airport train or bus, then switch to the Climate Card in the city.
</Callout>

## Which card fits your trip?

| Your trip | Best pick | Why |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| A few days, a few rides a day | **T-money** | Cheap card, no planning, works everywhere |
| Lots of shopping, little cash | **WOWPASS** | One card for payments and transit |
| Seoul only, many rides a day | **Climate Card** | Unlimited rides make heavy days cheap |
| Day trips beyond Seoul | **T-money** | The Climate Card stops at the city edge |
| Long stay or living here | **T-money**, or a Korean card later | Simple now, upgrade later |

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Can I top up T-money with a credit card?**
Mostly no. Station machines and convenience stores generally take cash for top-ups, so keep some won on hand.

**Does WOWPASS replace T-money?**
For many visitors, yes: it works as a transit card and a prepaid payment card. Remember the transit balance is topped up separately from the payment balance.

**Is the Climate Card worth it?**
If you'll take several subway or bus rides a day inside Seoul, it can save money. For light use, T-money is cheaper.

**Can I use T-money in other cities?**
Yes. T-money works on subways and buses in Busan, Daegu and most other cities, and in many taxis.
