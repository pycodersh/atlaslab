You've worked out how the machine works. Now comes the harder part: agreeing on a background with your friend while the timer runs.

## The exchange

**A:** 이 배경 어때? 난 이게 맘에 들어.  
*(What do you think of this background? I like this one.)*  
**B:** 너무 촌스럽지 않아? 난 이 배경이 더 나은 것 같은데.  
*(Isn't it too tacky? I think this one looks better.)*  
**A:** 응, 네가 고른 게 더 귀엽고 자연스러운 것 같아.  
*(Yeah, the one you picked looks cuter and more natural.)*  
**B:** 그래, 그럼 이 배경으로 사진 찍자!  
*(Okay, let's take the photo with this background then!)*

**맘에 들어** means "I like it"; 맘 is the short form of 마음. **촌스럽다** (*chonseureopda*) is the go-to word for something tacky or dated. **-ㄴ 것 같은데** ("I think ~, though") softens a disagreement, and **-자** ("let's") settles it.

## Words to know

| Korean | Romanization | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| 배경 | baegyeong | background |
| 촌스럽다 | chonseureopda | tacky, dated |
| 귀엽다 | gwiyeopda | cute |
| 자연스럽다 | jayeonseureopda | natural |
| 맘에 들다 | mame deulda | to like |
| 고르다 | goreuda | to choose |

## The options

| Korean | Romanization | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| 단색 | dansaek | plain colors |
| 계절 테마 | gyejeol tema | seasonal themes |
| 콜라보 | kollabo | character or brand collaborations |
| 프레임 | peureim | the frame around the strip |

Plain white, beige and pastels are the safe choices. Seasonal and collaboration frames change often.

## What you'll hear from your friend

| You'll hear | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 이게 더 예쁘지 않아? | Isn't this one prettier? |
| 이건 좀 별로야. | This one's not great. |
| 아무거나 괜찮아. | Anything's fine. |
| 시간 없어, 빨리 골라! | No time, hurry and pick! |

The last one comes up a lot: the selection screen has a timer, and it runs out faster than you think.

## When you can't decide

- **그냥 이걸로 하자.** (*geunyang igeollo haja*) Let's just go with this one.
- **랜덤으로 하자.** (*raendeomeuro haja*) Let's just go random.
- **다음엔 네가 골라.** (*daeumen nega golla*) You pick next time.

## Polite or casual?

| Casual (friends) | Polite | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| 이거 어때? | 이거 어때요? | How about this one? |
| 맘에 들어. | 마음에 들어요. | I like it. |
| 이걸로 하자. | 이걸로 해요. | Let's go with this. |

You'll almost always do this with friends, so casual is natural.

## What to actually say

- Suggesting: **이 배경 어때?**
- Disagreeing gently: **난 이게 더 나은 것 같은데.**
- Deciding: **그냥 이걸로 하자!**
