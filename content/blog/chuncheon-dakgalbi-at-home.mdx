Dakgalbi (닭갈비) is spicy marinated chicken stir-fried with cabbage, sweet potato and rice cakes on a huge iron pan at your table, a specialty of Chuncheon in Gangwon Province. Order by the number of people, let the staff stir it for you, and save room for fried rice made in the same pan.

<KeyTakeaways>

- **What it is:** chicken in gochujang marinade, stir-fried with vegetables and rice cakes
- **Where:** Chuncheon's famous dakgalbi street, plus chains all over Korea
- **Price:** around 13,000–16,000 KRW per serving, usually a two-serving minimum
- **Avoid:** skipping the fried rice at the end; it's the best part for many diners

</KeyTakeaways>

## Pan-fried vs charcoal dakgalbi

| | Iron pan (철판) dakgalbi | Charcoal (숯불) dakgalbi |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **How it's cooked** | Stir-fried on a large round iron pan at the table | Grilled piece by piece over charcoal |
| **What's in it** | Chicken, cabbage, sweet potato, rice cakes, perilla leaves | Marinated chicken only, sometimes with skin |
| **Sauce** | Thick, spicy gochujang marinade | Spicy, soy or salt options |
| **Finish** | Fried rice or noodles in the same pan | Usually none |
| **Where** | Most dakgalbi restaurants and chains | Specialist shops, more common in Chuncheon |

Despite the name, dakgalbi isn't made from chicken ribs; it's usually boneless thigh meat. Most visitors know the iron-pan version, which is what you'll find in Seoul and at chains across the country.

## How a dakgalbi meal works

When you sit down, staff bring a big pan of raw marinated chicken and vegetables and turn on the burner. They'll usually stir it with large spatulas until it's cooked, then tell you when it's ready. You can add extras like cheese, udon noodles or more rice cakes partway through. When most of the chicken is gone, order fried rice (볶음밥): staff toss rice, seaweed and sesame oil into the remaining sauce and press it flat to crisp.

- **Add-ons:** cheese, udon (우동 사리), rice cakes, sweet potato
- **Fried rice:** usually priced per portion, ordered near the end
- **Wraps:** some places give lettuce or perilla leaves for wrapping chicken

<Callout type="tip" title="Let staff stir">
The pan is very hot and the sauce burns quickly. Staff usually do the stirring; if they hand you the spatula, keep everything moving and turn the heat down when it starts to stick.
</Callout>

## Making it at home without the griddle

You don't need a giant iron pan. A wide frying pan or cast-iron skillet works well.

1. **Marinate** bite-sized chicken thigh in gochujang, gochugaru, soy sauce, garlic, sugar and a little curry powder for 30 minutes or more.
2. **Fry** cabbage, sliced sweet potato and onion in a little oil.
3. **Add the chicken and sauce**, then rice cakes, and stir-fry over medium heat until cooked.
4. **Finish** with perilla leaves and green onion.
5. **Make fried rice** in the leftover sauce with rice, chopped kimchi and seaweed.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Is dakgalbi spicy?**
Usually medium-spicy from gochujang. Ask for less spice, or add cheese to mellow it.

**Why is dakgalbi famous in Chuncheon?**
It grew popular in Chuncheon decades ago as an affordable chicken dish, and the city now has a whole street of dakgalbi restaurants.

**Can I order dakgalbi for one person?**
Many restaurants need two servings, but some offer single portions or lunch sets.

**What is the fried rice at the end?**
Rice, seaweed and sesame oil stir-fried in the leftover sauce and pressed into the pan to crisp.
