You get in the back seat, close the door, and the driver glances at the mirror and asks you something. It's always the same question, and it's the easiest one to prepare for.

## The exchange

**A:** 어디로 가시나요?  
*(Where are you headed?)*  
**B:** 명동으로 가주세요. 조금 서둘러 주세요.  
*(To Myeongdong, please. Could you hurry a little?)*

Put any place in front of **가 주세요** (*ga juseyo*, "please go") and it's a complete sentence. **조금** ("a little") keeps the request to hurry polite.

## What the driver will say

| You'll hear | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 어디로 가세요? / 어디까지 가세요? | Where to? |
| 안전벨트 매 주세요. | Please fasten your seatbelt. |
| 어디서 세워 드릴까요? | Where shall I stop? |
| 다 왔습니다. | We're here. |

## Words to know

| Korean | Romanization | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| ~로 가 주세요 | ~ro ga juseyo | please go to ~ |
| 여기로 가 주세요 | yeogiro ga juseyo | please go here |
| 서둘러 주세요 | seodulleo juseyo | please hurry |
| 세워 주세요 | sewo juseyo | please stop |
| 늦었어요 | neujeosseoyo | I'm late |

## When you can't pronounce the destination

This is the real problem for visitors, and one phrase solves it: **여기로 가 주세요** ("please go here"), said while holding up your phone with the place on a map. Drivers see this all the time and will often type it into their navigation themselves.

The Korean name on screen works much better than a romanized address, which many drivers find hard to read. Copy it from Naver Map or Kakao Map before you hail.

## Small talk

Drivers sometimes chat. It's friendly, not nosy.

| You'll hear | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 어디서 오셨어요? | Where are you from? |
| 한국 처음이세요? | First time in Korea? |
| 약속 있으세요? | Meeting someone? |

A short answer and a smile is plenty, or just **네**. Neither is rude.

## Getting out

**여기서 세워 주세요** (*yeogiseo sewo juseyo*, "please stop here") or **저기 앞에서 세워 주세요** ("stop just up there"). Say it a little before you arrive: drivers can't stop just anywhere. The doors aren't automatic, so open and close them yourself. [Paying for your taxi](/blog/en/k-patto/paying-for-taxi-korea) covers the last minute of the ride.

## Polite or casual?

Drivers speak to you in polite or formal Korean. Use **-요** back: **명동으로 가 주세요**. Calling the driver **기사님** (*gisanim*) is the respectful way to address them.

## What to actually say

- Destination: **명동으로 가 주세요.**
- Hard to pronounce: **여기로 가 주세요.** (show your phone)
- In a hurry: **조금 서둘러 주세요.**
- Stopping: **여기서 세워 주세요.**
