Korean convenience stores aren't just places to buy things. Many have a counter by the window, a hot water dispenser, a microwave, and people eating at ten at night. Cup ramen costs a couple of thousand won and makes a real meal, and the whole thing runs on two questions.

## The exchange

**A:** 여기서 먹고 가도 돼요?  
*(Can I eat here?)*  
**B:** 네, 젓가락도 가져가세요.  
*(Sure, and take chopsticks too.)*  
**A:** 뜨거운 물은 어디 있어요?  
*(Where's the hot water?)*  
**B:** 전자레인지 옆에 있어요.  
*(It's next to the microwave.)*

You don't strictly have to ask before sitting down; people just do. But **여기서 먹고 가도 돼요?** (*yeogiseo meokgo gado dwaeyo?*) tells you whether this branch has seats, and it's the polite move.

## Words to know

| Korean | Romanization | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| 뜨거운 물 | tteugeoun mul | hot water |
| 전자레인지 | jeonjareinji | microwave |
| 젓가락 | jeotgarak | chopsticks |
| 뚜껑 | ttukkeong | lid |
| 국물 | gungmul | broth |

## What staff will say

| You'll hear | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 젓가락 가져가세요. | Take some chopsticks. |
| 뜨거운 물은 저쪽에 있어요. | The hot water is over there. |
| 데워 드릴까요? | Shall I heat it up? |
| 국물은 저기 버리시면 돼요. | You can pour the broth over there. |

## The thing nobody tells you

**Cup ramen doesn't come with chopsticks.** There's a bin of disposable ones near the counter or the microwave. Grab a pair before you pour the water, or you'll be watching your noodles cook with nothing to eat them with.

## The steps

1. Pay for your cup ramen at the counter.
2. Take chopsticks.
3. Peel the lid back to the marked line, not all the way off.
4. Fill with hot water to the line inside the cup.
5. Close the lid and wait the time printed on it, usually three or four minutes.
6. Eat at the window counter.

## What people add

The store is also a pantry, and people build on the cup: a slice of **치즈** (cheese) melted into spicy ramen, a **삼각김밥** (triangle kimbap) dipped in the broth, or microwaved **만두** (dumplings) dropped in.

## Cleaning up

Look for a bin with a hole or a strainer for liquid: the leftover broth goes there, the cup in the trash. If you can't find it, ask **국물은 어디에 버려요?** (*gungmureun eodie beoryeoyo?*, "where do I pour the broth?").

## Which ones to try

| Korean | What it is |
|---|---|
| 신라면 | the default: spicy, beef broth |
| 진라면 | milder, sold as 매운맛 (spicy) and 순한맛 (mild) |
| 짜파게티 | black bean noodles, no soup |
| 불닭볶음면 | very spicy stir-fried noodles |
| 육개장 | thin noodles in a beef broth, a classic cup |

For something without chili, ask **안 매운 거 있어요?** ("is there something not spicy?").

## Polite or casual?

| Polite (staff) | Casual (friends) | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| 여기서 먹고 가도 돼요? | 여기서 먹고 갈까? | Shall we eat here? |
| 뜨거운 물 어디 있어요? | 뜨거운 물 어디 있어? | Where's the hot water? |

## What to actually say

- Seats: **여기서 먹고 가도 돼요?**
- Hot water: **뜨거운 물은 어디 있어요?**
- Broth: **국물은 어디에 버려요?**
