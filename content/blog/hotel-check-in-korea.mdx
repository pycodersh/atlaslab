You walk up to the front desk, and the staff smiles and asks one question. Everything after that follows a short, predictable script.

## The exchange

**A:** 어서 오세요! 체크인 도와드릴까요?  
*(Welcome! May I help you check in?)*  
**B:** 네, 체크인 부탁드립니다.  
*(Yes, I'd like to check in, please.)*  
**A:** 예약자분 성함 알려주시겠어요?  
*(May I have your name for the reservation?)*  
**B:** 김석진으로 예약했어요.  
*(Under "Kim Seokjin.")*  
**A:** 네, 확인되었습니다!  
*(Perfect, you're all set!)*

**성함** (*seongham*) is the honorific word for "name", used for guests; the everyday word is 이름. **부탁드립니다** is a formal "please", just right for a front desk.

## The one sentence you need

**[name]으로 예약했어요** (*… euro yeyakaesseoyo*, "I booked under [name]"). Say your name, add this, and staff will find the booking. If your name is hard to say in Korean, show the confirmation on your phone.

## What staff will ask you

| You'll hear | Meaning | Answer |
|---|---|---|
| 예약하셨어요? | Do you have a reservation? | 네, [name]으로 예약했어요. |
| 성함이 어떻게 되세요? | May I have your name? | [name]이에요. |
| 여권 보여 주시겠어요? | May I see your passport? | 네, 여기요. |
| 보증금 결제 도와드릴게요. | I'll take the deposit. | 네. |
| 조식 포함이세요. | Breakfast is included. | 아, 감사합니다. |

Have your **여권** (*yeogwon*, passport) out before they ask. Many hotels also put a refundable deposit (**보증금**, *bojeunggeum*) on a card.

## Questions worth asking

| Korean | Romanization | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| 체크아웃 몇 시예요? | chekeuaut myeot siyeyo? | What time is checkout? |
| 와이파이 비밀번호가 뭐예요? | waipai bimilbeonhoga mwoyeyo? | What's the Wi-Fi password? |
| 조식은 몇 시부터예요? | josigeun myeot sibuteoyeyo? | What time does breakfast start? |
| 엘리베이터 어디 있어요? | ellibeiteo eodi isseoyo? | Where's the elevator? |

## Arriving late

If you'll arrive late, message through the booking app or call: **오늘 늦게 도착할 것 같아요** (*oneul neutge dochakal geot gatayo*, "I think I'll arrive late today"). Larger hotels usually have a 24-hour desk; small guesthouses and hanok stays often don't.

## Polite or casual?

Front desk staff use formal **-습니다** and honorifics (**확인되었습니다**, **도와드릴까요?**). Polite **-요** is all you need back. **부탁드립니다** is a nice formal touch if you want to match them.

## What to actually say

- Arriving: **체크인 부탁드립니다.**
- Your booking: **[name]으로 예약했어요.**
- The passport: **네, 여기요.**
- Before going up: **와이파이 비밀번호가 뭐예요?**
