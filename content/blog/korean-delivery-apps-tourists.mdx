Food delivery is everywhere in Korea, and it reaches parks, hotels and apartments within about 30–60 minutes. Tourists can use it if the app accepts their card and phone number, so check the comparison below and set up the app before you're hungry.

<KeyTakeaways>

- **Most tourist-friendly:** Baemin, which has added English support and foreign card options
- **Phone number:** many apps need a number for verification or driver contact
- **Delivery fee:** usually a few thousand won, with minimum order amounts
- **Avoid:** ordering to a park without choosing the marked delivery zone

</KeyTakeaways>

## The apps compared

| App | English | Foreign cards | Korean number needed? | Best for |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Baemin (배달의민족)** | Yes, in recent versions | Often accepted | Sometimes, for verification | The widest restaurant choice |
| **Coupang Eats (쿠팡이츠)** | Partial | Hit-and-miss | Usually | Fast single deliveries |
| **Yogiyo (요기요)** | Limited | Rarely | Usually | Residents with Korean accounts |
| **English-language services** | Yes | Yes | No | Visitors who want no hassle, at higher prices |

App features change often, so check the current app store listing before relying on any one app.

## What to order

Almost every kind of Korean food delivers, and some dishes are practically designed for it. These are the classics locals order at home, at the office and in parks.

| Dish | Korean | Why it delivers well | Rough price |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Fried chicken** | 치킨 | Stays crispy; the classic night-in order | 20,000–30,000 KRW |
| **Jajangmyeon** | 짜장면 | Black-bean noodles, the original delivery food | 7,000–10,000 KRW |
| **Tteokbokki set** | 떡볶이 | Comes with fried snacks and sundae | 15,000–25,000 KRW |
| **Jokbal and bossam** | 족발 · 보쌈 | Braised or boiled pork with wraps, good for groups | 30,000–45,000 KRW |
| **Pizza** | 피자 | Korean-style toppings like sweet potato and bulgogi | 20,000–35,000 KRW |

Delivery orders usually come in disposable containers with chopsticks and side dishes; tick the "no cutlery" option if you don't need them.

## Ordering, step by step

| Step | What to do | Tip |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. Set the address** | Enter your hotel or pin the location | Add the building name, floor and room number |
| **2. Choose a restaurant** | Filter by category; check minimum order and delivery fee | Ratings and photos help a lot |
| **3. Customize** | Pick options such as spice level or extra rice | Look for 덜 맵게 (less spicy) options |
| **4. Pay** | Card or the app's payment methods | Some foreign cards fail; keep a backup card |
| **5. Receive** | The rider calls, texts or leaves it at the door | Choose "leave at door" if you can't take calls |

<Callout type="tip" title="Ordering to a park or hotel">
At Han River parks, choose the marked delivery zone (배달존) as your address and meet the rider there. At hotels, check whether riders can come up or you need to collect at the lobby.
</Callout>

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Can tourists use Baemin?**
Often yes, especially with recent English support and foreign card options. Some features may still need a Korean phone number.

**How long does delivery take?**
Usually 30–60 minutes, longer during dinner peaks and bad weather.

**Is there a delivery fee?**
Most orders have a delivery fee of a few thousand won and a minimum order amount, both shown before you pay.

**What if I don't have a Korean phone number?**
Ask your hotel or host for help, use an English-language delivery service, or pick up food yourself with a takeout order.
