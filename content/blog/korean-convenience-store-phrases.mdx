A Korean convenience store is part shop, part small restaurant: hot water for instant noodles, a microwave, a counter by the window, and enough hot food to make dinner. The Korean you need is minimal, but some of it comes from the staff, fast, while you're holding a basket.

## What staff will ask at the counter

| You'll hear | Meaning | Answer |
|---|---|---|
| 봉투 필요하세요? | Do you need a bag? | 네, 주세요. / 괜찮아요. |
| 데워 드릴까요? | Shall I heat it up? | 네, 데워 주세요. / 괜찮아요. |
| 포인트 적립하세요? | Collecting points? | 괜찮아요. |
| 신분증 보여 주세요. | Please show your ID. | 네, 여기요. |

**괜찮아요** (*gwaenchanayo*, "it's fine") answers most of these. Bags cost a little extra, which is why staff ask: [do you need a bag?](/blog/en/k-patto/need-a-bag-korean-checkout) walks through the whole checkout. Buying beer or soju means an ID check, covered in [why staff ask for your ID](/blog/en/k-patto/show-id-buying-alcohol-korea).

## Instant noodles

| Korean | Romanization | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| 뜨거운 물 어디 있어요? | tteugeoun mul eodi isseoyo? | Where's the hot water? |
| 여기서 먹어도 돼요? | yeogiseo meogeodo dwaeyo? | Can I eat here? |
| 젓가락 어디 있어요? | jeotgarak eodi isseoyo? | Where are the chopsticks? |

Hot water, chopsticks and the window seats are free for customers. The full routine, including where the leftover broth goes, is in [how to eat cup ramen](/blog/en/k-patto/cup-ramen-convenience-store-korea).

## Choosing something

**이걸로 할게요** (*igeollo halgeyo*, "I'll go with this one") is what Koreans say when they've been comparing and finally decide. It sounds more natural than **이거 주세요** at that moment.

## The signs: 1+1 and 2+1

**1+1** means bring two, pay for one. **2+1** means bring three, pay for two. Koreans read the numbers in English: *won peulleoseu won*. If you bring only one of a 1+1 drink, staff will often tell you to grab another, but not always. [Buy one, get one free](/blog/en/k-patto/one-plus-one-convenience-store-korea) explains mixing flavors.

## On the shelves

| Korean | What it is |
|---|---|
| 도시락 | lunch box meal |
| 삼각김밥 | triangle kimbap |
| 컵라면 | cup noodles |
| 바나나우유 | banana milk |
| 이온음료 | sports drink |
| 숙취해소음료 | hangover drink |

**숙취해소음료** is a very Korean product category, and it usually sits right by the register.

## Polite or casual?

| Polite (staff) | Casual (friends) | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| 이걸로 할게요. | 나 이거 살래. | I'll get this one. |
| 여기서 먹어도 돼요? | 여기서 먹고 가자. | Let's eat here. |
| 괜찮아요. | 됐어. | No thanks. |

## A full visit

> **You:** (at the counter) 이걸로 할게요.  
> **Staff:** 봉투 필요하세요?  
> **You:** 아니요, 괜찮아요.  
> **Staff:** 데워 드릴까요?  
> **You:** 네, 데워 주세요. 여기서 먹어도 돼요?  
> **Staff:** 네, 저기 자리 있어요.
