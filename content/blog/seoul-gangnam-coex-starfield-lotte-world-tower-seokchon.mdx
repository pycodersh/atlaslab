South of the Han River, Seoul turns glossy: flagship stores in Apgujeong, a giant library inside the COEX mall, and Korea's tallest building rising over a lake in Jamsil. This route moves west to east, ending at Seokchon Lake as the tower lights up.

<KeyTakeaways title="At a glance">

- **The route:** Apgujeong Rodeo Street → Starfield Library (COEX) → Lotte World Tower & Seoul Sky → Seokchon Lake
- **Getting around:** subway or taxi between the first three, then on foot from the tower to the lake
- **Cost:** Starfield Library and the lake are free; Seoul Sky needs a ticket
- **Best timing:** Seoul Sky in late afternoon, then the lake at sunset
- **Avoid:** turning up at Seoul Sky without a booked time slot on weekends

</KeyTakeaways>

## Apgujeong Rodeo Street

Apgujeong Rodeo Street and the lanes around it are Gangnam's upmarket shopping district: flagship fashion stores, K-beauty clinics, designer cafes and dessert shops. It sits a little apart from the rest of this route, so go first, in the late morning, while the shops are quiet.

| | |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Getting there** | Apgujeongrodeo Station (Suin-Bundang Line) |
| **Tip** | Galleria department store is at the station; the side lanes hold the smaller boutiques |

Staying nearby? Our [Andaz Seoul Gangnam review](/blog/en/k-patto/andaz-seoul-gangnam-review) covers the hotel at Apgujeong's front door.

Starfield Library is an open reading space in the middle of the COEX mall, with bookshelves several storeys high under a glass roof. Anyone can sit and read for free; most visitors come for the photos. The rest of COEX, one of Asia's largest underground malls, surrounds it.

| | |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Fee** | Free |
| **Hours** | About 10:30 am to 10 pm daily, according to the Korea Tourism Organization |
| **Getting there** | Connected to Samseong Station (Line 2) and Bongeunsa Station (Line 9) |

The shelves are best photographed from the upper walkway. Mornings are quieter than weekend afternoons.

Lotte World Tower, at 555 metres, is Korea's tallest building. Its observatory, Seoul Sky, fills the top floors from 117 to 123 and includes a glass-floored deck where you look straight down. On a clear day you can see across the whole city to the mountains.

| | |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Getting there** | Connected to Jamsil Station (Lines 2 and 8) via Lotte World Mall |
| **Tickets** | Timed entry; buy online in advance, especially for weekends and sunset slots |
| **Tip** | Check the weather before you book; haze or cloud can hide the view |

Ticket prices differ between the official site and resellers, so compare before you buy. Our [Signiel Seoul review](/blog/en/k-patto/signiel-seoul-review) covers the hotel on the tower's upper floors.

<img src="https://eecvvgkihtcgfikaimao.supabase.co/storage/v1/object/public/blog-covers/seoul-gangnam-coex-starfield-lotte-world-tower-seokchon-seokchon.webp" alt="Lotte World's castle on an island in Seokchon Lake with Lotte World Tower behind" loading="lazy" />

Seokchon Lake lies right beside the tower: two lakes joined by a road, with a walking path around both and the castle of Lotte World's outdoor park on an island. The full loop takes about an hour at an easy pace. In early April the path is lined with cherry blossoms and very busy.

At sunset, the tower lights up and reflects in the water. It's an easy, free end to the day.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Is Starfield Library free?**
Yes. It's an open space in the COEX mall, free for everyone.

**Do I need to book Seoul Sky in advance?**
It's strongly recommended for weekends and sunset slots. Entry is by time slot.

**How long is the walk around Seokchon Lake?**
The full loop around both lakes takes about an hour at an easy pace.

**Is Apgujeong close to COEX?**
Not walking distance. It's a short taxi ride or a subway trip with a transfer.
