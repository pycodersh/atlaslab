You're in the room, the microphones are on the table, and nobody knows how to find the song they want.

The search screen is all in Korean, and the remote has more buttons than you expected.

## The exchange

**A:** BTS 노래 부르고 싶은데 어떻게 찾아?  
*(I want to sing a BTS song, how do I search for it?)*  
**B:** 여기 검색창에 가수 이름 한국어로 치면 돼. 방탄소년단으로.  
*(Type the artist name in Korean in the search bar. 방탄소년단.)*  
**A:** 어, Dynamite 있다! 나 이거 부를래, 너무 좋아.  
*(Oh, Dynamite is here! I want to sing this, I love this song.)*  
**B:** 나도 나도! 빨리 눌러!  
*(Me too, me too! Hurry and press it!)*

> **Key Nuances**
> - **방탄소년단**: The search runs on Korean input — typing BTS finds nothing, 방탄소년단 does.
> - **검색창에 치면 돼**: "Just type it in the search bar" — 치다 is the everyday verb for typing.
> - **부를래**: "I'm going to sing (this)" — -ㄹ래 announces what you want to do, casually.
> - **빨리 눌러!**: "Hurry and press it!" — what everyone shouts once the song is found.

## Words to know

| Korean | Romanization | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| 검색창 | geom-saek-chang | Search bar |
| 가수 | ga-su | Singer, artist |
| 예약 | ye-yak | Queue, reservation |
| 번호 | beon-ho | Number |
| 추가시간 | chu-ga-si-gan | Extra time |
| 탬버린 | taem-beo-rin | Tambourine |

## How norebang works

Korean karaoke is private. You rent a room by the hour, not a spot at a bar. Groups of 2 to 10 people, your own screen and sound system, no audience.

**예약** (*ye-yak*) is the word for queuing a song. When you find the one you want, press it and it gets added to the list. Songs play in order, and anyone can add to the queue.

Most systems let you search by:
- Artist name (가수명) — type in Korean
- Song title (곡명) — English titles usually work in English
- Song number (번호) — faster if you know it, looked up on your phone beforehand

## Asking for more time

Rooms are booked in 30 or 60-minute blocks. When time is almost up, a staff member will knock or an alert appears on screen.

**추가시간 되나요?** — "Can we get extra time?"
*chu-ga-si-gan doe-na-yo?*

Usually yes. They'll tell you the cost and add it on.

## The tambourine

Every norebang room has one. Pick it up. There's no wrong way to use it, and it makes everything better.

If you're not singing, you're on tambourine duty.

## Useful phrases

**이 노래 취소할 수 있어요?** — "Can I cancel this song?"
**소리 좀 줄여주세요** — "Could you turn it down a bit?"
**마이크 볼륨 올려주세요** — "Please turn up the mic volume"
**음료 주문할 수 있어요?** — "Can we order drinks?"

Most norebangs sell snacks and drinks. Some let you bring your own. Ask at the front desk when you check in.

## What to actually do

1. Look up the Korean names of your favorite artists before you go
2. At the search screen, tap 가수명 and type in Korean
3. Find your song, press it, and it joins the queue
4. When time is running low, ask **추가시간 되나요?**
5. Pick up the tambourine

One person who knows how to search makes the whole room run smoothly. After the first song, everyone figures out the rest.
