You're in the room, the microphones are on the table, and nobody knows how to find the song they want. The search screen is in Korean, and the remote has more buttons than you expected.

## The exchange

**A:** BTS 노래 부르고 싶은데 어떻게 찾아?  
*(I want to sing a BTS song, how do I search for it?)*  
**B:** 여기 검색창에 가수 이름 한국어로 치면 돼. 방탄소년단으로.  
*(Type the artist name in Korean in the search bar. 방탄소년단.)*  
**A:** 어, Dynamite 있다! 나 이거 부를래, 너무 좋아.  
*(Oh, Dynamite is here! I want to sing this, I love this song.)*  
**B:** 나도 나도! 빨리 눌러!  
*(Me too, me too! Hurry and press it!)*

Artist searches often run on Korean names, so **방탄소년단** finds BTS where the letters "BTS" may not. **치다** is the everyday verb for typing. **부를래** uses **-ㄹ래**, the casual way to say what you want to do.

## Artist names in Korean

Save these on your phone before you go.

| Artist | Korean name |
|---|---|
| BTS | 방탄소년단 |
| BLACKPINK | 블랙핑크 |
| SEVENTEEN | 세븐틴 |
| Stray Kids | 스트레이 키즈 |
| NewJeans | 뉴진스 |
| IVE | 아이브 |

Songs with English titles, like Dynamite, are often listed in English, so once you're in the artist's list they're easy to spot.

## Words on the screen

| Korean | Romanization | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| 검색 | geomsaek | search |
| 가수명 | gasumyeong | artist name |
| 곡명 | gongmyeong | song title |
| 번호 | beonho | song number |
| 예약 | yeyak | add to the queue |
| 취소 | chwiso | cancel |

Searching by number is the fastest. Many people look the number up on their phone first.

## What you'll hear in the room

| You'll hear | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 다음 곡 뭐야? | What's the next song? |
| 예약했어? | Did you queue it? |
| 이거 취소해. | Cancel this one. |
| 시간 얼마 안 남았어. | There's not much time left. |

At a regular noraebang, when time is nearly up you can ask the counter **시간 추가 돼요?** (*sigan chuga dwaeyo?*, "can we add time?").

## Asking for help with the machine

| Korean | Romanization | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| 이 노래 취소할 수 있어요? | i norae chwisohal su isseoyo? | Can I cancel this song? |
| 마이크 소리 좀 키워 주세요. | maikeu sori jom kiwo juseyo | Please turn the mic up. |
| 음료 주문할 수 있어요? | eumnyo jumunhal su isseoyo? | Can we order drinks? |

## Polite or casual?

| Casual (friends) | Polite (staff) | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| 어떻게 찾아? | 어떻게 찾아요? | How do I find it? |
| 이거 부를래. | 이거 부를게요. | I'll sing this one. |

## What to actually say

- Searching: **가수 이름으로 찾아 봐.**
- Found it: **이거 예약해!**
- More time at the counter: **시간 추가 돼요?**
