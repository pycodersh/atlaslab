Gangneung is the east coast's favourite day trip from Seoul: under two hours by KTX, with sea cliffs, a beach lined with cafes and a busy central market. This route starts with the cliff walk at Jeongdongjin in the morning, then works north into the city for art, coffee by the sea and dinner at the market.

<KeyTakeaways title="At a glance">

- **The route:** Jeongdongjin Sea Fan Trail → ARTE Museum Gangneung → Anmok Beach Coffee Street → Jungang Market
- **Getting around:** taxi or bus between stops; the trail is about 30 minutes south of the city
- **Cost:** the trail and the museum charge entry; the beach and market are free
- **Best timing:** the trail first, since it closes in the late afternoon
- **Avoid:** going on days with a high-wave warning, when the trail shuts

</KeyTakeaways>

## Jeongdongjin Sea Fan Trail

The Jeongdong-Simgok Sea Fan Trail (Badabuchae-gil) runs along a stretch of coast between Jeongdongjin and Simgok Harbour that was closed to the public for decades because of military patrols. A boardwalk and steps now follow the shoreline at the foot of the cliffs, past rock layers that are a protected natural monument. It's about 3 km one way and takes around an hour.

| | |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Hours** | 9:00 am–5:30 pm from April to October, 9:00 am–4:30 pm from November to March; ticket sales end an hour before closing |
| **Fee** | ₩5,000 for adults, less for youth and children |
| **Closures** | Shuts when high-wave advisories are issued; check on the day |
| **Facilities** | No toilets on the trail itself |

You can start from either end, at the Jeongdong or Simgok ticket office. A taxi back to your starting point is the easiest way to return.

## ARTE Museum Gangneung

ARTE Museum is an immersive digital art space near Gyeongpo Lake: dark rooms where projected waves, flowers and waterfalls fill the walls and floor. It's a good indoor stop after the morning outdoors, and popular with families.

| | |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Hours** | 10 am–8 pm daily, last entry 7 pm |
| **Fee** | Ticketed; prices vary by age and seller, so check the official site |
| **Getting there** | Taxi from the city centre or Gyeongpo Beach |

Gangneung calls itself Korea's coffee city, and Anmok Beach is where that started. A strip of cafes, many with roof terraces, faces the sea along the beachfront road. Pick one with a view, order a hand-drip coffee or a local roast, and watch the waves.

Jungang Market is Gangneung's main traditional market, in the city centre. The ground floor and the lanes outside are full of snack stalls: fried chicken, croquettes, rice cakes and local seafood, with fish to eat fresh in the basement. It's a good place for an early dinner before the train back.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**How long is the Sea Fan Trail?**
About 3 km one way, taking around an hour. You walk it in one direction and come back by taxi.

**Is the Sea Fan Trail open every day?**
It's open year-round, but closes when high-wave advisories are issued.

**Can I do Gangneung as a day trip from Seoul?**
Yes. The KTX takes under two hours, though the trail is a further 30 minutes south of the city.

**Is Anmok Beach good for swimming?**
It's mainly a place for coffee and walks. Swimming is only allowed in the official beach season.
