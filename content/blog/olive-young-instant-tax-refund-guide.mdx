Olive Young is Korea's biggest health and beauty chain, and the best value comes from reading the deal tags and checking out tax-free with your passport. Look for 1+1 and 기획 (special set) labels first, and hand over your physical passport before the cashier starts scanning.

<KeyTakeaways>

- **Best value:** 1+1 deals and 기획 sets, which add refills or minis at the single-item price
- **Tax-free:** most central stores refund tax at the counter for purchases of about 15,000 KRW or more; bring your physical passport
- **Timing:** the big seasonal sales (roughly every quarter) bring the deepest discounts
- **Avoid:** buying a single item when the set version sits on the next shelf

</KeyTakeaways>

## How to read Olive Young price tags

Shelf tags are in Korean, but only a handful of words matter. Once you know them, you can spot a good deal in seconds.

| Tag | Meaning | What you get |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1+1** | Buy one, get one | Two full-size items for one price |
| **2+1** | Buy two, get one | Three items for the price of two |
| **기획 (gihoek)** | Special set | Main product plus a refill, mini or mask |
| **증정 (jeungjeong)** | Free gift | A bonus item attached to the box |
| **단독 (dandok)** | Olive Young exclusive | A version you won't find in other stores |
| **세일 / 할인** | Sale / discount | The red price is the current price |

Single items and set versions often sit side by side, sometimes in nearly identical boxes. Compare the volume (ml) printed on the front before you pick one up.

## Tax-free checkout, step by step

Many Olive Young stores in tourist areas offer an instant tax refund. The tax comes off at the counter, so you don't need to queue at the airport. The rules below are a general guide; minimums and caps change, so check at the counter.

<figcaption>Large stores in tourist areas usually show a Tax Free sign at the entrance or by the counter</figcaption>
</figure>

| Step | What to do | Tip |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. Check eligibility** | Look for a Tax Free sign at the entrance or counter | Most stores in Myeongdong, Hongdae, Gangnam and Seongsu have one |
| **2. Hand over your passport** | Give your **physical passport** before scanning starts | Photos and copies are not accepted |
| **3. Check the screen** | The total should drop before you pay | Ask if it doesn't change |
| **4. Keep the receipt** | Customs may ask to see your goods when you leave | Don't use up everything before your flight |

A single receipt needs to reach a minimum amount (around 15,000 KRW) to qualify. Per-receipt and per-trip caps also apply. For very large purchases, the airport refund counter is the backup.

## Asking for products in Korean

Staff in tourist-area stores often speak some English, Japanese or Chinese. A few Korean words still help a lot, especially for skin type.

| You want | Say | Romanization |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Something for oily skin | 지성 피부용 있어요? | Jiseong pibu-yong isseoyo? |
| Something for dry skin | 건성 피부용 있어요? | Geonseong pibu-yong isseoyo? |
| Sensitive skin | 민감성 피부 | Mingamseong pibu |
| The best seller | 제일 잘 나가는 거 뭐예요? | Jeil jal naganeun geo mwoyeyo? |
| To try it | 테스트해 봐도 돼요? | Teseuteu-hae bwado dwaeyo? |

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Which Olive Young store is best for tourists?**
The large stores in Myeongdong and Seongsu carry the widest range, and their staff are used to tax-free checkout and foreign customers. Smaller neighborhood stores are quieter but stock fewer brands.

**Do I need to show my passport for every purchase?**
Only if you want the tax-free price. Without a passport you pay the normal price, which still includes the 1+1 and sale discounts.

**When is the Olive Young sale?**
The big seasonal sales usually run for about a week, several times a year, often in March, June, September and December. Check the Olive Young app or in-store posters for exact dates; smaller weekly deals change every week.

**Can I return a product I opened?**
Opened beauty products generally can't be returned unless they are faulty. Use the testers before you buy, and keep your receipt for unopened returns.
