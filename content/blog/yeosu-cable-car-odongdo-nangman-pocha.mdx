Yeosu is a harbour town on Korea's south coast, known for its night sea. Its main sights sit close together around the old port, so this whole day can be done on foot: a morning on Odongdo island, a cable car ride over the water, lunch around Yi Sun-sin Square and seafood under the tents of Nangman Pocha Street after dark.

<KeyTakeaways title="At a glance">

- **The route:** Odongdo Island → Yeosu Maritime Cable Car → Yi Sun-sin Square → Nangman Pocha Street, about 4 km on foot
- **Getting there:** KTX from Seoul to Yeosu-EXPO Station, a short walk or taxi from Odongdo
- **Cost:** Odongdo and the square are free; the cable car is paid
- **Best timing:** the cable car in late afternoon, the pocha tents after sunset
- **Avoid:** riding the cable car on very windy days, when it can stop

</KeyTakeaways>

Odongdo is a small wooded island joined to the mainland by a long causeway. Paths loop through a forest of camellia trees and bamboo to a lighthouse and viewpoints over the sea cliffs. The camellias flower red from winter into early spring. Walking the causeway takes 10–15 minutes; a small road train runs along it for those who'd rather ride.

## Yeosu Maritime Cable Car

<figure className="post-figure">
<img src="https://eecvvgkihtcgfikaimao.supabase.co/storage/v1/object/public/blog-covers/yeosu-cable-car-odongdo-nangman-pocha-cablecar.webp" alt="Dolsan Bridge crossing the harbour in Yeosu, seen from Dolsan Park" loading="lazy" />
<figcaption>Dolsan Bridge and the harbour, seen from the cable car's Dolsan end. Photo: Mobius6 (<a href="https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">CC BY-SA 4.0</a>), via <a href="https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Dolsan_Bridge_20180929_003.jpg" rel="noopener noreferrer" target="_blank">Wikimedia Commons</a></figcaption>
</figure>

The Yeosu Maritime Cable Car crosses the harbour between Jasan Park, near Odongdo, and Dolsan Park on the island of Dolsan. Some cabins, called crystal cabins, have glass floors so you can see the sea below. The ride takes about 13–15 minutes each way. At dusk and after dark the view takes in the lit-up Dolsan Bridge.

| | |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Hours** | Usually 9:30 am to 9:30 pm; may change with the weather |
| **Tickets** | Standard and crystal (glass-floor) cabins, one-way or round trip; crystal cabins cost more |
| **Watch out** | Stops in strong wind or lightning |

A round trip from Jasan is the simplest way to keep to the walking route.

Yi Sun-sin Square, by the harbour in the old town, honours the admiral who defended Korea's south coast against Japanese invasions in the 1590s and whose fleet was based in Yeosu. The square has his statue and a full-size replica of a turtle ship you can walk through. The streets around it are full of seafood restaurants and snack shops, so this is the place for a late lunch.

A few minutes' walk north is Jinnamgwan, a long wooden hall that served as the headquarters of the naval command in the Joseon era.

## Nangman Pocha Street

Nangman Pocha Street is a row of tent bars and seafood stalls on the Jongpo seafront, open in the evening. Locals and visitors share plates of grilled and stir-fried seafood with drinks, facing the harbour lights and the bridge. *Nangman* means romance, and the name comes from Yeosu's reputation for its night sea.

| | |
| :--- | :--- |
| **When** | Evenings; it comes alive after dark |
| **Tip** | Dishes are sized for sharing, so it's best in a group of two or more |

## Frequently Asked Questions

**How long is the Yeosu cable car ride?**
About 13–15 minutes each way between Jasan and Dolsan.

**Are the glass-floor cabins worth it?**
If you don't mind heights, yes. They cost more and can have a longer queue.

**Can I walk from Odongdo to Yi Sun-sin Square?**
Yes, about 3–4 km along the harbour, or a short taxi ride.

**When is Nangman Pocha Street open?**
In the evenings. It's quiet in the daytime and busiest after dark.
