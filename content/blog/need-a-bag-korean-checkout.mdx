You put your things on the counter. The staff scans them, says something, and looks at you, waiting. It's almost always about the bag.

## The exchange

**A:** 계산할게요.  
*(I'd like to pay.)*  
**B:** 봉투에 담아 드릴까요?  
*(Shall I put it in a bag?)*  
**A:** 네, 담아주세요.  
*(Yes, please.)*

**계산할게요** (*gyesanhalgeyo*) is optional, since putting your items on the counter is enough, but it starts the exchange cleanly. Some staff ask the shorter **봉투 필요하세요?** ("do you need a bag?").

## Answering

| Korean | Romanization | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| 네, 담아 주세요. | ne, dama juseyo | Yes, please. |
| 괜찮아요. | gwaenchanayo | No thanks. |
| 그냥 주세요. | geunyang juseyo | I'll take it as is. |

**괜찮아요** ("it's fine") is a polite no to almost any offer at the counter: a bag, a receipt, a straw.

## Bags cost money

Korea has been cutting back on free plastic bags, so stores ask before charging for one. It's usually a small amount, a few hundred won at most, added to your total. That's why staff ask instead of bagging automatically: they need your OK to charge you.

## What staff will ask you

| You'll hear | Meaning | Answer |
|---|---|---|
| 봉투 필요하세요? | Do you need a bag? | 네 / 괜찮아요 |
| 영수증 드릴까요? | Would you like a receipt? | 괜찮아요 |
| 포인트 적립하세요? | Collecting points? | 괜찮아요 (needs a Korean membership) |
| 데워 드릴까요? | Shall I heat it up? | 네, 데워 주세요 |
| 카드 꽂아 주세요. | Please insert your card. | (insert it) |

**데워 드릴까요?** comes up when you buy a lunch box or something else meant to be eaten warm. At many stores the microwave is self-service instead, by the window counter.

## Paying

**카드요** (*kadeuyo*, "by card") is enough. Card works with no minimum, and you insert or tap it yourself at the reader facing you. Foreign cards work at most stores; a very small branch occasionally rejects one. There's no tipping.

<Callout type="tip" title="The trash bag option">

Many stores also sell **종량제 봉투** (*jongnyangje bongtu*), the official trash bags Korean households must use. If you're staying in an apartment for a while, ask **종량제 봉투로 주세요** and carry your shopping home in the bag you'll need anyway. The bags are specific to each district, so buy them near where you're staying.

</Callout>

## Polite or casual?

| Polite (staff) | Casual (friends) | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| 봉투 필요하세요? | 봉투 필요해? | Do you need a bag? |
| 괜찮아요. | 괜찮아. / 됐어. | No thanks. |

## What to actually say

- The bag: **네, 담아 주세요.** or **괜찮아요.**
- Receipt and points: **괜찮아요.**
- Paying: **카드요.**
