You can see a lot of Jeju without renting a car, using the island's bus network, taxis for short hops, and a day tour or two for the far corners. Base yourself in Jeju City or Seogwipo, plan around bus frequency, and group sights by area.

<KeyTakeaways>

- **Buses:** cover the whole island and accept T-money; frequent on main routes, sparse in rural areas
- **Best bases:** Jeju City (airport side) and Seogwipo (south coast)
- **Day tours:** the easiest way to reach scattered east and west sights in one day
- **Avoid:** packing far-apart sights into one day by bus; journeys take longer than maps suggest

</KeyTakeaways>

## Getting around compared

| Option | Best for | Cost | Note |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Express and trunk buses** | Crossing the island, airport to Seogwipo | Low; T-money accepted | Main routes run often |
| **Local buses** | Villages and beaches | Low | Can be an hour or more apart |
| **Taxi** | Short trips, late evenings | Moderate | Call via Kakao T; rural pickups can be slow |
| **Day tour** | East or west highlights in one day | Moderate | Bookable online; English-speaking guides available |
| **Hired driver / taxi tour** | Groups and families | Higher | Set route for the day |

## Where to stay

| Base | Good for | Getting around |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Jeju City** | Arrival and departure days, markets, food | Airport buses, many routes start here |
| **Seogwipo** | Waterfalls, coastal walks, Olle trails | Good bus links along the south coast |
| **Aewol / Hallim (west)** | Cafes and beaches | Fewer buses; better with tours or taxis |
| **Seongsan (east)** | Sunrise Peak, Udo ferry | Bus links, but far from the west |

<Callout type="tip" title="Plan by area">
Split Jeju into east, south and west days. On the east day, combine Seongsan Ilchulbong and Udo; on the south day, Seogwipo's waterfalls and Olle trails; on the west day, a tour or taxi for cafes and beaches.
</Callout>

## Top sights and how to reach them

| Sight | Area | How to get there without a car |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Seongsan Ilchulbong** | East | Express or trunk bus to Seongsan |
| **Udo island** | East | Ferry from Seongsan Port, then buses or bikes on the island |
| **Cheonjiyeon Falls** | South | Walk or short bus ride in Seogwipo |
| **Hallasan** | Center | Bus to the trailheads (Seongpanak, Gwaneumsa) |
| **Hyeopjae Beach** | West | Bus from Jeju City, about an hour |
| **Manjanggul Cave** | Northeast | Bus plus a short walk |

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Can you get around Jeju without a car?**
Yes. Buses cover the island, and taxis and day tours fill the gaps. It takes more planning, especially for the west coast.

**Do Jeju buses take T-money?**
Yes. T-money and other Korean transit cards work on Jeju buses, and transfers are discounted.

**Is renting a car in Jeju easy for foreigners?**
You need an International Driving Permit. Many visitors skip it and use buses, taxis and tours instead.

**How many days do I need in Jeju?**
Three to four days covers the main areas without a car. Add a day for hiking Hallasan.
