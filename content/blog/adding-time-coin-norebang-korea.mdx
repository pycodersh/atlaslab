The counter hits zero, the screen pauses, and you still have three songs left in you. At a coin noraebang, that's easy to fix.

## The exchange

**A:** 어, 시간 다 됐네.  
*(Oh, time's up.)*  
**B:** 아쉽다, 더 부르고 싶은데. 추가 요금 얼마지?  
*(That's a shame, I want to sing more. How much is the extra charge?)*  
**A:** 응, 천 원에 세 곡 더 부를 수 있어.  
*(Yeah, you can sing three more songs for 1,000 won.)*  
**B:** 그래? 그럼 우리 조금만 더 부르고 가자.  
*(Really? Then let's sing a little more before we go.)*

These are two friends, so it's all casual speech (반말). **시간 다 됐네** uses **-네**, which reacts to something you've just noticed. **아쉽다** is the feeling of wanting a bit more of something good. **곡** (*gok*) counts songs: 세 곡, three songs.

## Words to know

| Korean | Romanization | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| 시간 다 됐어 | sigan da dwaesseo | time's up |
| 아쉽다 | aswipda | what a shame |
| 추가 요금 | chuga yogeum | extra charge |
| 곡 | gok | song (counter) |
| 조금만 더 | jogeumman deo | just a little more |

## How coin noraebang works

**코인노래방** (*koin noraebang*) is self-service: no front desk, no staff. You feed the machine coins or a bill, and it gives you a few songs or a block of minutes. Prices are posted on the machine and vary by place. When the credit runs out, the screen pauses until you add more.

| | 코인노래방 | 일반 노래방 |
|---|---|---|
| Booking | walk in | rent by the hour at a counter |
| Paying | per song or per few minutes | hourly rate |
| Group size | one or two | small to large groups |
| More time | add coins | ask the counter |

## What you'll hear and see

| You'll see | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 남은 곡 | songs left |
| 남은 시간 | time left |
| 금액을 투입해 주세요 | please insert money |
| 시간이 종료되었습니다 | your time has ended |

The machine's messages use formal **-습니다** and **-주세요**, like announcements.

## Useful questions

| Korean | Romanization | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| 몇 곡 남았어? | myeot gok namasseo? | How many songs are left? |
| 동전 어디서 바꿔요? | dongjeon eodiseo bakkwoyo? | Where can I get change? |
| 기계가 고장 난 것 같아요. | gigyega gojang nan geot gatayo | I think the machine is broken. |

Many machines take bills directly; others need coins from a change machine nearby. If something breaks, look for a contact number on the wall.

## Polite or casual?

| Casual (friends) | Polite | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| 시간 다 됐네. | 시간 다 됐네요. | Oh, time's up. |
| 조금만 더 부르고 가자. | 조금만 더 부르고 가요. | Let's sing a bit more. |
| 얼마지? | 얼마예요? | How much is it? |

## What to actually say

- Time's up: **아, 시간 다 됐네.**
- Want more: **조금만 더 부르고 가자.**
- One last song: **한 곡만 더 하자.**
