You wake up tired. Your flight is in the evening, and checkout is at 11. One question at the front desk might buy you another hour.

## The exchange

**A:** 저 혹시, 체크아웃 시간을 조금 연장할 수 있을까요?  
*(Excuse me, is it possible to extend the checkout time?)*  
**B:** 네, 손님! 1시간까지는 무료 연장됩니다.  
*(Yes! We can extend it for up to one hour free of charge.)*  
**A:** 다행이네요! 그럼 12시까지 체크아웃하면 되나요?  
*(What a relief! So can I check out by 12 PM?)*  
**B:** 네 맞습니다, 12시까지 카드 키 반납해 주시면 됩니다!  
*(That's right, just return your key card by 12 PM!)*

**연장** (*yeonjang*) means extension. **다행이네요** ("what a relief") is the natural reply when something goes your way. **반납** (*bannap*) is returning something: a key card, a library book, a rental bike.

## Words to know

| Korean | Romanization | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| 연장 | yeonjang | extension |
| 무료 | muryo | free of charge |
| 추가 요금 | chuga yogeum | extra charge |
| 카드 키 | kadeu ki | key card |
| 반납 | bannap | returning (an item) |

## What staff will say

| You'll hear | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 1시간까지 무료예요. | Up to an hour is free. |
| 추가 요금이 발생합니다. | There'll be an extra charge. |
| 오늘은 만실이라 어려워요. | We're fully booked today, so it's difficult. |
| 카드 키 반납해 주세요. | Please return your key card. |

Whether it's free depends on the hotel and on how busy it is. Some give an hour if the room isn't needed; others charge by the hour or a half-day rate. If you hear **추가 요금**, you can decide whether it's worth it.

## Other front-desk requests

| Korean | Romanization | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| 짐 잠깐 맡길 수 있을까요? | jim jamkkan matgil su isseulkkayo? | Could I leave my bags for a while? |
| 택시 불러 주실 수 있어요? | taeksi bulleo jusil su isseoyo? | Could you call a taxi? |
| 공항 셔틀 있어요? | gonghang syeoteul isseoyo? | Is there an airport shuttle? |

## Polite or casual?

Staff speak formal **-습니다** (**연장됩니다**, **맞습니다**). You can use polite **-요** with **혹시** in front: that's polite enough for any hotel. Between travelers: **체크아웃 늦게 해도 된대** ("they said we can check out late").

## What to actually say

- Asking: **혹시 체크아웃 시간을 조금 연장할 수 있을까요?**
- Confirming: **12시까지 괜찮을까요?**
- Leaving: **카드 키 여기 있어요.**

Ask the night before or early in the morning, when the desk knows how busy the day is.
