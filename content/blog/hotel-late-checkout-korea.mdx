You wake up tired. The airport run is at 2pm. Checkout is at 11.

One question at the front desk might buy you an extra hour.

## The exchange

**A:** 저 혹시, 체크아웃 시간을 조금 연장할 수 있을까요?  
*(Excuse me, is it possible to extend the checkout time?)*  
**B:** 네, 손님! 1시간까지는 무료 연장됩니다.  
*(Yes! We can extend it for up to one hour free of charge.)*  
**A:** 다행이네요! 그럼 12시까지 체크아웃하면 되나요?  
*(What a relief! So can I check out by 12 PM?)*  
**B:** 네 맞습니다, 12시까지 카드 키 반납해 주시면 됩니다!  
*(That's right, just return your key card by 12 PM!)*

> **Key Nuances**
> - **혹시 ~ 연장할 수 있을까요?**: 혹시 ("by any chance") turns the ask into a polite request; 연장 means extension.
> - **손님 / 무료**: "Guest" and "free of charge" — hear both in one sentence and the answer is yes, at no cost.
> - **다행이네요**: "What a relief" — the natural reply when something goes your way.
> - **반납**: Returning something — a key card, a library book, a rental bike. Same word everywhere.

## Words to know

| Korean | Romanization | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| 연장 | yeon-jang | Extension |
| 무료 | mu-ryo | Free of charge |
| 손님 | son-nim | Guest, customer |
| 다행이네요 | da-haeng-i-ne-yo | What a relief |
| 카드 키 | ka-deu ki | Key card |
| 반납 | ban-nap | Return (an item) |

## When late checkout is free vs. paid

Most Korean hotels offer **1 hour free** if you ask politely and the room isn't booked for that day. After that, it usually becomes a half-day charge.

**1시간 더 연장할 수 있을까요?** — "Could I extend by one more hour?"

If they say it'll cost extra, you'll hear:

**추가 요금이 발생합니다** (*chu-ga yo-geum-i bal-saeng-ham-ni-da*) — "An additional charge will apply."

At that point you can decide — worth it for the sleep, or not.

## Other useful front-desk requests

**짐을 잠깐 맡길 수 있을까요?** — "Could I leave my luggage for a bit?"
After checkout, if your flight is later. Most hotels keep bags for free.

**택시 불러주실 수 있어요?** — "Could you call a taxi for me?"
Front desks do this all the time. Faster than hailing one outside.

**공항까지 셔틀버스 있나요?** — "Is there a shuttle to the airport?"
Worth asking at any hotel near a transit hub.

## What to actually do

1. Call down or go to the front desk the night before or that morning
2. Say **혹시 체크아웃 시간을 조금 연장할 수 있을까요?**
3. If it's free, confirm the new time — **12시까지 괜찮을까요?**
4. Return the key card at the agreed time — **반납** is the word they'll use

Ask early, before the front desk gets busy. The earlier you ask, the more likely the room is still available.
