You walk in, and a staff member says something as you pass. You nod and keep walking. It was an offer to help, and in a store this size you'll probably want to take it.

## The exchange

**A:** 도움 필요하시면 말씀해 주세요.  
*(Let me know if you need any help.)*  
**B:** 저기, 혹시 컬러 립밤 어디 있어요?  
*(Excuse me, where can I find the tinted lip balms?)*  
**A:** 립밤 코너는 이쪽 안쪽에 있어요.  
*(The lip balm section is right over here inside.)*  
**B:** 아, 안쪽에 있었네요. 감사합니다!  
*(Ah, it was in the back. Thank you!)*

**저기** gets someone's attention, and **혹시** ("by any chance") softens the question that follows. Together they make a very natural, polite opener.

## The one pattern you need

**[item] 어디 있어요?** (*eodi isseoyo?*, "where is [item]?")

| What you want | How to ask |
|---|---|
| 선크림 (sunscreen) | 선크림 어디 있어요? |
| 마스크팩 (sheet mask) | 마스크팩 어디 있어요? |
| 쿠션 (cushion foundation) | 쿠션 어디 있어요? |
| 클렌징 오일 (cleansing oil) | 클렌징 오일 어디 있어요? |
| 토너 (toner) | 토너 어디 있어요? |

Most product names are English said with Korean sounds. **선크림** ("sun cream") is what Koreans say instead of sunscreen.

## Words for directions

| Korean | Romanization | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| 저기요 | jeogiyo | excuse me (to get attention) |
| 혹시 | hoksi | by any chance |
| 이쪽 / 저쪽 | ijjok / jeojjok | this way / that way |
| 안쪽 | anjjok | further in, at the back |
| 코너 | koneo | section |

## What staff will say to you

| You'll hear | Meaning | Answer |
|---|---|---|
| 도움 필요하시면 말씀해 주세요. | Let me know if you need help. | a nod, or 네 |
| 찾으시는 거 있으세요? | Are you looking for something? | 선크림 찾고 있어요. |
| 피부 타입이 어떻게 되세요? | What's your skin type? | 건성이에요. |
| 샘플 드릴까요? | Would you like a sample? | 네, 주세요. |

For skin type, the four answers are **건성** (*geonseong*, dry), **지성** (*jiseong*, oily), **복합성** (*bokapseong*, combination) and **민감성** (*mingamseong*, sensitive).

The greeting at the door doesn't need an answer. A nod is enough.

## Polite or casual?

| Polite (staff) | Casual (friends) | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| 선크림 어디 있어요? | 선크림 어디 있어? | Where's the sunscreen? |
| 찾고 있어요. | 찾고 있어. | I'm looking for it. |

With a friend, drop the **-요**. With staff, keep it, and add **저기요** first.

<Callout type="tip" title="Just browsing">

If you'd rather look alone, **구경할게요** (*gugyeonghalgeyo*, "I'm just looking") is a polite way to say so. Staff will leave you to it.

</Callout>

## What to actually say

- Getting attention: **저기요.**
- Finding something: **선크림 어디 있어요?**
- Skin type: **건성이에요.**
- Samples: **네, 주세요.**
