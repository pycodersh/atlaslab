Shopping in Hongdae, Dongdaemun or Seongsu is a must in Seoul. Browsing the racks is easy, but asking to try something on or finding the fitting room can make anyone nervous. Here is the polite Korean you need, from the rack to the register.

## The exchange

**손님 (customer):** 저, 입어 봐도 돼요?  
*(Excuse me, can I try this on?)*  
**직원 (staff):** 네, 피팅룸 저쪽에 있어요.  
*(Sure, the fitting room is over there.)*  
**손님:** 마음에 들어요. 이걸로 할게요.  
*(I like it. I'll take this one.)*  
**직원:** 네, 카운터로 안내해 드릴게요.  
*(Great, I'll take you to the counter.)*

## Polite or casual?

You're talking to shop staff, so polite speech (**존댓말**) ending in **-요** is standard. The casual forms are for shopping with friends.

| Casual (friends) | Polite (to shop staff) | Meaning |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 입어 봐도 돼? | 입어 봐도 돼요? | Can I try this on? |
| 저쪽에 있어. | 저쪽에 있어요. | It's over there. |
| 마음에 들어. | 마음에 들어요. | I like it. |
| 이걸로 할게. | 이걸로 할게요. | I'll take this one. |

> **이걸로 할게요 (I'll take this one):**
> Literally "I'll go with this one," it's the most natural way to make your choice at a shop, and you'll hear it far more than **이거 살게요** (I'll buy this).

## Before you try clothes on

Essential questions about sizes, colors and stock:

| Korean | Romanization | Meaning |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **이거 다른 색상도 있어요?** | *igeo dareun saeksangdo isseoyo?* | Do you have this in other colors? |
| **더 큰 사이즈 있어요?** | *deo keun saijeu isseoyo?* | Do you have a bigger size? |
| **새 상품 있어요?** | *sae sangpum isseoyo?* | Do you have a new one (not the display item)? |
| **거울 어디 있어요?** | *geoul eodi isseoyo?* | Where is the mirror? |

## What staff will tell you

Common rules you'll meet in Korean clothing shops:

| You'll hear | Meaning | Context |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **흰 옷은 피팅이 안 돼요.** | White clothes can't be tried on. | A common policy to avoid makeup marks. |
| **페이스 커버 써 주세요.** | Please put on a face cover. | A thin mesh hood for trying on tops. |
| **천천히 둘러보세요.** | Take your time looking around. | A welcome when you walk in. |
| **결제 도와드릴게요.** | I'll help you pay. | Calling you to the register. |

> **페이스 커버 (face cover):**
> Many Korean clothing shops keep disposable mesh hoods in the fitting room. Slip one over your head before pulling on a top, and it keeps foundation and lipstick off the clothes.

## What to actually say

- Asking to try something on: **입어 봐도 돼요?** (*Can I try this on?*)
- Making your choice: **이걸로 할게요.** (*I'll take this one.*)
- Asking for a fresh one from stock: **새 걸로 주세요.** (*A new one, please.*)
- Asking about tax refunds: **택스 리펀드 돼요?** (*Can I get a tax refund?*)
