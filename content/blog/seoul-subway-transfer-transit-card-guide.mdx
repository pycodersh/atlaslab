Seoul's subway and buses use one fare system, and a transit card unlocks the discounts that single tickets miss. Tap in and out on every ride, transfer within the time limit, and you'll pay a base fare plus a little extra for distance instead of paying again each time.

<KeyTakeaways>

- **Transit card:** T-money, a Climate Card or WOWPASS works on subways and buses
- **Fares:** distance-based; transfers between subway and bus are discounted
- **Tap out:** always, including on buses, or you lose the transfer discount
- **Avoid:** single-journey tickets; they cost more and need a deposit refund

</KeyTakeaways>

## Single ticket vs transit card

| | Single-journey ticket | Transit card |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Price** | Higher, plus a refundable deposit | Lower card fare |
| **Transfers** | No bus-subway transfer discount | Discounted transfers |
| **Hassle** | Buy at a machine each time, then get the deposit back | Tap and go |
| **Works on buses** | No | Yes |
| **Best for** | A one-off ride | Everyone else |

## How fares and transfers work

| Rule | What it means |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Base fare** | Covers a set distance |
| **Distance surcharge** | Small extra charges for longer trips |
| **Transfers** | Tap into the next bus or subway within 30 minutes of tapping out, or within 60 minutes from 21:00 to 07:00 |
| **Transfer count** | Up to a few transfers per journey |
| **Tapping out** | Required on buses and subway exits to calculate the fare |

<Callout type="tip" title="Which card?">
For most visitors, a T-money card from a convenience store is the simplest. Heavy Seoul users may prefer the unlimited Climate Card. See our [transit card comparison](/blog/en/k-patto/climate-card-vs-t-money-seoul).
</Callout>

## A transfer example

Say you ride the subway from Hongik University to Seoul Station, tap out, then catch a bus to Namsan within the transfer time. With a transit card you pay the base fare once, plus a small distance charge, instead of two full fares. Without tapping out, the bus would charge a fresh fare.

| Leg | What you tap | What you pay |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Subway in** | Tap at the gate | Base fare |
| **Subway out** | Tap at the exit | Distance charge if any |
| **Bus in (within time limit)** | Tap at the front door | Transfer discount applied |
| **Bus out** | Tap at the back door | Any extra distance |

## Wrong gate or not enough balance

**Tapped in on the wrong side?** Some older stations have separate gates for each direction. Tap out and tap back in on the correct side within 15 minutes, at the same station and on the same line, and the system treats it as a free transfer. It works with transit cards only, not single-journey tickets, and switching to a different line at the same station counts as a new trip.

**Not enough balance to get out?** Every station has a fare adjustment machine (교통카드 정산기) inside the gates, next to the exits. Put your card on it, add the missing amount (usually cash), then tap out as normal.

| Problem | Fix |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Wrong direction gate** | Tap out, then in on the right side within 15 minutes; free on a transit card |
| **Balance too low at the exit** | Use the fare adjustment machine (정산기) beside the exit gates |
| **Gate won't open** | Press the call button on the gate; staff can help over the intercom |

## Topping up and refunds

| Task | Where | Note |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Buy a T-money card** | Convenience stores, some station machines | A few thousand won for the card |
| **Top up** | Station machines and convenience stores | Mostly cash |
| **Check balance** | Station machines or the gate screen | Shown each time you tap |
| **Refund leftover balance** | Convenience stores or stations | Small fee; card cost isn't refunded |

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Is a T-money card worth it for a short trip?**
Yes. Even for a few days, it saves money on every ride and makes bus transfers cheaper.

**Do I get a transfer discount between bus and subway?**
Yes, with a transit card, if you tap out and transfer within the time limit.

**What happens if I forget to tap out on a bus?**
Your next ride won't count as a transfer, so you pay a new base fare. On distance-based buses, such as red express buses, you can also be charged the route's maximum fare. See [riding city buses](/blog/en/k-patto/how-to-ride-city-buses-in-seoul).

**How long do I have to transfer?**
30 minutes after tapping out, or 60 minutes between 21:00 and 07:00.

**Can I refund my T-money balance?**
Yes, at convenience stores or stations, minus a small fee. The card's own cost isn't refunded.
