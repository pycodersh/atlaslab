On check-out day or between hotels, you don't have to drag your suitcase around Seoul. Use a subway station locker for a few hours, or a luggage delivery service to send your bags to the airport or your next hotel while you sightsee.

<KeyTakeaways>

- **Subway lockers:** self-service, a few thousand won for several hours
- **Luggage counters:** staffed storage at major stations for heavier bags
- **Delivery services:** send bags from your hotel to the airport, often booked a day ahead
- **Avoid:** counting on a large locker at popular stations on weekends; they fill up

</KeyTakeaways>

## Your options compared

| | Subway locker | Staffed luggage counter | Luggage delivery |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **How it works** | Touchscreen kiosk, pay and lock | Staff tag and store your bag | Drop at hotel or counter; collect at airport or next hotel |
| **Best for** | A few hours between sights | Heavy or many bags | Your last day before an evening flight |
| **Rough price** | A few thousand won for the first 4 hours, more per extra hour | Priced by bag size | Roughly 15,000–25,000 KRW per bag |
| **Booking** | Walk up; some reservable in an app | Walk up during opening hours | Book online, usually by the day before or morning |
| **Hours** | Station hours, roughly early morning to midnight | Roughly daytime to evening | Set pickup and delivery windows |

Prices and hours vary by operator and station, so check the screen or the service's website.

## Using a subway locker

| Step | What to do |
| :--- | :--- |
| **1. Find the lockers** | Search 물품보관함 in your map app or look for signs near exits |
| **2. Choose a size** | Small, medium or large; large fits a full-size suitcase |
| **3. Pay** | Card or transit card at the kiosk; many have English |
| **4. Set access** | Use a PIN, your card or a QR code on your phone |
| **5. Collect** | Return to the same locker and unlock; pay any extra time |

<Callout type="tip" title="When lockers are full">
Popular stations like Myeongdong, Hongik University and Seoul Station fill up fast. Check nearby stations, use a luggage storage app that partners with shops and cafes, or ask your hotel to keep your bags after check-out.
</Callout>

## Luggage delivery to the airport

Several services pick up bags in central Seoul and deliver them to Incheon Airport, so you can spend your last day free. Book ahead, drop your bag at the hotel front desk or a counter by the set time, and collect it at the airport counter before check-in.

| Tip | Why |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Book the day before** | Same-day slots can sell out |
| **Keep valuables with you** | Passports, electronics and medicine stay in your bag |
| **Note the pickup counter** | Different terminals have different counters |
| **Allow time** | Collect bags before airline check-in closes |

## Frequently Asked Questions

**How much do subway lockers cost in Seoul?**
Usually a few thousand won for the first few hours, depending on size, with extra charges per additional hour.

**Do subway lockers fit large suitcases?**
Large lockers fit most full-size suitcases, but there are fewer of them and they fill quickly at busy stations.

**Can I send my bags to Incheon Airport?**
Yes. Luggage delivery services collect bags in Seoul and deliver them to airport counters. Book ahead and check the drop-off deadline.

**Can my hotel keep my bags after check-out?**
Most hotels store bags for free on the day of check-out. Ask at the front desk.
