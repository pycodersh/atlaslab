You bring your items to the register, and the staff asks two questions before the total appears: one about a bag, one about how you're paying. Both are easy once you know they're coming.

## The exchange

**A:** 봉투 필요하세요? 100원 추가되세요.  
*(Do you need a bag? It's an extra 100 won.)*  
**B:** 아니요, 그냥 가방에 넣을게요.  
*(No, I'll just put it in my bag.)*  
**A:** 결제는 카드로 도와드릴까요?  
*(Shall I help you pay by card?)*  
**B:** 네, 카드로 결제해 주세요.  
*(Yes, please process it by card.)*

**도와드릴까요?** ("shall I help you…?") is the extra-polite service phrasing you'll hear at every register. **추가되세요** is service-industry Korean too: grammatically odd, but it's what staff say.

## Words at the register

| Korean | Romanization | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| 봉투 | bongtu | bag |
| 추가 | chuga | extra charge |
| 결제 | gyeolje | payment |
| 카드 / 현금 | kadeu / hyeongeum | card / cash |
| 영수증 | yeongsujeung | receipt |
| 일시불 | ilsibul | paying in full |

## What staff will ask you

| You'll hear | Meaning | Answer |
|---|---|---|
| 봉투 필요하세요? | Do you need a bag? | 네, 담아 주세요. / 괜찮아요. |
| 결제 어떻게 하시겠어요? | How would you like to pay? | 카드로 할게요. |
| 할부로 하시겠어요? | In installments? | 일시불로 해 주세요. |
| 멤버십 있으세요? | Do you have a membership? | 없어요. / 괜찮아요. |
| 영수증 드릴까요? | Would you like a receipt? | 네, 주세요. / 괜찮아요. |

**괜찮아요** (*gwaenchanayo*, "it's fine") is a polite no to almost any offer at the register: the bag, the receipt, the points. One word covers three questions.

The installment question comes from Korean credit cards, which can split a payment over months. With a foreign card, **일시불로 해 주세요** (*ilsibullo hae juseyo*) is the answer: one single charge.

## Paying by card

Foreign Visa and Mastercard cards usually work. The reader faces you on the counter, so tap or insert the card yourself. Bags cost a little extra in Korea, which is why staff ask instead of bagging automatically. [Do you need a bag?](/blog/en/k-patto/need-a-bag-korean-checkout) covers the same question at convenience stores.

<Callout type="tip" title="Bring your passport">

Many Olive Young branches in tourist areas take the tax off at the counter if you show your physical passport before they start scanning. The [Olive Young shopping guide](/blog/en/k-patto/olive-young-instant-tax-refund-guide) explains the minimum and how it works.

## Membership and points

**멤버십 있으세요?** (*membeosip isseuseyo?*) asks about the Olive Young app, which gives points and member prices. Signing up usually needs a Korean phone number, so for a short trip **없어요** ("I don't have one") is fine and the sale goes through normally.

## Polite or casual?

Staff speak in extra-polite service Korean. You only need polite **-요**: **카드로 할게요**, **괜찮아요**. With a friend in the queue, it's casual: **봉투 필요 없지?** ("we don't need a bag, right?").

## What to actually say

- The bag: **괜찮아요.** or **네, 담아 주세요.**
- Paying: **카드로 할게요.**
- Installments: **일시불로 해 주세요.**
- Receipt and points: **괜찮아요.**
