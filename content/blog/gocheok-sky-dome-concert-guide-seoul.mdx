Gocheok Sky Dome is Korea's first domed stadium, built for baseball and now used for major concerts and award shows. It's only about a 3 minute walk from the subway, but that small station is the hardest part of the night. Plan your way home before you plan your outfit.

<KeyTakeaways title="At a glance">

- **Where:** 430 Gyeongin-ro, Guro-gu, in southwest Seoul (Korean name: 고척스카이돔)
- **Getting there:** Guil Station, Line 1, Exit 2, about a 3 minute walk
- **How long:** arrive at least 2 hours before showtime if you need to collect tickets or buy merch
- **Bring:** your original passport, a small bag and binoculars for upper seats
- **Cost:** seat prices vary by show; check your ticket page

</KeyTakeaways>

## Getting to Gocheok Sky Dome

| Route | How | Good to know |
|---|---|---|
| **Guil Station, Exit 2** | Line 1 | Closest. Take a regular train, because express trains skip Guil |
| **From Seoul Station** | Line 1 toward Incheon | About 30 minutes, no transfer |
| **Gaebong Station, Exit 2** | Line 1 | About a 15 minute walk |
| **Guro Station, Exit 2** | Line 1 | About a 20 minute walk |
| **By bus** | Dongyang Mirae University / Guro Seongsim Hospital stop | Traffic is heavy on event days |

Check the destination on the train before you board. Line 1 splits south of Guro, so a train toward Incheon is the one you want from central Seoul. The venue operator recommends public transport over driving.

## Tickets and ID

If you bought through a global ticket site, the rules on your booking page come first. In general:

- **Bring your original passport.** Photos and copies may be refused.
- **The name has to match.** The name on your booking should match your passport exactly.
- **Check the pickup location.** It changes by show, so read the concert notice before you travel.
- **Don't arrive at the last minute.** ID checks can take 20 to 40 minutes on busy nights, and big shows draw long lines for merch and pickup.

Only buy from official sellers. Tickets from resellers can be canceled, and your name may not match at the door.

## Bags and lockers

Many concerts limit bags to about A4 size, and the limit is set for each show. Don't count on finding a locker near the dome on concert day. Guides disagree on whether lockers are open inside the venue, and those at Guil, Gaebong and Guro stations fill quickly.

A safer plan is to leave your luggage at your hotel, or use a locker at a larger station on the way. Our [subway locker guide](/blog/en/k-patto/hands-free-seoul-subway-lockers-guide) explains the options.

## Food and comfort

The dome isn't surrounded by a big restaurant district, so eat before you queue. Don't assume you can leave and come back in, since re-entry depends on the event.

The building is fully enclosed, which helps in bad weather, though the fixed roof has leaked in the past.

## Picking a seat

The dome holds about 16,000 in fixed seats and more when floor sections are added. Because it was built for baseball, the view depends heavily on your angle.

## Getting home

Guil Station is small, its gates fill quickly, and each direction has its own stairs. Before you leave the station on the way in, note which side you'll need on the way back.

| Heading to | Try this | Note |
|---|---|---|
| **Seoul, Suwon, Anyang** | Walk to Guro Station | About 20 minutes, and express trains stop there |
| **Incheon, Bucheon** | Walk to Gaebong Station | About 15 minutes, and express trains stop there |
| **Staying nearby** | Wait 20 to 30 minutes | Let the first wave clear, then walk to Guil |
| **Late night** | Night bus N16 | Runs toward Seoul Station and Myeongdong |

Taxis are hard to find right after a show, so walk a few blocks first. Check the last train for your exact direction before the concert starts, and keep our [Korean taxi guide](/blog/en/k-patto/korea-taxi-survival-guide) handy.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What is the nearest station to Gocheok Sky Dome?**
Guil Station on Line 1. Exit 2 is about a 3 minute walk from the dome.

**Do express trains stop at Guil Station?**
No. Take a regular Line 1 train, or use an express train to Guro or Gaebong and walk.

**Are there lockers at Gocheok Sky Dome?**
Don't rely on them. Sources differ on lockers inside the venue, and nearby station lockers fill quickly on concert days.

**How do I avoid the crowd after a concert?**
Walk to Guro Station if you're heading toward Seoul, or Gaebong Station if you're heading toward Incheon. You can also wait 20 to 30 minutes before going to Guil.
