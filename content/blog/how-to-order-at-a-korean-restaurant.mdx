Restaurant Korean is more useful than cafe Korean because the exchange is longer. You get seated, you order, you ask for something mid-meal, and you pay. Four separate moments, each with its own phrases.

None of them are complicated. But if you only know 주세요, the middle two will catch you out.

---

## Getting a table

Staff usually ask before you say anything:

**몇 분이세요?** — *How many people?*

- 두 명이요. — Two.
- 세 명이요. — Three.
- 혼자예요. — Just me.

명 is the counter for people. 한 명, 두 명, 세 명, 네 명.

If nobody comes to you: 자리 있어요? — *Do you have a table?*

---

## Calling the staff

This is the part that feels rude to English speakers and is completely normal in Korea.

**여기요!** or **저기요!** — said clearly across the room.

You are supposed to do this. Waiting quietly to be noticed can mean waiting a very long time. Many restaurants also have a call button on the table — press it and someone comes.

---

## Ordering

- 김치찌개 주세요. — Kimchi jjigae, please.
- 저는 비빔밥으로 할게요. — I'll go with bibimbap.
- 삼겹살 이인분 주세요. — Two portions of samgyeopsal, please.

**인분** is the portion counter for meals, and grilled meat is almost always ordered this way. Most meat dishes have a two-portion minimum.

If you need the menu adjusted:

- 덜 맵게 해 주세요. — Please make it less spicy.
- 고기 빼고 주세요. — Without the meat, please.

---

## Mid-meal: the phrases nobody teaches

This is where restaurant Korean actually earns its keep.

**반찬 좀 더 주세요.** — *More side dishes, please.*

Side dishes are free and refillable. Asking for more is expected, not cheap. Some places have a self-service station instead — 반찬은 셀프예요 means help yourself.

**물 좀 주세요.** — *Water, please.* Also often self-service, in a dispenser by the wall.

**앞접시 주세요.** — *A small plate, please.* Useful when sharing.

**가위 주세요.** — *Scissors, please.* For cutting meat or noodles. Completely normal request.

---

## Paying

In most Korean restaurants you pay at the counter on the way out, not at the table.

- 계산할게요. — I'll pay.
- 얼마예요? — How much is it?
- 카드로 할게요. — By card.
- 따로따로 계산해 주세요. — Separate bills, please.
- 같이 계산할게요. — Together.

Splitting is common among friends but one person paying for the group is also normal, especially if they invited you. If someone says 제가 살게요 — *I'll pay* — the polite response is a light protest and then thanks.

## Table words

| Korean | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 반찬 | side dishes |
| 공기밥 | bowl of rice |
| 앞접시 | small plate |
| 수저 | spoon and chopsticks |
| 물티슈 | wet wipe |
| 셀프 | self-service |
| 포장 | takeaway |

**수저** is worth knowing because it is one word for the pair. Cutlery is usually in a drawer under the table, not on it — check there before asking.

---

## A full meal

> **Staff:** 몇 분이세요?
> **You:** 두 명이요.
> **You:** 여기요! 김치찌개 하나, 된장찌개 하나 주세요.
> **You:** (later) 반찬 좀 더 주세요.
> **You:** (at the counter) 계산할게요. 카드로 할게요.
