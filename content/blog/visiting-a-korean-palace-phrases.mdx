Gyeongbokgung is the first stop on most Seoul itineraries, and it's one of the few sights where a little Korean really changes the day, mostly because of hanbok rental and all the photo requests.

## Tickets

| Korean | Romanization | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| 어른 두 장 주세요. | eoreun du jang juseyo | Two adult tickets, please. |
| 몇 시까지 해요? | myeot sikkaji haeyo? | What time do you close? |
| 한복 입으면 무료예요? | hanbok ibeumyeon muryoyeyo? | Is it free if I wear hanbok? |

**장** (*jang*) counts tickets and other flat things. Visitors wearing **한복** get in free at the main Seoul palaces, which is why rental shops cluster around the gates.

## Renting hanbok

| Korean | Romanization | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| 한복 빌리고 싶어요. | hanbok billigo sipeoyo | I'd like to rent a hanbok. |
| 몇 시간이에요? | myeot siganieyo? | For how many hours? |
| 이거 입어 봐도 돼요? | igeo ibeo bwado dwaeyo? | Can I try this on? |
| 머리도 해 주세요. | meorido hae juseyo | Hair styling too, please. |

Shops rent by blocks of a few hours, and hair styling is often an add-on. Ask what's included before you pay.

## What staff will say

| You'll hear | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 몇 분이세요? | How many people? |
| 몇 시간 대여하실 거예요? | How many hours will you rent? |
| 몇 시까지 반납해 주세요. | Please return it by (a time). |
| 소지품은 사물함에 넣어 주세요. | Please put your things in a locker. |

## Photos

| Korean | Romanization | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| 사진 찍어 주실 수 있어요? | sajin jjigeo jusil su isseoyo? | Could you take a photo for us? |
| 여기서 찍어 주세요. | yeogiseo jjigeo juseyo | Please take it from here. |
| 한 장만 더요. | han jangman deoyo | Just one more. |
| 네, 찍어 드릴게요. | ne, jjigeo deurilgeyo | Sure, I'll take it. |

People count in with [하나, 둘, 셋](/blog/en/k-patto/korean-two-number-systems), and you'll often hear **김치** instead of "cheese".

## Around the grounds

| Korean | What it is |
|---|---|
| 매표소 | ticket office |
| 입구 / 출구 | entrance / exit |
| 궁 / 궁궐 | palace |
| 문 | gate |
| 안내도 | site map |
| 해설 | guided commentary |

Gyeongbokgung runs guided tours, including in English, at set times, and holds a guard-changing ceremony at the main gate. Check the official schedule on the day.

## Polite or casual?

Use polite **-요** with staff and strangers: **사진 찍어 주실 수 있어요?** With friends in hanbok, it's casual: **여기서 찍자!** ("let's take one here!").

<Callout type="info" title="The palaces">

**경복궁** is the largest and closes on Tuesdays. **창덕궁** is a UNESCO site, and its Secret Garden needs a separate timed entry. **덕수궁** is small and central, next to City Hall. **창경궁** is quieter and joins Changdeokgung. A combined ticket covers several palaces and Jongmyo Shrine. Closing days and prices change, so check before you go.

</Callout>

## What to actually say

- Tickets: **어른 두 장 주세요.**
- Hanbok: **한복 빌리고 싶어요.**
- Photos: **사진 찍어 주실 수 있어요?**
