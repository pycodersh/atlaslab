Street food stalls are where Korean gets practical: no English menu, a moving queue, and a vendor who speaks fast. The whole exchange is four or five short sentences, and the same ones work at every stall in the country.

## Ordering

| Korean | Romanization | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| 이거 주세요. | igeo juseyo | This one, please. (pointing) |
| 떡볶이 1인분 주세요. | tteokbokki irinbun juseyo | One portion of tteokbokki, please. |
| 이거 하나, 저거 두 개 주세요. | igeo hana, jeogeo du gae juseyo | One of this, two of that, please. |
| 여기서 먹을게요. | yeogiseo meogeulgeyo | I'll eat here. |
| 포장해 주세요. | pojanghae juseyo | Please pack it to go. |

Portions use **인분** with Sino-Korean numbers (일인분, 이인분). Single items like fried snacks are counted with **개** and native numbers (한 개, 두 개). The [counters guide](/blog/en/k-patto/korean-counters-gae-myeong-jan-byeong) covers both.

## Spice and payment

| Korean | Romanization | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| 많이 매워요? | mani maewoyo? | Is it very spicy? |
| 덜 맵게 해 주세요. | deol maepge hae juseyo | Less spicy, please. |
| 매운 거 못 먹어요. | maeun geo mon meogeoyo | I can't eat spicy food. |
| 카드 돼요? | kadeu dwaeyo? | Do you take card? |
| 다 해서 얼마예요? | da haeseo eolmayeyo? | How much altogether? |

Ask **카드 돼요?** before you order. Many stalls take cards now, but some market vendors are still cash-only.

## What the vendor will ask you

| You'll hear | Romanization | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| 몇 인분 드려요? | myeot inbun deuryeoyo? | How many portions? |
| 매운 거 괜찮으세요? | maeun geo gwaenchanuseyo? | Is spicy okay for you? |
| 드시고 가세요? | deusigo gaseyo? | Eating here? |
| 순대는 내장도 드려요? | sundaeneun naejangdo deuryeoyo? | Do you want the offal with your sundae? |

The last one surprises people. Sundae is often served with liver and lung slices. **순대만 주세요** ("just the sundae") skips them.

## Polite or casual?

Vendors at older stalls often switch between polite and casual speech (**먹고 가?**, "eating here?"), especially with younger customers. It's friendly, not rude. Keep using **-요** yourself.

## What's on the menu

| Korean | What it is |
|---|---|
| 떡볶이 | rice cakes in spicy sauce |
| 순대 | Korean blood sausage |
| 튀김 | fried snacks: vegetables, squid, dumplings |
| 어묵 | fish cake on a skewer, in broth |
| 김밥 | rice roll in seaweed |
| 핫도그 | corn dog, not a hot dog |

<Callout type="tip" title="The free broth">

The broth that 어묵 sits in is free, and there are usually paper cups by the pot. Ladling yourself a cup is expected.

</Callout>

## A full exchange

> **You:** 떡볶이 1인분 주세요.  
> **Vendor:** 매운 거 괜찮으세요?  
> **You:** 덜 맵게 해 주세요.  
> **Vendor:** 드시고 가세요?  
> **You:** 포장해 주세요. 얼마예요?  
> **Vendor:** 사천 원이요.
