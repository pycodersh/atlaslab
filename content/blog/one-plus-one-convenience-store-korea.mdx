You put one drink on the counter. The staff scans it, says something, and points past you toward the fridge. You didn't do anything wrong: your drink is on promotion, and you're being told to grab another one free.

## The exchange

**A:** 이거 원플러스원 상품이에요.  
*(This is a 1+1 item.)*  
**B:** 아, 행사 상품이에요?  
*(Oh, it's on promotion?)*  
**A:** 네, 진열대에서 하나 더 가져오시면 돼요.  
*(Yes, just grab one more from the shelf.)*

**행사** (*haengsa*) literally means "event", and it's the word on every promotion sign in the store. **하나 더 가져오시면 돼요** ("you can just bring one more") is the staff's cue that the second one is free.

## 1+1 vs 2+1

| Tag | Read as | You bring | You pay for |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1+1 | won peulleoseu won | two | one |
| 2+1 | tu peulleoseu won | three | two |
| 증정 | jeungjeong | one | one, with a gift attached |

Koreans read the numbers in English. If you bring two of a 2+1 item, you'll hear **하나 더 가져오세요** ("bring one more"): the third one is the free one.

## Words to know

| Korean | Romanization | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| 행사 상품 | haengsa sangpum | promotional item |
| 진열대 | jinyeoldae | shelf |
| 하나 더 | hana deo | one more |
| 다른 맛 | dareun mat | a different flavor |
| 공짜 | gongjja | free |

## What staff will say

| You'll hear | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 원플러스원이에요. | It's buy one, get one. |
| 하나 더 가져오세요. | Bring one more. |
| 같은 행사 상품이면 돼요. | Anything in the same promotion works. |
| 그건 행사 아니에요. | That one isn't on promotion. |

## Can you mix flavors?

Often, yes. Items in the same promotion usually share a price and a tag, and you can combine flavors or products within that group. If you're not sure, ask **다른 맛도 돼요?** (*dareun matdo dwaeyo?*, "does a different flavor work?"). The usual answer is **네, 돼요**.

Promotions change every month, so check the tag on the shelf edge, not last week's memory.

## Don't want the extra one?

**괜찮아요** (*gwaenchanayo*) is a polite no. You pay the same either way, so it only makes sense if you can't carry it. Some chains' apps can store the free item for later, but that needs a Korean account.

## Polite or casual?

| Polite (staff) | Casual (friends) | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| 이거 행사 상품이에요? | 이거 원플원이야? | Is this on promotion? |
| 다른 맛도 돼요? | 다른 맛 골라도 돼? | Can I pick a different flavor? |

Among friends, 1+1 is often shortened to **원플원**.

## What to actually say

- Checking a tag: **이거 행사 상품이에요?**
- Mixing: **다른 맛도 돼요?**
- Turning it down: **괜찮아요.**
